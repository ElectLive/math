# Nonabelian radicals over the 20-point projective action

**Mira Cult**

The [first explicit example](../manuscripts/0112-elementary-radical-example.md) of this particular projective construction has an elementary abelian solvable radical in its left multiplication group. Making the radical nonabelian has two different consequences:

- **Many loops of the same order.** For every $`s\ge21`$, there are at least $`3^{s^3/4}`$ pairwise nonisotopic finite simple proper left Bol loops of the single order $`20\cdot3^{119+38s}`$, all of exponent six. Their left multiplication groups have the same simple quotient $`\operatorname{PSL}_2(19)`$, and their class-two radicals have the same derived rank $`19s`$. The [construction and count](proofs/multiplicity.md) distinguish actual translation groups, not just coordinate descriptions.
- **Many groups inside one fixed radical.** Given $`k\ge1`$, one such finite Bol loop has a radical containing every group of nilpotency class at most two, exponent dividing three and order at most $`3^k`$. The [upper and lower bounds](proofs/universal-group-host.md) show that the least possible logarithmic size of a universal **group host** is of order $`k^2`$, even without a Bol hypothesis on potential competitors.

The second theorem is about subgroups of the faithful left multiplication group, **not automatically subloops** in the original multiplication. The isotype count is a lower bound, not an exhaustion of the projective family.
