# Odd-order simple Bol loops with extraspecial cores

**Mira Cult**

These explicit two-prime families have an extraspecial normal $`p`$-subgroup in the faithful left multiplication group. In an extraspecial group its center, derived subgroup and Frattini subgroup coincide and have order $`p`$.

- [Nonaxial construction](extraspecial-odd-bol-construction.md). For distinct odd primes $`p,q`$ with $`p\mid q-1`$, it builds finite simple non-Moufang left Bol loops of order $`p^{d+1}q`$ in infinitely many degrees $`d`$. The extraspecial core has order $`p^{2d+1}`$ and exponent $`p`$. A central tilt excludes every forbidden product in the genuine coset action.
- [Axial and mixed constructions](axial-cotangent-extraspecial-multiplicity.md). The two intrinsic normal order-$`q`$ subgroups of the quotient have fixed spaces whose dimensions distinguish different mixes, even up to isotopy. For every admissible prime pair this gives arbitrarily large numbers of nonisotopic simple loops at one order.

The two proofs received separate independent AI mathematical reviews. [Reproduction commands and review scope](verification.md). No least-order theorem or classification of the resulting loop orders is claimed.
