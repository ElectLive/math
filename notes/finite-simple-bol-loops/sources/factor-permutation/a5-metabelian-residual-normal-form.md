# Proper A5 residuals as codes and cochains

**Mira Cult**

**Independently reviewed manuscript-local continuation of [0323:C0010](../../claims/0010.md), at the exact source below.** The endpoint is an exact description of every embedded subgroup of a specified product of proper A5 coordinate images, together with the action of their coordinate normalizers. It is not a classification of Bol folders, and it does not assume an odd top, top surjectivity, an exact-factorization presentation, or a particular selected marked set.

Let I be finite and choose an actual embedded L_i≤A5 at each coordinate, with specified complements as below. The proper A5 subgroup inventory is {1,C2,C3,C5,V4,S3,D10,A4}; see the A5 local-group analysis in [paired support](radical-free-a5-nonsurjective-paired-support.md), reviewed at [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/82c3b17fc6f25e1a0dc6b0096219ad0efad5a41d). For abelian L_i put M_i=L_i and D_i=1. For S3, D10, A4 respectively choose M_i=C3,C5,V4 and actual complements D_i=C2,C2,C3. Then L=∏L_i=M⋊D with M=⊕_p M_p elementary abelian at p=2,3,5 and D=D2×D3 elementary abelian. A coordinate of D2 acts only on a 3- or 5-primary M coordinate, and a coordinate of D3 acts only on a 2-primary V4 coordinate. In particular D_p centralizes M_p for p=2,3; D5=1. The prescribed complements fix the section D→L used in all cocycle formulas.

## Exact embedded subgroup data

The subgroups R≤L whose i-th projection is exactly L_i correspond bijectively, with L and its chosen complements fixed, to triples (B,W,z) satisfying:

* B≤D is a vector subspace respecting its canonical primary splitting B=B2×B3;
* W≤M is B-invariant, equivalently W=⊕_p W_p with W_p a B-invariant Fp-subspace;
* z:B→M/W is a normalized 1-cocycle for the induced action, z(bc)=z(b)+b·z(c);
* for each i, the projection to D_i of B is all D_i, and the subgroup of L_i generated by the projection of W and the i-th coordinates of (representatives of) (z(b),b), b∈B, is L_i.

The actual subgroup is R={ (m,b) : b∈B, m+W=z(b) }. The stated projection test is independent of representatives and can be checked using a basis of B and W. Conversely, R∩M=W, pr_D(R)=B, and its cosets in M/W are uniquely z(b). Subgroup closure is precisely the cocycle identity. Thus the data are exact and no unrestricted subgroup search in L is needed.

## Linear cohomology reduction

For p∈{2,3,5} put A_p=M_p/W_p. The B-action on A_p factors through B_{p'} and B_p acts trivially. Inflation-restriction and coprime averaging give the canonical isomorphism

 H¹(B,A_p) ≅ Hom(B_p,A_p^{B_{p'}}).

For p=5 the right side is 0. For p=2 it is Hom(B2,A2^{B3}); for p=3 it is Hom(B3,A3^{B2}). Explicitly, every cocycle has the form

 z_p(b2,b3)=ℓ_p(b_p)+(b−1)a_p,

where ℓ_p lies in that Hom space (ℓ5=0), and a_p∈A_p. Conversely every such formula is a cocycle. To prove it, restriction to B_p is a homomorphism into B_{p'}-fixed elements, and subtracting it leaves a cocycle on the p'-group B_{p'}, killed by averaging. The choice of a_p is determined modulo A_p^B, while ℓ_p is uniquely the restriction to B_p. In particular the nontrivial cohomology parameters are only a binary linear map on reflection shadows and a ternary linear map on A4 shadows, with the indicated fixed-space constraints. The B-invariant W_p can further be decomposed into isotypic character blocks for B_{p'} over Fp; if Fp lacks the relevant roots, use irreducible Fp[B_{p'}]-summands rather than incorrectly assuming all characters are Fp-valued.

The a_p terms are essential for exact embedded subgroups. A conjugation by an element of M changes z by a coboundary and can eliminate them in an abstract M-conjugacy classification. But arbitrary M-conjugation on proper coordinates need not extend to a whole-folder conjugation preserving τ, the full strips, the other endpoint frames, or the chosen top image. Thus no such elimination is justified in an actual folder until a compatible gauge is constructed. This is the same distinction that the reviewed nilpotent-top reflection-free gauge resolves in its narrower setting, not an assumption that it works for arbitrary odd tops.

### With full projections, only abelian coordinates receive cohomology

Let E2 be the sum of M2 coordinates whose actual L_i is abelian C2 or V4, and E3 the sum of coordinates whose actual L_i is C3. Full local projections force B3 onto the nontrivial C3 complement at each A4 coordinate, and B2 onto the reflection complement at each S3 coordinate. Those actions have no nonzero fixed vector on the respective V4 and C3 modules. Therefore `M2^{B3}=E2` and `M3^{B2}=E3`. Coprime averaging makes invariants exact for B-invariant quotients, even when W has correlated coordinate projections, giving canonical identifications

    (M2/W2)^{B3} = image(E2) ≅ E2/(W2∩E2),
    (M3/W3)^{B2} = image(E3) ≅ E3/(W3∩E3).

Indeed lift a fixed quotient vector and average its lift over the prime-to-p group; this produces an invariant lift. Thus ℓ2 and ℓ3 take values entirely in these abelian-coordinate quotients. If E2=E3=0, then `H¹(B,M/W)=0`. This last statement permits elimination of all cocycle classes under ambient M-conjugacy; it still does not discard the actual coboundaries for a τ-constrained folder problem.

### Small controls

In D10², take M5=F5². If B2 is the diagonal reflection subgroup, both coordinate sign characters agree, and the correlated line W5={(x,x):x∈F5} is B2-invariant. If B2=C2² acts by independent coordinate reflections, that line is not invariant: changing just one sign sends (1,1) outside it. Thus correlated codes are allowed within equal-character blocks but cannot cross distinct sign characters.

In A4×C2, the C2 factor belongs to M2, not D2. This is therefore a control on the invariant code W2, not a binary cohomology parameter. If the A4 C3-shadow is active, it acts irreducibly on V4 and trivially on the other C2, so every invariant W2 splits across those two isotypic parts. If the shadow vanishes, correlated lines such as ⟨(v,1)⟩ with v≠0 are possible; they then fail the full A4-projection test. Similarly in S3×C3, the abelian C3 is part of M3, not D3. An active S3 reflection separates the sign and trivial M3-character blocks; if it vanishes, a diagonal C3 code is allowed, again with the reduced local projection. These tests prevent confusion between code correlations and lift cohomology.

For a genuine binary lift control, use A4×D10: the D10 reflection contributes B2. Its possible homomorphism into a V4 quotient is zero when the A4 B3 shadow is active, since that quotient has no fixed vector. If the A4 shadow vanishes, the Hom space can be nonzero, but that datum fails full A4 projection. For a ternary lift control use S3×A4: the A4 shadow supplies B3, and its homomorphism into the S3 normal C3 quotient vanishes under an active reflection. Nonzero classes after removing that reflection fail full S3 projection. These are analytic controls on the parameters and the independent projection test, not Bol folders.

## Exact coordinate-normalizer equivalence

Within the fixed product L, two data describe the identical embedded R iff B,W,z agree exactly. For ambient coordinate relabeling let ν consist of an allowed permutation of coordinates and local maps from S5 carrying each L_i to its target L'_{ν(i)}. Such maps give α:M→M', β:D→D' and a uniquely determined normalized 1-cocycle t:D→M' by ν(0,d)=(t(d),β(d)). They carry (B,W,z) to

 B'=β(B),   W'=α(W),
 z'(β b)=α(z(b))+t(b) mod W'.

This formula is necessary and sufficient for νRν⁻¹=R'. To classify subgroups up to the entire product S5≀Sym(I), range over precisely the coordinate maps that take the actual projection groups to their target projection groups. For an actual canonical folder, restrict further to those ambient maps that extend to the full socle/top automorphism, carry the full strips and H-lifts, and commute with τ. Such admissibility is a separate global condition and can be smaller than the full coordinate normalizer. Formula above is not asserted to be the full loop-isomorphism criterion.

## Full strips and relation to existing path results

For a general H0≤A5^Ω, split the full coordinate projections into Scott strips. All other proper-coordinate images are solvable; Goursat splits the product of the full A5 strips from the solvable residual R. This full/proper splitting is the [strip-forest interface](radical-free-strip-forests.md), independently reviewed at [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/6d3a97daa9cf20bbae0416295c8c2374c2cd8ede). Thus these codes and cochains apply to that actual residual, including when H0 contains individual full singleton strips. H0's solvable radical is precisely R, so R and its characteristic primary structure are intrinsic. Actual H-lifts normalize R and permute its strips and coordinate frames, but need not induce the pure Q-action.

The existing reviewed nilpotent-top path parametrization has a much narrower C5/A4 endpoint structure and additional global sign-saturation, acyclic graph and τ-compatible gauge deductions. It does not make these assumptions automatic for arbitrary odd nonsurjective tops or S3/D10 local projections. The present algebraic lemma handles all proper local A5 types and any top, but by itself imposes no selected-mark collision, normal-subloop, core, generation, simplicity or properness condition. To obtain a folder classification one must add those actual conditions and the extension of the displayed coordinate-normalizer action to the full folder. The exact-source reviews below apply to this algebraic subgroup theorem.
