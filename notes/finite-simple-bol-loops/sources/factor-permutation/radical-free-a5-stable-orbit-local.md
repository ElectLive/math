# Direct-sum moments on an inversion-stable A5 factor orbit

**Mira Cult**

**Independently reviewed theorem under pinned inputs.** This continues [0323-finite-simple-non-moufang-bol-loops:C0010](../../claims/0010.md). Let (G,H,K,τ) be the faithful generated canonical folder of a finite simple nonassociative left Bol loop. Suppose Soc(G)=N=A5^Ω and G=N⋊Q, with Q finite odd nilpotent acting faithfully by pure factor permutations. Let O⊆Ω be one Q-orbit preserved setwise by τ. The strict normal-quotient criterion gives HN=G, because a proper subgroup of nilpotent Q lies in a proper normal maximal subgroup. Put H0=H∩N, choose i∈O, write H_i for the actual H-stabilizer of factor i, J_i≤A5 for its local image, and L_i≤A5 for the local projection of H0.

Then exactly one of the following necessary local alternatives holds:

* L_i=J_i=A5; or
* J_i=A4 and L_i is V4 or A4. In this case EVERY selected marked involution has a double-transposition local mark at EVERY one of its fixed factors in O. Consequently τ has even local outer parity throughout O.

These are necessary conditions for any such folder; neither alternative is asserted to occur. This is not a paired-orbit theorem: the full-projection option can include diagonal strips coupled to other orbits, and the proper option is a necessary moment constraint rather than an actual realization. The existing [cross-orbit strip restriction](radical-free-a5-orbit-tensors.md) was independently reviewed at exact source [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/f86624c05f531304f76d6bc69aa0f0b1dc73759e) and retains its separate scope.

Let ψ=sgn⊗(the six-point Sylow-5 permutation representation minus the trivial module), an S5-extension of the irreducible five-dimensional A5-module. Its values on the involution types identity, double transposition and transposition are 5,1,1. For O form the direct sum U=⊕_{j∈O}ψ_j, with N acting through the j-th simple factor on ψ_j, and Q permuting the distinct summands. As an N-module the summands are pairwise inequivalent, and transitivity of Q makes U irreducible as a G-module. Since τ preserves O, U extends via the natural S5≀Sym(O) automorphism action to G⋊<τ>.

Restriction/transport identifies U^H with ψ^{J_i}: an H-fixed vector is determined by its component at i, which must be fixed by the actual factor stabilizer; conversely such a vector is transported to all factors of O. Every λ∈Λ has an involutory permutation on the odd-sized set O and hence at least one fixed factor. Its trace on U is the sum of the positive local ψ-values at these fixed factors; swapped summands contribute zero. Thus every λ has strictly positive trace. Since Λ is G-invariant, Σ_{λ∈Λ}ρ(λ) is scalar on the G-irreducible U and has positive trace, hence is nonzero. The original transversal identity (Σ_K k)(Σ_H h)=Σ_G g forces this scalar to annihilate U^H, so ψ^{J_i}=0.

If L_i=A5 then J_i=A5. Suppose L_i is proper. If L_i=1, the local map H_i→A5 factors through H_i/H0, a subgroup of odd nilpotent Q; its image is 1, C3 or C5, each having a nonzero ψ-fixed vector. If L_i≠1, it is normal in J_i, so J_i lies in its proper A5-normalizer. The proper-subgroup list and character averaging give ψ-fixed dimension 0 only for A4. Explicitly, the dimensions on 1, C2, C3, V4, C5, S3, D10, A4 are respectively 5, 3, 1, 2, 1, 1, 1, 0. Therefore J_i=A4. As J_i/L_i is an odd quotient of a subgroup of Q, and the normal subgroups of A4 are 1,V4,A4, only L_i=V4 or A4 are possible.

In this proper case replace ψ by the natural deleted five-point A5-representation χ4, also extended to S5, and form its direct sum over O. It is G-irreducible and has an H-fixed vector because A4 fixes a point of the natural five-point action. On the three local involution types χ4 takes values 4,0,2, respectively. Its marked-class sum is a scalar and annihilates the H-fixed vector; each selected λ has a nonnegative trace equal to the sum of its fixed-factor local values. Hence each term, and every summand of each term, is zero. Every fixed factor of EVERY selected marked involution therefore has a double-transposition local mark.

Finally local outer parity of τ is constant on O: conjugating pure Q-factor permutations by τ produces elements of G, whose local components are inner A5 automorphisms. Each marked involution has the same local parity as τ, since its G multiplier has even local components. An involution on an odd orbit has a fixed factor, and a double transposition is even. Thus τ's local parity is even on O. No claim is made about τ-paired orbits, arbitrary nonnilpotent odd tops, or exact-factorization exhaustiveness; independent exact-source review is recorded below. No computation is used. The next obligation is to combine these local restrictions with the global correlations between stable and paired factor orbits. No permanent claim ID is allocated; C0010 and the unrestricted classification remain partial.

A useful exact marked-weight consequence is that, for any collection of inversion-stable orbits with proper H0 projections, every one of their fixed factors contributes precisely its double-transposition class size 15 to each original marked N-class. If their total fixed-factor count is f_s, the global correlated fixed-factor weight has a factor 15^{f_s}; after removing it, only the other fixed factors contribute to the remaining selection weight. This removes local choices on those proper orbits without asserting independence of any remaining marks.
