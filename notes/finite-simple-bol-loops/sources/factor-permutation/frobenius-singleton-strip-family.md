## The exact factorization in S

The complement C_m fixes the identity coordinate e∈F. Define

    Y0=diag(B)⋊P,
    Y1={ (a,c)∈T^F⋊C_m : a_e∈C and χ(a_e)=c }.

Y1 is a subgroup: evaluation at e is a homomorphism on T^F⋊C_m, and the defining relation is a fiber product. The intersection Y0∩Y1 is trivial. Indeed equality of their e-coordinate gives an element of B∩C=1, so the diagonal B base is trivial; χ(1)=1 forces the C_m component to be trivial, and Y1 has no F top component. Furthermore

    |Y0|=|B|mn,       |Y1|=|C|·|T|^(n−1),
    |Y0||Y1|=|T|^n mn=|S|.

Thus S=Y0Y1 is an exact factorization. In particular

    H=G∩(Y0×Y1),      K={(s,s⁻¹):s∈S},

is the standard exact-factorization folder, and |K|=|S|. The F-kernel is [F,c] for every nonidentity c∈C_m: for any nonidentity c∈C_m the map f↦f⁻¹f^c is injective by fixed-point-freeness and hence surjective. Thus P_ab≅C_m. Since T^F is perfect, S′=T^F⋊F. The anti-diagonal generators contain S′×S′ (the commutator calculation in the pinned interface), and their abelianization image is exactly the anti-diagonal C_m. Hence the group generated by K is precisely G. Hence G is the faithful generated canonical group once H is core-free.

Let N1,N2 be the two T^F rows and N=N1×N2. On the top quotient

    Q=G/N=(F×F)⋊C_m,

C_m acts by its given action on the first F and inverse action on the second. The image of H is U≅F⋊C_m, with first F full and second F trivial. Its socle intersection is

    H0=diag(B) × (kerχ at e × ∏_{x≠e}T_x).                  (1)

The n−1 full singleton T factors on the second row are genuine original full strips of H0. The subgroup H0 has order |T|^n/m, H has order n|T|^n, and [G:H]=mn|T|^n. These singleton full projections distinguish this family from the earlier proper-only endpoint family, even under arbitrary canonical-group isomorphisms: the socle and its simple factors are characteristic as a collection, so the number of full coordinate projections of H∩Soc(G) is preserved. The earlier family has none, whereas here there are n−1>0.

## Every original normal cut is strict

The factor action of Q is transitive on each row and faithful, so C_G(N)=1. Any nontrivial normal subgroup of G intersects N in N1, N2 or N. If its intersection is N1, its image in G/N1 centralizes N2 and lies in the first F kernel of the second-row action. It is therefore N1⋊D for some Q-normal D≤F in that first kernel. Similarly an intersection N2 gives N2⋊D for Q-normal D≤F in the second kernel. If it contains N it is N⋊D for D◁Q. These descriptions include arbitrary F-invariant normal subgroups and make no assumption that D is τ-stable.

Write I for the top labels of K in Q. They are the anti-diagonal images of all p∈P, so |I|=mn and either top-coordinate projection maps I bijectively onto P. For N1⋊D, retaining the whole second row and quotienting the first F by D leaves the second top projection untouched. Hence |π_D(I)|=mn and |KN1D/N1D|=mn|T|^n. The actual projected stabilizer has the second-row base in (1), with top image of order mn/|D|; consequently

    [G:HN1D]=n|T|/|kerχ|=nm|B|,
    |KN1D/N1D|/[G:HN1D]=|C|·|T|^(n−1)>1.                 (2)

The hidden D lifts may be chosen with identity in the retained second row, so they add no retained base.

For N2⋊D, retaining the first row, U meets the second F kernel trivially, and the projected stabilizer base is diag(B). The first top projection again makes |π_D(I)|=mn. Thus

    [G:HN2D]=n|T|^n/(|B||D|),
    |KN2D/N2D|/[G:HN2D]=m|B||D|>1.                         (3)

Finally let L=N⋊D with D◁Q and D<Q. If D≤F×F, put B_D=pr2(D)≤F. Since U contains the entire first F and the complement, UD contains (1,B_D) and

    [Q:UD]=n/|B_D|.

Projection of I to the second top coordinate is all of P; modulo D it still surjects onto P/B_D. Therefore

    |π_D(I)|≥mn/|B_D|=m[Q:UD]>[Q:UD].                       (4)

No splitting of D inside F×F is used.

If D is not contained in F×F, choose d∈D with nonidentity complement component c. For each nonidentity c the bijection f↦f⁻¹c(f) shows every (f,c)∈P is F-conjugate to c. Thus both top-coordinate conjugation actions of d on F are fixed-point-free, even when F is nonabelian. Consequently [F,d]=F on each of the two normal F factors. Normality of D forces F×F≤D. The image of D in C_m is proper because D<Q; U maps onto all of C_m, while I maps onto all of C_m as well. Hence [Q:UD]=1 but |π_D(I)|>1. This covers all proper nontrivial normal subgroups. The inequalities are original projected-section inequalities; none assumes the projected data form a loop.

The original strict normal-subloop criterion therefore gives simplicity. H is core-free because every nontrivial normal subgroup meets N and H0 contains neither complete N row: diag(B) is proper on the first row and kerχ is proper on the distinguished second factor. Since H≠1, the faithful loop is nonassociative. Also H<N⋊U<G because U<Q and n>1, so its canonical left multiplication group is imprimitive. Under the pinned simple-Moufang primitivity theorem, the loop is not Moufang.

For T=A5, B=C5, C=A4, χ:A4→C3, F=C7 and C3 acting by multiplication 2, the construction gives the fixed Frobenius-147 example with H0=diag(C5)×(V4×A5^6) and order 21·60^7. This places that example inside the classical exact-factorization route. This does not exhaust exact factorizations in the same group S.


## Exact isomorphism criterion within this family

For the stated data, the loops are isomorphic exactly when there are isomorphisms γ:T→T̃, β:F→F̃ and r:C_m→C_m̃ with γ(B)=B̃, γ(C)=C̃, χ̃γ|C=rχ, and βρ(c)β⁻¹=ρ̃(r(c)). This reviewed equivalence is conditional on the pinned canonical-folder functoriality and intrinsic centralizer recovery of S.

Necessity: the two intrinsic minimal socle blocks and their centralizers recover S and identify any τ-commuting folder isomorphism with the diagonal square of an isomorphism S→S̃, possibly followed by swap. The endpoint top images in S/T^F are P and C_m, of unequal order, so the distinguished endpoints cannot swap. The coordinate permutation f conjugates the regular F actions, so f(x)=uβ(x). The second endpoint's base intersection has one coordinate projection ker χ<C and all other projections T, so it singles out the identity coordinate. Thus f(e)=e and u=1. Its top image is the distinguished complement, yielding r and the action intertwining equation.

The faithful embedding S≤Aut(T^F) determines an isomorphism by its socle action. Write its coordinate isomorphisms as a_x. Since the top action is pure and transitive, the outer classes of a_x are constant; write a_x=Inn(t_x)γ0. Conjugating a pure top element then gives the coordinate correction t_x t_(p⁻¹x)⁻¹, after relabeling. There is no independent unseen top cocycle because C_S(T^F)=1. The first endpoint's diagonal B base projects isomorphically onto B in every coordinate; its image is the diagonal B̃, so every a_x restricts to the same map B→B̃. At the identity coordinate the second endpoint base gives a_e(ker χ)=ker χ̃. More strongly, its entire image under evaluation at e is the graph {(c,χ(c)):c∈C} in T×C_m. An automorphism of S has coordinate correction t_e t_{p⁻¹e}⁻¹ at the identity, after aligning the standard pure top representatives. For p in the complement, p fixes e, so this correction is 1. Therefore evaluating the second endpoint forces a_e(C)=C̃ and χ̃ a_e = rχ. Taking γ=a_e gives γ(B)=B̃ as well. This derivation does not require all a_x to agree or the top cocycle to vanish.

Conversely, such γ,β,r give the coordinatewise γ and β-relabeling isomorphism of the semidirect products; it maps both endpoints, hence the paired canonical folders, exactly. Extra coordinate gauges or complement cocycles can occur in an individual isomorphism, but cannot create identifications outside the displayed criterion. Endpoint order distinction requires F nontrivial. Both endpoint projections of H are full: Y0 and Y1 each surject onto C_m, so any endpoint element has an opposite endpoint with inverse abelian image. Also T^F is the unique minimal normal subgroup of S and C_S(T^F)=1, exactly as in the pinned recovery proof. Thus the recovered endpoint and faithfulness hypotheses used above hold here.

## All stabilizer top images in the first fixed envelope

In the A5,C7,C3 instance, fix the same entire canonical G and K but
allow any stabilizer satisfying the original faithful simple-folder
conditions. Write Q=(F7²)⋊C3 with c acting by diag(2,4), θ swapping
the two coordinates and inverting c, N=A5^14, U=HN/N and H0=H∩N.
The following necessary reduction does not assume an exact-factorization
origin or the special H constructed above.

**Reviewed theorem under the stated inputs.** U has order 21 and, up to an actual θ-compatible automorphism of Q, is the standard F7⋊C3 subgroup using either one of the two eigenlines. Thus there is exactly one allowed θ-compatible top-image orbit. Its index is 7 and |H0|=60^7/3.

Proof. Let L=N⋊V, with V=F7²=Q′. It is a nontrivial proper normal subgroup. The actual full marked section K projects onto all three elements of G/L≅C3. If U mapped trivially to Q/V then HL=L and [G:HL]=3=|KL/L|, contradicting the original strict normal-quotient criterion for a simple folder. Thus U maps onto Q/V≅C3. Let W=U∩V. Conjugation by a lift of a generator of Q/V acts on V with distinct eigenvalues 2 and 4. Therefore W is 0, the 2-eigenline, the 4-eigenline, or V. In particular the possible orders of U are 3,21,147. This also follows directly from the full transversal's Q-normal quotient, without assuming U is θ-stable.

The actual transversal gives |H|=|G|/|K|=7·60^7. Thus

 |H0|=|H|/|U| = 60^7 [Q:U]/21.

If |U|=147 then this is not an integer, since 7∤60. If |U|=3, it is 7·60^7/3, which has a factor 7; but H0≤N and 7∤|N|. Therefore |U|=21, W is one eigenline and the displayed value of |H0| follows.

For the exact θ-compatible orbit, θ itself interchanges the two eigenlines and commutes with θ. Fix the first eigenline. Each order-21 U over that line is determined by the second coordinate of a lift of c, because first-coordinate shifts are absorbed by W. Conjugation by any θ-fixed diagonal vector (a,a) is an inner automorphism commuting with θ; its effect on this second coordinate is multiplication by the nonzero scalar 1−4. Varying a realizes all second-coordinate shifts. Each such vector has a pure-top lift in G fixed by τ, so its inner conjugation commutes with τ and preserves the actual full K (equivalently its full outside conjugacy class). Canonical τ itself lifts θ and preserves K because K is inverse-closed. These actual τ-compatible automorphisms are transitive on the order-21 subgroups, including the interchange of the two eigenlines. ∎


After the top image is normalized, its actions on the two socle rows
have orbit sizes 7 and 1,3,3. Classifying all H0 and coherent lifts,
subject to every actual collision and strict normal cut, remains open.
