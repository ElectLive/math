# Exclusion of a single faithful transitive odd-nilpotent A5 factor orbit

**Mira Cult**

**Independently reviewed exclusion under pinned inputs.** This continues [0323-finite-simple-non-moufang-bol-loops:C0010](../../claims/0010.md). Let (G,H,K,τ) be the faithful generated canonical folder of a finite simple nonassociative left Bol loop. Suppose Soc(G)=N=A5^Ω and G=N⋊Q, where Q is a nontrivial finite odd nilpotent group acting faithfully and transitively on Ω by pure factor permutations. Then no such folder exists. The prime-power case is proved first, followed by the mixed-prime extension. This does not assume that Q acts regularly or that the envelope is a paired wreath product. It treats a single factor orbit; it does not cover multiple orbits, nonnilpotent odd tops or arbitrary outer lifts.

The faithful-folder and strict normal-quotient inputs are C0001 at [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/9431ade99316d6bc62efb389492fef385b510b45); canonical inversion and G-conjugacy-invariance of Λ=τK use C0007 at [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/932ead5db5f39045e2085caa6b062f6b981b2813). The A5 subgroup and diagonal-strip facts are those pinned in [the A5 path source](radical-free-a5-path-classification.md) at [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/ad94accf574f89bf1e8487bb046cf30fb685cd0c). The actual-H class-sum identity and the character ψ below are in the reviewed fixed-coordinate analysis [of canonical A5 inversion](radical-free-a5-code-inversion.md) at [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/42e39c3cfbb5ded0d294a1a01fd6ab7dea8d6c0e). ## Fixed factors and the actual marked-weight bound

Assume first that Q is a nontrivial p-group. Let n=|Ω| and let θ be τ's induced involution on Q. Simplicity forces HN=G. Otherwise HN/N is contained in a maximal subgroup of the finite p-group Q; that maximal subgroup is normal of index p. Its preimage is a proper normal subgroup containing H, and G=HK makes K map onto its quotient, giving equality in the cited strict normal-quotient test. Put H0=H∩N.

τ induces inversion on Q_ab: G is generated by its τ-inverted section K and N is perfect, so G_ab=Q_ab. Consequently θ is nontrivial, since a nontrivial finite p-group has nontrivial abelianization. The action degree n is odd, so τ fixes a factor. Let L be that factor's Q-stabilizer; θ(L)=L. Put f equal to the number of τ-fixed factors, and k=[Q:C_Q(θ)]. Coprime fixed-coset lifting gives

    f=[C_Q(θ):C_L(θ)],   n=[Q:L],   k=(n/f)[L:C_L(θ)].          (1)

For completeness, if qL is θ-fixed, then q⁻¹θ(q)∈L defines an outside involution of L⋊<θ>; all such involutions are L-conjugate because |L| is odd, so qL has a representative fixed by θ. In particular f divides n. If f=n, τ is the identity permutation on Ω, and faithful Q-action forces θ=1, contradiction. Thus n/f is a nontrivial p-power and

    n≥pf.                                                       (2)

The outside involutions of Q⋊<θ> form one Q-conjugacy class, whose k members have the same number f of fixed factors and (n−f)/2 swapped pairs. Because Λ=τK is G-conjugacy invariant and contains τ, its selected N-class weight is the same at all k top labels. A swapped pair contributes a class factor 60. A fixed factor contributes either an even local identity/double-transposition choice of sizes 1 or15, or an odd local transposition class of size10. Keep every correlation among these local choices: their actual total weight D only needs

    1≤D≤16^f,
    |K|=k·60^{(n−f)/2}D,
    |H0|=60^{(n+f)/2}/(kD).                                    (3)

The parity can even vary between fixed factors; the bound remains valid. The size formula uses |H||K|=|G| and HN=G, not an induced quotient folder. Since k is a nontrivial p-power, integrality in (3) immediately gives p∈{3,5}.

## A positive character moment excludes 5-containing proper projections

H maps onto Q, so its conjugation permutes the coordinate projections of H0 transitively. Suppose they are proper and contain elements of order 5. Each is C5 or D10, and its A5-normalizer is D10. Choose coordinate identifications so these normalizers are the same D10 in each factor; then every local component of actual H normalizes the appropriate projection and lies in that D10, while its top part permutes factors. Conjugating the coordinate identifications by A5 elements only introduces inner local components, so it does not permit an outer F20 stabilizer action.

Let ψ=sgn⊗(the six-point Sylow-5 permutation character minus 1), regarded as the degree-five irreducible representation of A5 extended to S5. Its D10-fixed space is a line: D10 has orbit sizes 1 and 5 on the six Sylow-5 subgroups, so the deleted permutation module has one fixed vector, and D10 consists of even permutations. Its values on identity, double transpositions and transpositions are respectively 5,1,1. In the N-irreducible tensor representation ψ^{⊗n}, the tensor of these fixed lines is therefore fixed by actual H, including its factor permutations. This representation extends to the automorphism wreath S5≀Sym(Ω), and hence to the formal G⋊<τ> through its conjugation action on N. The restriction to G is irreducible because its restriction to N already is. For any λ∈Λ, the trace of λ on ψ^{⊗n} is 5 per swapped pair times one of 5,1,1 at every fixed factor: λ²=1 makes the local product on a swapped pair the identity. Thus every such trace is strictly positive.

Since Λ is G-invariant, its representation sum commutes with G and is a scalar on this irreducible G-module. Its positive trace makes that scalar nonzero. The inverse-closed transversal K gives the unique product G=KH as well as G=HK. Thus (Σ_{k∈K}k)(Σ_{h∈H}h)=Σ_{g∈G}g and nontriviality of the representation imply that the Λ-sum annihilates every H-fixed vector. This contradiction excludes all 5-containing proper coordinate projections, without assuming independent residual projections or independent marks.

## Full projections and the remaining valuation test

If H0 has one full A5 projection then transitivity makes all projections full, and H0 is a product of full twisted diagonal strips. Their partition is Q-invariant and has equal block sizes. Singleton strips give H0=N, contradicting corefreeness; otherwise every block has odd size at least 3, so if m is the number of strips, m≤n/3. Put

    A=(n+f)/2,  h=A−m ≥ n/6+f/2.

Then (3) gives 60^h=kD. For p=3, k is a power of 3, so 20^h divides D. By (2), h≥f, and D≥20^f>16^f, contradiction. For p=5, 12^h divides D; by (2), h≥4f/3, so D≥12^{4f/3}>16^f, again a contradiction. Thus all H0 projections are proper.

The character moment now says these proper projections have no factor 5, so 5 does not divide |H0|. If p=3, k is a 3-power and (3) forces 5^A|D. Since n≥3f, 5^A≥25^f>16^f, contradiction.

If p=5, the faithful embedding Q≤Sym(n) and n a 5-power give

    v5(k)≤v5(|Q|)≤v5(n!)=(n−1)/4.

Since 5∤|H0|, equation (3) gives

    v5(D)=A−v5(k)≥(n+2f+1)/4≥(7f+1)/4.

Therefore D≥5^{(7f+1)/4}>16^f, because 5^7>16^4. This final contradiction proves the theorem.

## Extension to every odd nilpotent top

Write Q=∏_p Q_p, its Sylow direct product. Simplicity forces HN=G: a proper subgroup of a finite nilpotent group lies in a proper normal maximal subgroup, and its inverse image contradicts the same strict normal-quotient test as in the p-group argument. Choose a τ-fixed factor (the degree is odd) and let L be its θ-stable stabilizer in Q, where θ is τ's induced automorphism. Nilpotence gives L=∏_p L_p with L_p=L∩Q_p. Hence the factor set identifies as a Q-set with

    Ω≅Q/L≅∏_p Q_p/L_p.

Let n_p=[Q_p:L_p] and n=|Ω|=∏ n_p. Faithfulness of Q on Ω makes every Q_p faithful on Q_p/L_p: the kernel is ∏ core_{Q_p}(L_p). In particular n_p>1 whenever Q_p≠1. Each Q_p is characteristic, so θ preserves it. Because G is generated by τ-inverted K and N is perfect, θ inverts Q_ab; its restriction to (Q_p)_ab is inversion. A nontrivial finite p-group cannot have trivial abelianization, so θ is nontrivial on every nontrivial Q_p.

By coprime fixed-coset lifting, the number of θ-fixed points on Q_p/L_p is

    f_p=[C_{Q_p}(θ):C_{L_p}(θ)].

The fixed-point set on Ω is the product, so f=∏ f_p; similarly n/f=∏ n_p/f_p. Each f_p divides n_p. If f_p=n_p, θ acts trivially on the faithful Q_p-permutation action and hence on Q_p, contradicting the preceding paragraph. Thus n_p/f_p≥p and

    n≥f·∏_{Q_p≠1}p.                                             (1)

All outside involutions of Q⋊<θ> are Q-conjugate because |Q| is odd. Let k=[Q:C_Q(θ)]=∏ k_p. The actual selected marked classes have a common weight over the k inverted top labels, and a fixed factor contributes at most 16 local involutions while a swapped pair contributes 60. With 1≤D≤16^f and H0=H∩N,

    |K|=k·60^{(n−f)/2}D,
    |H0|=60^{(n+f)/2}/(kD).                                    (2)

The marked choices may be fully correlated. Every nontrivial Q_p has k_p>1, so integrality in (2) forces every prime in Q to be 3 or 5. If only one prime occurs, the prime-power argument above applies. Assume both occur; (1) gives n≥15f.

All H0 projections are H-conjugate because H maps onto transitive Q. Full projections give a product of full diagonal strips. Singleton strips put N≤H, contrary to corefreeness; otherwise the equal odd block sizes are at least 3, so the number of strips m≤n/3. With h=(n+f)/2−m, equation (2) gives 60^h=kD. Since k is odd, 4^h divides D. But h≥n/6+f/2≥3f, so D≥64^f>16^f, contradiction.

Thus the projections are proper. If they contain 5, the degree-five ψ tensor moment above gives an actual H-fixed vector and has strictly positive trace on every selected marked involution, contradicting the transversal class-sum identity. Hence 5∤|H0|. Equation (2) forces v5(D)=(n+f)/2−v5(k). Since the faithful Q5-action has degree n5, it embeds in Sym(n5), so v5(k)≤v5(|Q5|)≤(n5−1)/4. The nontrivial 3-factor has n3≥3, hence n5≤n/3. Therefore

    v5(D)≥(n+f)/2−(n5−1)/4≥(5n+6f+3)/12≥(27f+1)/4.

It follows that D≥5^{(27f+1)/4}>16^f, contradiction. This proves the stated odd-nilpotent theorem. Neither mixed marked-class independence nor a quotient loop was used.

## A positive-character interface for multiple orbits

The proper-projection argument has a useful independent scope. Let G=A5^Ω⋊Q have a faithful pure factor-permutation action, not necessarily transitive or of prime-power order. Suppose (G,H,K,τ) is a faithful generated canonical folder with H surjecting onto Q. There is no nonempty τ-stable union U of Q-orbits on which every H0 coordinate projection is proper and contains 5. Indeed label ψ on U and the trivial representation on its complement. This remains a nontrivial irreducible N-module extending to G⋊<τ>, since U is stable under both actions. Every selected outside involution has positive trace on it. The characteristic C5 and its D10 normalizer provide the H-fixed tensor as above, giving the same class-sum contradiction. This argument uses the actual H action, without a quotient-folder construction or independence of residual projections. It does not handle outer F20 local actions, nor does it eliminate all other possible projections on a τ-stable orbit.
