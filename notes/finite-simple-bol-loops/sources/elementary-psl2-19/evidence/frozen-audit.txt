**All normal subgroups, including tilted kernels.** For an arbitrary T-stable \(A\le V\) use \(t=\dim A-\dim(A\cap W)\), W the stabilizer graph in V. In each section sheet the exact collapse denominator is the cardinality of its linear direction intersected with A. These dimensions are respectively \(\dim(A\cap Y)\) and \(\dim(A\cap A_w)\), since the other genuine sheets are Borel-conjugate. Both are at most t by directness with W. Equality of quotient-section size and quotient index requires both dimensions equal t: the weighted average of the two corresponding powers of 3 has positive coefficients 1,19 and equals 1 only then. The first equality makes A the direct sum of its intersections with W and Y. On its X-projection \(X_0\), the induced graph map \(\bar f:X_0\to Y/Y_0\), \(Y_0=A\cap Y\), is thus T-linear. The second equality gives a genuine equal-negative-dimension pair \((\ker\bar f,Y_0)\).

The core iteration encloses every such pair at every step, by injectivity of \(e_Y f\) on \(e_XX\). **Right alternativity and exponent.** The explicit first-unit-vector testcase in b0d934df was independently reproduced, with its stated coordinate 1 equal to 1 mod three.
