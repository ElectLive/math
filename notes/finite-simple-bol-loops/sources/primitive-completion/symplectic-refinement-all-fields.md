# Minus-refinement exclusion via Bruck-loop orders

Mira Cult
**Independently reviewed under the scope below.** For every $`q=2^f\ge4`$ and $`r\ge2`$, no left Bol loop has full multiplication group contained in the natural affine semilinear $`\Gamma\mathop{\mathrm{Sp}}\nolimits_{2r}(q)`$ action on minus-type quadratic refinements. The deduction is **CFSG-dependent** through Baumeister–Stein; it does not certify the C0016 or C0017 aggregates. The plus-type action is excluded separately by the general displacement argument cited below.

This addresses the residual frontier in [C0010](../../claims/0010.md). It uses the [C0016 general $`\Delta_0`$ appendix](../foundations/C0016.md#general-delta_0-classification-and-fixed-point-argument), independently reviewed at its recorded source revision, and the separately reviewed $`q=4`$ argument linked there. The exponent-two step extends the argument in [the $`\mathop{\mathrm{Sp}}\nolimits_4`$ manuscript](sp4-minus-bruck-exclusion.md). Source revision inspected for this synthesis: the recorded source revision.

Let q=2^f>=4, r>=2, and V be a 2r-dimensional symplectic space. In the affine action of GammaSp(V) on minus-type quadratic refinements, suppose a left Bol loop Q has full multiplication group contained in that action. Center at the loop identity 0 and use the previously reviewed Delta_0 classification. For every nonzero Q_e-singular x, both translations have the form L_x(v)=v+N_x v+x, R_x(v)=v+N'_x v+x, with B(v,N_x v)=B(v,N'_x v)=0. Set N_0=0.

For any y in the loop write R_y(v)=T_y(v^sigma)+y. Comparing L_x(y) and R_y(x) gives B(y,T_y(x^sigma)+x)=0 for every Q_e-singular x. These vectors span V: fix a singular hyperbolic pair e,f; every vector a of its complement is the sum (a+e+Q_e(a)f)+e+Q_e(a)f of singular vectors. Replacing x by t x for arbitrary t in F_q gives t^sigma B(y,T_y(x^sigma))+t B(y,x)=0. If sigma is nontrivial, independence of t^sigma and t over F_q makes B(y,x)=0 for all singular x, so y=0. Thus every right translation is F_q-affine-linear. When sigma=1, spanning instead gives T_y^{-1}y=y, hence R_y(y)=T_y y+y=0. Therefore Q has exponent two. Interchanging left and right translations similarly gives F_q-linearity of all translations, although that is unnecessary for the order obstruction. The opposite loop is a right Bruck loop of exponent two.

By Baumeister and Stein, *The finite Bruck loops*, [arXiv:0908.2597v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0908.2597v2), Corollary 3.6, a finite Bruck loop of 2-power exponent has |Q|=2^a product_i(t_i+1), with each t_i=9 or a Fermat prime at least 5. Consequently the odd part of |Q| is a product of (t_i+1)/2. The cited source directly states the order formula and uses CFSG-dependent classification.

Every odd prime divisor ell of an allowed factor has v_2(ord_ell(2)) either 1 or 2. Indeed for t_i=9 the factor is 5 and ord_5(2)=4. For a Fermat prime t_i=2^(2^k)+1, k>=1, the factor is 2^(2^k-1)+1. Its exponent E=2^k-1 is odd. If ell divides this factor then 2^E=-1 mod ell, so ord_ell(2) divides 2E but not E, and therefore has 2-adic valuation exactly 1.

The number of minus refinements is q^r(q^r-1)/2, whose odd part is 2^(fr)-1. Write N=fr>=4. If N has an odd divisor h>1, any prime ell dividing 2^h-1 has ord_ell(2)>1 odd, contradicting the preceding restriction. Hence N is a power of 2. If N>=8, then 17 divides 2^N-1, but ord_17(2)=8, contradicting the restriction again. Thus N=4, forcing f=r=2. That q=4, r=2 action was excluded independently by the reviewed q=4 argument in C0016 at its recorded source revision.

Thus, conditional on the cited general Delta_0 classification, its action model, and the q=4 local exclusion, no left Bol loop exists in the full semilinear Sp_{2r}(q) action on minus refinements for r>=2 and q>=4. This implies no finite simple proper left Bol loop in those actions. The case q=2 is outside scope. automated reviewers a separate automated checker independently accepted this deduction at its recorded source revision, including the translation comparison, spanning and exponent-two steps, the arithmetic obstruction, and the stated use of Baumeister–Stein Corollary 3.6. The review assumes the previously reviewed general Delta_0 classification and q=4 exclusion, and covers the specified natural semilinear action. No CFSG-free claim or arbitrary almost-simple action identification is made.
