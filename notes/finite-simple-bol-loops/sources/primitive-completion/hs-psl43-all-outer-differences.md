# Candidate HS Case A exclusion for PSL4(3), all outer differences

Mira Cult
This result, with scoped independent review below, concerns only factor-preserving HS Case A for [C0010](../../claims/0010.md) and [C0014](../../claims/0014.md), conditional on the C0014 folder hypotheses and the CTblLib character table, fusion and group-identification inputs below. Put T=PSL4(3), n=6065280, B=T², and Out(T)=C2² with elements 1,d,g,dg, where d is the diagonal outer class and g the transpose-inverse outer class. For λ∈Λ let α,β be its induced automorphisms of the two factors. The quotient outer difference δ=[α][β]^-1 is constant over Λ because Λ⊆τG, G has diagonal outer image and Out(T) is abelian. The map λ↦(α,β) is injective: if two elements induce the same pair, their product is in G∩C_ext(B)=C_G(B)=1. This proof does not require the action of the full extension on B to be faithful.

Each B-orbit of λ is contained in Λ, giving for every λ (including components equal to 1) the necessary product condition |C_T(α)||C_T(β)|≥n. For every λ, the products of two elements of its B-orbit have coordinate sets C(α)×C(β), where C(γ)=(γ^T)². Since the folder condition gives Λ²∩H={1} and H∩B=Δ(T), this forces C(α)∩C(β)={1} for every λ, including identity-component cases; it is not restricted to the nondegenerate element singled out in C0014. A T-class of involutions with centralizer c has n/c elements; the identity contributes one and has centralizer n. We use these table inputs:

Count candidate ordered automorphism pairs, separately for each fixed δ, using the product and common-square tests; overcounting pairs from distinct λ-orbits is safe.

* δ=dg: inner nonidentity times dg is impossible (2880·720<n); identity/dg contributes at most 2·8424. For d/g only the two 51840-centralizer classes pass (5616·576<n), giving at most 4·1080·117 more. Total ≤522288<n.
* δ=g: d/dg is impossible (5616·720<n). For inner/g, two inner classes paired with either 51840 graph class contribute at most 2·7371·234; identity/g contributes 2·(117+117+10530). Total ≤3471156<n.
* δ=d: nonidentity inner/d is impossible by the common square class. Identity/d contributes 2·1080. For g/dg only the 51840-centralizer graph classes pass (576·720<n), contributing 2·234·8424. Total ≤3944592<n.
* δ=1: identity/identity and identity/nonidentity-inner contribute 1+2·7371. Nonidentity inner/inner pairs in the same class have identical nontrivial captured sets; the two distinct classes fail the product test (2880·1152<n). The unique d class paired with itself likewise fails the intersection test. The dg class paired with itself fails the product test (720²<n). For g/g, same-class pairs fail the intersection test, while all distinct-class pairs are conservatively counted: 2·117²+4·117·10530. Total ≤4970161<n.

For the same-class exclusion, C(α) is invariant under T-conjugacy and nontrivial for α≠1: some w has α(w)≠w, since otherwise α is the identity. The preceding explicit-pair obstruction remains valid: it concerns a single pair satisfying the pair tests, whereas this argument adds the size and B-conjugacy closure of the entire Λ. Thus in every outer-difference case |Λ|<n, contradicting |Λ|=n. This establishes the stated narrow exclusion only subject to the external CTblLib inputs and independent mathematical review; it makes no general claim for other socles or all primitive types.
