## Primitive group and marked automorphism

Let T=A₆≅PSL₂(9), t=|T|=360. Let b≥2 be even, and let P=S₇ wr S_b act naturally on k=7b points, partitioned into b blocks of size 7. The point stabilizer and the stabilizer of its block are

    Q=(S₆×S₇^(b−1))⋊S_{b−1},     B=S₇^b⋊S_{b−1}.

Define θ:Q→Aut(T) by the faithful conjugation action of the pointed S₆ on its normal A₆, with all other S₇ factors and S_{b−1} mapped to 1. The latter permutes only the other blocks, so θ is a homomorphism and its image contains Inn(T).

Use the induced-function twisted-wreath construction in Liebeck–Praeger–Saxl, *On the O'Nan–Scott theorem for finite primitive permutation groups*, J. Austral. Math. Soc. A **44** (1988), 389–396, §1 III(c), p.391, [DOI 10.1017/S144678870003216X](https://doi.org/10.1017/S144678870003216X). It produces G=N⋊P with N≅T^k, where N acts regularly on G/P and the local Q-action is θ. The source explicitly warns that its local image condition alone does not prove primitivity; we check it here.

No homomorphism B→Aut(T) extends θ. Restriction to the pointed S₇ would extend the faithful S₆ embedding, but |S₇|>|Aut(A₆)|=1440 and the only proper nontrivial normal subgroup of S₇ is A₇, so any homomorphism S₇→Aut(A₆) has image of order at most 2. Further, every subgroup Q<K≤P contains B: Q is maximal in B because S₆ is maximal in the pointed S₇; if K is not contained in B, take h∈K moving the pointed block. Q and hQh⁻¹ together contain every independent S₇ base factor, so generate S₇^b. Together with Q they generate B. Thus θ extends to no K>Q.

This nonextension proves primitivity. An intermediate subgroup P≤L≤G is (L∩N)P, where D=L∩N is P-invariant. Since θ(Q) contains Inn(T), each coordinate projection of D is a normal subgroup of T, and transitivity makes the projections all 1 or all T. For a nontrivial D they are all T. A subdirect subgroup of a finite direct power of a nonabelian simple group is a product of full diagonal subgroups on a partition of its coordinates. If D is proper, the block containing the distinguished coordinate has stabilizer K>Q; the action of K on the corresponding diagonal copy of T gives a homomorphism K→Aut(T) extending θ. This contradicts nonextension. Hence D is 1 or N, and P is maximal in G. Conversely, extension of θ to a block stabilizer gives the proper invariant subgroup of functions satisfying the stronger block-equivariance relation. This explains the nonextension test; its review is recorded below.

Let α be a field involution of PSL₂(9) and let b₀=(α,…,α)∈Aut(T)^k. In the standard induced wreath coordinates, P's arrows have local automorphisms in Aut(T). Since Out(T)≅C₂² is abelian, conjugation by α changes every local arrow only by an inner automorphism. Consequently b₀ normalizes G and [b₀,P]≤N, so it acts trivially on G/N. It is outside G: its factor permutation is the identity, P acts faithfully on the factors, and α is outer. The [PSL₂ manuscript](tw-psl2-factorwise-inversion.md) gives the field-class and outer-group source inputs. This establishes the displayed group, embedding and marked involution, not the existence of a loop section.

## Full conjugacy class and inverted-element generation

The local S₆ is the field extension T⟨α⟩, so it preserves the particular T-class C=α^T. Indeed, an odd involution in S₆ is either a transposition or a product of three disjoint transpositions. Both have centralizer of order 48 in S₆, with an odd centralizer element, so their T-centralizer has order 24. The cited PSL₂(9) local calculations show that a diagonal outer involution has T-centralizer 8 or 10, and that the diagonal-field coset has no involutions. Thus this S₆ outer coset is the field coset. Either field class generates the same index-two extension with T; no choice of a particular transposition as α is assumed. Every local P-arrow lies in this S₆ and preserves C. Since N acts transitively on C^k,

    b₀^G=b₀^N=C^k.                                            (1)

Let K_full={g∈G:(b₀g)²=1}, and let L=⟨K_full⟩. First N≤L: for n∈N, b₀^n is an involution in b₀N, so b₀b₀^n∈K_full. Its coordinatewise entries generate [T,α]=T, because that subgroup is nontrivial and normal in the nonabelian simple group T.

Next every involution h∈P has a lift in K_full. Its factor permutation has fixed points and transposed pairs. At a fixed factor, the local arrow d of h satisfies d²=1 and belongs to S₆=T⟨α⟩. The outer coset of αd is therefore either the field coset, containing α, or the inner coset, containing 1; choose an involution or identity in that coset. On each transposed pair choose any arrow in the prescribed outer coset and take its inverse on the reverse arrow. These reverse outer cosets agree because h²=1, the outer group is abelian and α²=1. The resulting element of b₀Nh is an involution. Thus the image of K_full in P contains every involution. The group P=S₇ wr S_b is generated by involutions: transpositions in the S₇ base factors and pure block transpositions generate it. Therefore L contains N and maps onto P, giving L=G. This verifies the full inverted-element generation condition for the group, but it does not select a subset with the transversal, collision and conjugacy properties of Λ.

## Exact capacities and a first collision filter

Let C=α^T. The reviewed local calculation gives two T-classes of field involutions, each of size a=3(3²+1)/2=15. For every perfect matching of the b seven-point blocks, take the pure block-permutation involution h∈P interchanging the matched blocks without changing within-block labels. It swaps all k factors in k/2=7b/2 pairs. In b₀Nh, involutivity leaves exactly t choices for one arrow in each pair and forces the other; thus the fibre contains t^(7b/2) involutions. The (b−1)!! perfect matchings give disjoint fibres, of combined size

    (b−1)!! t^(7b/2).

Since (b−1)!!≥(b/2)^(b/4), this exceeds the required |N|=t^(7b) when b/2>t^14. Thus neither the reviewed upper bound i₂(P)M^k nor the stronger sum of exact **individual** involution-fibre capacities yields a contradiction from |Λ|=|N| alone in this group. These fibres have not been assembled into Λ and this comparison says nothing by itself about collisions between choices.

There is a decisive first collision filter. Choose induced coordinates consistently across the seven-point blocks. Since θ is trivial on the block-permutation factor S_{b−1}, each pure block permutation has identity local arrows, so [b₀,h]=1. Suppose Λ⊂b₀G contains b₀, is N-conjugacy invariant, consists of involutions and satisfies P∩Λ²={1}. For λ∈Λ∩b₀Nh with h a perfect block matching, independently conjugate on the two coordinates of each transposition to obtain λ'=ch, where c∈C^k=b₀^N and [c,h]=1. Then c,λ'∈Λ, so h=cλ'∈P∩Λ², a contradiction. Thus all perfect-matching fibres used above are forbidden in a genuine marked section.

For even b≥4, consider instead nonidentity pure block involutions with exactly two fixed blocks. There are binom(b,2)(b−3)!! of them. Each has 7(b−2)/2 transposed factor pairs and 14 fixed factors. At a fixed factor, b₀Nh contains the two T-classes of field involutions, each of size a. If every fixed-site arrow lies in the particular class C=b₀^T, the same inner-conjugation argument produces c∈b₀^N and a commuting h, so the element is forbidden by the collision condition. By (1), after allowing N-conjugation of λ, this all-C pattern is exactly the pattern excluded by collision with the marked G-class b₀^G: the preceding argument excludes it, whereas an opposite-class fixed arrow remains outside C under N-conjugation and cannot equal the corresponding arrow of any c∈b₀^G. Such equality would be necessary for cλ∈P because P acts faithfully on the factors. After removing **only** this marked-class collision pattern, the exact number of choices left in each fibre is

    t^[7(b−2)/2] ((2a)^14−a^14).

The double-factorial number of labels grows faster than any fixed-base exponential in b, so the sum of these remaining local counts eventually exceeds t^(7b). This is a calculation after one specifically stated collision filter, not a lower bound for the size of a valid Λ. Cross-label collisions, P-conjugacy and generation of an actual selected section can eliminate further choices. The subsequent [cross-label theorem](tw-cross-label-block-exclusion.md), independently accepted by three reviewers at its recorded source revision, uses those correlations to exclude the entire stated control family and a broader coherent alternating-socle family. The capacity comparison here remains valid and explains why the additional argument is necessary; neither result resolves general TW actions.
