## Conditional theorem and interface

**Conditional theorem.** Assume CFSG, the source facts and all family-specific results listed below, and the following SD folder/inversion interface. Let Q be a finite simple nonassociative left Bol loop and let G=LMlt(Q) act primitively on Q, with SD_k socle B=T^k, k≥3, where T is nonabelian finite simple. Let H=G_1, with B∩H=Δ(T), and K={L_x:x∈Q}. Assume τ∈Aut(G), τ(B)=B, τ(k)=k⁻¹ for every k∈K, and the folder identities G=⟨K⟩, 1∈K, kKk⊆K; put G̃=G⋊⟨t⟩, t²=1, Λ=tK. Assume |Λ|=[G:H]=|T|^(k−1), Λ²∩H={1}, and the cited cyclic semiregularity/forbidden-prime consequence: every element of Λ² has order dividing |T|^(k−1). Then no such Q exists. This conditional assembly has scoped independent review and excludes no other O’Nan–Scott type or SD₂.

The common-class arguments additionally require, for the particular T to which they are applied, an Aut(T)-invariant T-class J of involutions with J⊆(α^T)² for every nonidentity involution α∈Aut(T); one involution class is a sufficient route only via the separately cited square lemma. Raw-F3 arguments instead need n=|T|≥2²⁰, a=|Aut(T)|, b=#{α∈Aut(T):α²=1}, and b³+3ab<n², or a verified upper bound B≥b satisfying B³+3aB<n². They do not require J. Some small cases use their own finite scalar criteria rather than the large-socle or raw-F3 thresholds. All stated numerical screens are sufficient, not necessary.

## Factor action and injectivity

P is the image of G (not G̃) on the k simple factors. It is primitive: B is transitive, G=BH, and any P-invariant factor partition produces an H-normalized subgroup properly between Δ(T) and B, contradicting primitivity. τ preserves B because B is characteristic in G; the image of Λ under the factor action of G̃ lies in a single coset of P, not necessarily P itself. P may be A_k or S_k and the proof uses separate alternating-top and nonalternating-top arguments. To count elements of Λ by their automorphisms of B one needs C_G(B)=1: a nontrivial centralizer is transitive by primitivity and semiregular because B is transitive, hence regular, but then its transitive centralizer B would be regular, contradicting B∩H=Δ(T)≠1. Thus conjugation on B is injective on tG, hence on Λ. Cyclic semiregularity is needed to exclude top r-cycles where r∤|T|; it does not follow from Λ²∩H={1} alone without conjugacy- and power-closure. See [the A₅ all-factor manuscript](sd-a5-all-factors.md), sections “Folder setting and semiregularity,” “General counting lemma,” and “Extension to arbitrary primitive top group.”
