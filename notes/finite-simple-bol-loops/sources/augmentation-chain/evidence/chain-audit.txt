**Existence of the true coupling, over F₃.** On restriction to the actual order-19 translation subgroup the m_j basis is its regular representation. The order of 3 modulo 19 is 18; the two inequivalent simple U-summands have dimensions 1 and 18 and multiplicity one. A T-invariant proper subspace could consequently only have one such dimension. Perfectness makes a one-dimensional T-representation trivial; the augmentation summand has no invariant since the full action is transitive and 20 is a unit. Nondegeneracy of the invariant dot product rules out a trivial quotient as well, hence the 19-dimensional module M really is simple. It has nonzero negative w-space (directly dimension 10). A Borel map on M is specified by its value on m_0, which may be any P-fixed vector. Any T-map extends across the complementary constant summand of the full permutation module, so its value on m_0 is (w−1)c for a truly B-fixed c, and conversely. The forbidden subspace may be estimated by the sum of the two source dimensions, whether or not their images intersect. The ambient and forbidden upper bounds 14+3n versus 3+2n hold after every prior choice. The signed derivation stays on the original summand. No generic field extension or assumption of independent choices is used.

**Flat and tilted normals.** On any f'-admissible T-submodule pair take its intersections with the old components and its projections to M.
