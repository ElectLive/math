# Projective principal-module collision control and exclusion

**Mira Cult**

**Scoped independent mathematical and finite-code review recorded below.** This is a continuation of [C0010](../../claims/0010.md). For the elementary-radical S5-envelope interface, the projective principal indecomposable is not excluded by the two fiber-dimension inequalities, corefreeness and all F20-conjugates of the transposition collision condition alone. The following finite module control establishes that limited claim; it is not an envelope or loop construction.

Use Nagy arXiv:0709.4544v1, Lemma 3.3 for the six-point action and Lemma 4.1 for M,N, with the matrices transcribed in [verify_bruck_s5_dimension_nine.py](../code/verify_bruck_s5_dimension_nine.py) at source [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/d9bc55095490d42e41c359bbb88b67c78f6d4998), and form V=M⊗N. The self-contained [finite verifier](../code/verify_bruck_s5_projective_control.py) uses the same explicit generator model. It enumerates the 120 paired permutations, chooses P=<p> with p=(0 1 2 3 4), q=(0 1 3 2)(4), and works over F2. The 16-dimensional action has Hom_S5(1,V)=0, Hom_S5(M,V)=1 and Hom_S5(N,V)=0, computed by fixed-point ranks in V, V⊗M, V⊗N (M and N self-dual). Their invariant nondegenerate forms come from the usual dot product on the even-weight six-point module modulo constants and on the even-weight five-point module, respectively. On a Sylow D8 generated by q and (0 3), fixed space has dimension 2. The group algebra of D8 is local and self-injective, so V|D8 embeds in the injective hull (F2D8)^2; equal dimension 16 makes this an isomorphism. Hence V is projective over S5 by induction from a Sylow subgroup and odd-index averaging. Its unique simple socle is M; a projective-injective module with simple socle M is its injective/projective cover P(M), consistently with the reviewed dimension-16 result.

Let F=<p,q>. The P-fixed part V^P has dimension 4 and is free over F/P≅C4, and the P-nonfixed part is S³, S the projective four-dimensional simple F-module. For a hypothetical W of dimension 7 the P-idempotent and semisimplicity of the P-nonfixed part force W^P to be the unique dimension-three q-invariant subspace (1+q)V^P and [P,W] to be one of seven S-lines. The test constructs all seven directly. The transposition t=(0 1) has rank 8 on V; consequently the two standard fiber inequalities force w=7 exactly. For the seven W, intersection dimensions with (1+t)V are 0,0,2,0,2,3,0 (in the script's stable basis ordering). The unique S5-stable S-line, the socle, is the line with intersection dimension 3. The four zero-intersection spaces contain no nonzero S5-submodule: every such submodule meets the unique simple socle, which is not contained in them. F is transitive on the ten transpositions, so each of these W also avoids the image of 1+t' for every F-conjugate t'.

The control does not impose existence of a conjugacy-invariant transversal K, its generation property, or loop simplicity; it does not prove or suggest an actual loop with radical P(M). It only disproves an attempted uniform exclusion using the specified local conditions. The finite verifier has the scoped independent review below.

## Uniform counterfamily

The same script finds the sum B of S-lines 0 and 1, an eight-dimensional F-submodule, disjoint from both (1+t)V and the simple S5-socle. Let A be any of the four seven-dimensional zero-collision spaces. For every k≥1 take V_k=P(M)^k and W_k=A⊕B^(k−1). Both W_k∩(1+t)V_k and W_k∩soc(V_k) vanish; the latter makes W_k corefree because every nonzero S5-submodule meets the socle. Since F is transitive on transpositions, the no-collision condition holds for every transposition. Moreover dim W_k=8k−1, dim V_k=16k and rank(1+t)=8k, so both fiber inequalities are equalities. Thus these local necessary conditions do not bound the multiplicity of the principal projective summand. This remains solely a module countercontrol; it does not exhibit K or any loop.

## Exclusion of the displayed family

Assume the faithful simple exponent-two Bruck-envelope hypotheses and the recorded fiber, folder and block inputs in [the radical analysis](bruck-s5-radical.md) and [the uniform support manuscript](bruck-s5-uniform-principal-support.md). For the particular W_k just constructed, there is in fact no such envelope. Projectivity gives H²(S5,V_k)=0, so G=V_k⋊S5. Fiber saturation says that K contains every involution over each transposition: the number required in its fiber is 2^(16k−(8k−1)−1)=2^(8k), equal to the number of involution lifts because rank(1+t)=8k.

Normalize a Sylow-five subgroup P of H to the standard P by conjugation in V_k. Since P is normal in F=π(H), H=W_k N_H(P), and N_{V_k}(P)=V_k^P. Let q generate F/P and take vq in N_H(P), with v in V_k^P. In the A-coordinate, (1+q)V^P=A^P. In each B-coordinate, B^P=0 and V^P is free over F2[C4]. As (vq)^4 belongs to W_k^P, the norm of the B-coordinate of v is zero; in a free C4-module the norm kernel is (1+q)V^P. Conjugating by an element of V_k^P kills all B-coordinates. Then (vq)^2 differs from q² by an element of A^P≤W_k, so the pure double transposition q² belongs to H.

Write q²=tu for disjoint transpositions. Fiber saturation places the pure lifts t,u in K, so q²=tu belongs to H∩K², contrary to the folder transversal condition. This excludes only the displayed W_k and the exact module V_k=P(M)^k; it does not classify other W, extensions with additional N-blocks, or arbitrary principal modules.
