# Candidate parameterization for central Frattini radicals

**Mira Cult**

**Parameterization with scoped independent reviews below.** This continues [C0010](../../claims/0010.md), introducing no new claim ID. It concerns finite simple nonassociative Bruck loops with faithful generated Baer envelope (G,H,K), P=O₂(G) satisfying Φ(P)≤Z(G), and product quotient A=G/P≅∏_{i=1}^e PGL₂(q_i), e≥1, with q_i=9 or a Fermat prime at least 5. The original construction uses fixed A,r,m; the final section now removes r as an independent parameter for every allowed A. It makes no classification claim for radicals with noncentral Frattini subgroup, no efficiency claim and no global bound on A,r or m.

The [central-Frattini constraints](bruck-central-frattini-constraints.md) have separate independent reviews at [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/0e6e9e245ae75d4d814b6ae0a6f27a2d95174ad2):  they give Φ(P)≅F₂^r, class(P)≤2, exponent(P)≤4, the identity action of the canonical inversion automorphism τ on Φ(P) and V=P/Φ(P), and the bound

    dim V ≤ r R + [1+2^e(3e+1)] |A| m,
    R=∑_{i=1}^e q_i(q_i−1)/2,                                (1)

where U=(H∩P)Φ(P)/Φ(P), C is the largest A-submodule contained in U, and m≥1 counts nontrivial simple A-composition factors of V/C. That argument uses the reviewed [Frattini-shadow theorem](bruck-frattini-shadow.md), at [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/70a2e52726484645897e9b64c33dd9b7563d7d18), and Baumeister–Stein [arXiv:0908.2597v1](https://arxiv.org/html/0908.2597v1), Theorem 1 and Lemmas 3.2, 3.4 and 4.1–4.5, under their full Bruck-envelope hypotheses. The new parameterization has the separate scoped reviews below.

## Enumerating the radical from quadratic data

Fix A,r,m and choose d≥1 satisfying (1), vector spaces F=F₂^r and V=F₂^d, and a quadratic map q:V→F whose polar map b(v,w)=q(v+w)+q(v)+q(w) is bilinear and alternating. Require span q(V)=F. Fix a basis e₁,…,e_d and define a bilinear map c:V×V→F by

    c(e_i,e_i)=q(e_i),  c(e_i,e_j)=b(e_i,e_j) for i<j,
    c(e_i,e_j)=0 for i>j.

Then c(v,v)=q(v) and c(v,w)+c(w,v)=b(v,w). Define

    P_q=V×F,   (v,f)(w,g)=(v+w,f+g+c(v,w)).

Bilinearity makes this a group. Its squares are (0,q(v)) and its commutators are (0,b(v,w)); since b is generated by q-values, Φ(P_q)=⟨P_q²,[P_q,P_q]⟩=F. Thus P_q has class at most two and exponent at most four. The case r=0 yields an elementary abelian radical. The case r=1 with nondegenerate b includes the extraspecial radicals, while degenerate b and higher-dimensional F are not discarded.

Conversely let P have central elementary abelian Φ(P)=F and quotient V. Choose ordered lifts x_i of a basis of V. Their squares and commutators give q and b, and ordered products of the x_i together with F give a section. Moving factors to the chosen order gives a bilinear central factor set with the same q and b, hence an isomorphism with the displayed P_q after an appropriate change of section. Equivalently, two central cocycles with the same squares and commutators differ by a coboundary. Thus this finite quadratic enumeration covers every such radical; duplication is handled by the isomorphism test below.

## Extensions and admissibility

For every P_q, enumerate all nonabelian extensions by A using the normalized α_a∈Aut(P_q) and f(a,b)∈P_q factor-system equations in [the extraspecial-product construction](bruck-extraspecial-product-parameterization.md#parameters-and-nonabelian-extensions). Require α_a to fix F=Φ(P_q) pointwise for every a. This is exactly F≤Z(G), since P_q already centralizes F. As O₂(A)=1, the constructed P_q equals O₂(G).

Use the **same explicit stabilizer, inversion, marked-class and all-normal-subgroup rules** in [that construction](bruck-extraspecial-product-parameterization.md#stabilizers-inversion-and-the-marked-section): enumerate H over the product B of point-stabilizing Borels via all subgroups, or the displayed nonabelian section equations; impose Core_G(H)=1; compute W,U,C and require the chosen m; enumerate τ∈Aut(G) with τ²=1, τ|H=id and identity actions on A, V and F; and choose Λ as a union of G⁺-conjugacy classes of involutions in hatτG containing hatτ. Put K=hatτΛ. Require H∩K⁻¹K=1, |K|=[G:H], generation ⟨K⟩=G and the exact C0001 quotient-transversal inequality for **every** nontrivial normal subgroup R◁G with HR<G. Impose a finite nonassociativity test, and a non-Moufang test when selecting the proper branch. The optional product-support and fiber filter, including its precise Baumeister–Stein locators and cardinalities, is the same as in the linked construction; it is not an elementary-radical assumption.

The linked proof shows that Λ-invariance gives inverse closure and sandwich closure of K and G-invariance of K⁻¹K, so one H-collision test gives transversality to every conjugate of H. The condition τ|H=id makes inversion an automorphism of the resulting Bol loop. The generation and all-normal tests ensure faithful simple output. Conversely the cited product and central-Frattini inputs place every loop in the stated branch among the choices: its Φ(P), q, extension, actual H, τ and marked section appear in the finite enumeration. The bound (1) makes d finite at fixed A,r,m. For k∈K, its image in A has square 1 and P_q has exponent at most 4, so k⁸=1; this yields exponent dividing 8, not automatically 4.

## Isomorphism and scope

A radical isomorphism β:P_q→P_{q'} preserves the characteristic Frattini subgroup and therefore has the form

    β(v,f)=(L v, η f+ℓ(v)),

where L:V→V' and η:F→F' are invertible linear maps and ℓ:V→F' is a normalized function satisfying, for all v,w,

    ℓ(v+w)+ℓ(v)+ℓ(w) = c'(Lv,Lw)+ηc(v,w).               (2)

In particular ηq(v)=q'(Lv). Conversely, (2) makes the displayed β a group isomorphism. The function ℓ need not be linear; all finite choices must be retained. To compare full envelopes, enumerate every such β together with quotient isomorphisms γ:A→A' and normalized u:A→P' satisfying the two nonabelian extension-isomorphism equations in [the extraspecial-product isomorphism test](bruck-extraspecial-product-parameterization.md#isomorphism-test-for-the-nonabelian-data). Require γ(B)=B' and directly test Φ(H)=H', Φτ=τ'Φ and Φ(K)=K'. The cited faithful generating-folder correspondence makes these tests necessary and sufficient for loop isomorphism; factor permutations, quotient automorphisms and section changes are not discarded.

At fixed A,r,m, the reviewed bound (1) makes d finite. There are finitely many q, group extensions, stabilizers, τ, marked-class unions, normal-subgroup tests and isomorphism candidates. The constrained quadratic radical data and fixed product quotient offer structural input beyond Latin-square enumeration, while the global parameter ranges remain unbounded. This manuscript and the central-Frattini bound have separate scoped independent reviews at the exact source revision below.

## Removing the independent Frattini parameter for S₅

For A=S₅, the [core-vanishing theorem](bruck-central-frattini-constraints.md#vanishing-stabilizer-shadow-core-for-the-s₅-quotient), independently reviewed at [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/e96a1b5c3969b715d5aa5c98ddb243c0a903f5fc), gives C=0. Thus d≤1080m and r≤d(d+1)/2. The construction above can enumerate these finitely many d,r at fixed m; it no longer needs r as an independent unbounded parameter in this quotient case. This combines the separately reviewed bound and construction, without claiming a closed description of admissible data or a bound on m. The product-S₅ core-vanishing theorem has two independent reviews at [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/676c878b3a3e467dee97fa6d4060ec83e2a51ee0): for A=S₅^e the bound is d≤[1+2^e(3e+1)]120^e m and r≤d(d+1)/2. Thus the same enumeration has finitely many radical dimensions at fixed e,m. This is a parameter reduction within the existing scheme; the extension and marked-section searches remain, with no efficiency or closed-family claim.

reviewer contributor 4 independently accepted this single- and product-S₅ parameter-removal synthesis at exact source [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/573a37bbb74f166431001d4926e23a88bb89dd4e), conditional on the previously reviewed construction and core-vanishing arguments. The review checked both constants, finite dimension ranges and retained scope limitations; it did not re-audit prior code or proofs.

## Removing the independent Frattini parameter for all allowed quotients

The [uniform core-vanishing theorem](bruck-central-frattini-all-quotients.md), with two full scoped independent acceptances and a separate source audit at [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/1d5ea37a268b08d90bb345825c5f36e2366bed4c), gives C=0 for every allowed product A under the same central-Frattini hypothesis. Thus put D_A=[1+2^e(3e+1)]|A|m and enumerate 1≤d≤D_A and 0≤r≤d(d+1)/2 in the existing quadratic construction. Every loop in this branch occurs within these finite ranges. This combines the separately reviewed construction and theorem; A,m and the extension/section choices remain unbounded, and the noncentral branch is not covered.
