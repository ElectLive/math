# Candidate exclusion of the V6* principal block for arbitrary N-block

**Scoped independent mathematical and computational review recorded below.** Assume a faithful Baer envelope of a finite simple exponent-two Bruck loop, elementary abelian V=O₂(G), G/V≅S₅, and an S₅-module isomorphism V=X⊕Y, with X=V₆* the dual of the six-dimensional principal module in [bruck-s5-radical.md](bruck-s5-radical.md) and Y=N^a⊕U^b for arbitrary a,b≥0. This does not require quotienting by Y. Under the [block decomposition and H²(Y)=0](bruck-s5-module-blocks.md), reviewed by an independent check at the recorded source revision, the following finite computation and argument propose to exclude this case. Identification of principal class 1 with V₆* has the separate scoped review described below.

The module model is explicit: on six projective points put $`c=(12)(34)(56)`$, $`d=(2345)`$; represent $`J=\mathbb F_2^6/\langle(1,1,1,1,1,1)\rangle`$ in the first five coordinates, and define the six-dimensional primal generator matrices by appending the columns $`32\mathbin{\mathrm{xor}}21`$ and $`32\mathbin{\mathrm{xor}}1`$, respectively. The module $`X`$ has the inverse-transpose generator matrices; [the existing principal-H¹ verifier](../code/verify_s5_principal_h1_bounds.py) uses the same model. Vectors below are bit masks in this dual basis. The certificate checks a Sylow-5 subgroup and its normalizing order-4 subgroup, finds the P-fixed space $`\{0,31,32,63\}`$, verifies that 32 is fixed by all 120 group elements, and that the normalizer generator has image $`(1+q)X^P=\{0,32\}`$. Thus $`X^P`$ is the two-dimensional regular sign module, with globally fixed line $`\langle32\rangle`$. These checks are independent of the class-1 identification.

Let P=C₅≤F=F₂₀=π(H), W=H∩V, and T=⟨t⟩ for a transposition. The P-fixed subspace of X is the 2-dimensional regular module for F/P acting through sign; its unique nonzero fixed line is S₅-fixed. Since Y^P=0, any nonzero F-invariant W^P meets that S₅-fixed line, contradicting core-freeness. Hence W^P=0. All F-composition factors of W are the projective 4-dimensional simple, so H²(F,W)=0. Existence of H makes the restricted group-extension class vanish on F.

The adapted stable-elements script [verify_bruck_s5_v6dual_h2.py](../code/verify_bruck_s5_v6dual_h2.py) is copied from [verify_s5_v6_h2.py](../code/verify_s5_v6_h2.py), reviewed at exact source the recorded source revision. It changes the generator matrices to the inverse-transpose action, adjusts the H¹ output, and adds the H¹ restriction, fixed-line and transposition fixed/image controls. This adapted calculation and its consequences have the scoped review recorded below. In a direct Python 3.12 run (2.20 seconds wall time on this workspace), it reports:

- H²(D₈,X) dimension 4; the stable subspace dimension 1.
- The nonzero stable class restricts nontrivially to a cyclic C₄≤F and trivially to T. It corestricts and verifies a global 2-cocycle on all group triples.
- H¹(S₅,X) dimension 1 (64 derivations, 32 principal derivations). The nontrivial class restricts nontrivially to H¹(F,X): the P-fixed elements are [0,31,32,63], the C₄ coboundaries in that space are [0,32], and the projected cocycle value is 31.
- On X, |C_X(t)|=|(1+t)X|=8, so H¹(T,X)=0.

Assuming the stable-elements theorem and independent verification of the adapted calculation, H²(S₅,X)→H²(F,X) is injective. H²(S₅,Y)=0, so the extension G splits. This conclusion is obtained from the stabilizer restriction, not assumed.

The P-idempotent gives V/W≅X^P⊕Q as F-modules, with Q projective. Thus H¹(F,V/W)=H¹(F,X^P) has dimension 1: X^P is the 2-dimensional sign-regular module, and for a C₄-generator q, the norm vanishes and (1+q) has rank 1. The global cocycle above represents its nonzero class. Global cocycle automorphisms of the split group and conjugation by V therefore put H into standard form W⋊F.

For a transposition, H¹(T,X)=H¹(T,U)=0, and H¹(T,N) is the 2-dimensional natural module of C_{S₅}(t)/T≅S₃. Hence H¹(T,V)=(F₂²)^a with diagonal S₃ action. The zero class is the unique S₃-fixed point; all other orbits have size 3 or 6. The conjugation-invariant t-fiber of K is a union of V-orbits indexed by H¹(T,V) and closed under S₃. As in Baumeister–Stein [arXiv:0908.2597v1](https://arxiv.org/html/0908.2597v1) Theorem 1(b), Lemmas 4.1 and 4.5, its number of V-orbits is a positive power of 2. Modulo 3, either no such union exists or the zero class is selected. In the latter case t∈K, and conjugation gives every standard transposition. Two disjoint standard transpositions t,u have tu∈F≤H, contradicting the transversal property. Thus this branch is excluded, conditional on the cited finite calculation and its independent review.
