# Extraspecial radical constraints for product PGL₂ quotients

**Argument with scoped independent reviews below.** Let X be a finite simple nonassociative right Bruck loop with faithful Baer envelope (G,H,K), extraspecial P=O₂(G), and A=G/P≅∏ᵢPGL₂(qᵢ), where e≥1 and each qᵢ is 9 or a Fermat prime at least 5. The loop is not assumed to have exponent 2. Put Z=Z(P)=Φ(P)=⟨z⟩, V=P/Z, W=P∩H and U=WZ/Z. The extraspecial square form q on V has nondegenerate polar form b. This continues [C0010](../../claims/0010.md), without a new ID or classification claim.

The [Frattini-shadow manuscript](bruck-frattini-shadow.md#invariant-functional-detection-without-a-quotient-folder), independently reviewed at the recorded source revision, supplies the canonical inversion automorphism τ fixing V, and the injection


```math
\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}\nolimits_A(V,\mathbf1)\longrightarrow\mathop{\mathrm{Hom}}\nolimits_B(U,\mathbf1),\tag{1}
```


where B=HP/P is the product of the point-stabilizing Borels. Its [core-reduced module bound](bruck-frattini-shadow.md#core-reduced-module-and-all-support-shadow-bounds), independently reviewed at the recorded source revision, applies without assuming that the Frattini group quotient is a loop folder. Baumeister–Stein [arXiv:0908.2597v1](https://arxiv.org/html/0908.2597v1), Theorem 1 and Lemmas 3.2(1)–(2), 3.4 and 4.1–4.5, give the quotient, inversion, marked-section conjugacy invariance, and joint-support fiber data used below. Those earlier reviews cover the stated inputs; the new deduction has the separate reviews below.

Since Z is characteristic of P and has order 2, it is central in G; corefreeness gives W∩Z=1. Hence W is elementary abelian, U is totally singular, and WZ is the elementary abelian preimage of U in P. Let C be the largest A-submodule contained in U, namely the sum of all such submodules. If x∈C^A, then the nonzero functional b(x,−), when x≠0, is A-invariant and vanishes on U because U is isotropic. The injection (1) forces b(x,−)=0; nondegeneracy gives x=0. Therefore


```math
C^A=0.\tag{2}
```


## Marked involutions bound the stabilizer core

Use the formal semidirect product $`G^+=G\rtimes\langle\hat\tau\rangle`$, with an external involution even if τ=1, and put Λ=τ̂K. Every element of Λ is an involution and Λ is G⁺-conjugacy-invariant by Baumeister–Stein Lemma 3.2(2). Let $`E_C`$ be the preimage of C in P and $`W_C=W\cap E_C`$. Since C⊆U and W∩Z=1, E_C=W_CZ is elementary abelian and W_C has codimension one in E_C. The submodule C is A-invariant, so E_C is normal in G. It is also τ-stable, because τ acts trivially on V.

For λ=τ̂k∈Λ, let t=kP∈A and let α be conjugation by λ on E_C. If im(1+α) met W_C nontrivially, choose x∈E_C with λ'=xλx⁻¹=wλ for 1≠w∈W_C. Both λ and λ' lie in Λ. Since τ fixes H pointwise, wτ̂=τ̂w, so their K-coordinates lie in the same H-coset and are distinct, contrary to the folder transversal property. Thus im(1+α)∩W_C=0. As W_C has codimension one, rank(1+α|E_C)≤1. Its projection onto C is 1+t, and therefore


```math
\mathop{\mathrm{rank}}\nolimits(1+t\mid C)\le1\qquad(k\in K).\tag{3}
```


This uses marked-section invariance, not G-conjugacy invariance of K, and applies also when X has exponent 4 or 8. In particular, Baumeister–Stein Lemmas 4.4–4.5 ensure that every singleton-coordinate outer-involution class occurs in the image of K.

For each factor PGL₂(qᵢ), let Tᵢ be its class of outer involutions, embedded with identity in the other factors. It has size qᵢ(qᵢ−1)/2 by Lemma 4.4 and generates that factor: the subgroup generated by this nonempty conjugacy class is normal, contains an outer element, and has a nontrivial commutator with the simple derived subgroup (whose centralizer in PGL₂(qᵢ) is trivial). Simplicity then puts the derived subgroup inside it, so it is the whole factor. Thus the union of the Tᵢ generates A. Define


```math
R=\sum_{i=1}^{e}\frac{q_i(q_i-1)}2.
```


By (3), every map 1+t on C for t in this union has image dimension at most 1. The simultaneous map


```math
C\longrightarrow\bigoplus_{t\in\cup_i T_i}(1+t)C,\qquad x\longmapsto((1+t)x)_t
```


has kernel C^A=0 by (2). Consequently


```math
\dim C\le R.\tag{4}
```


## A dimension bound at fixed quotient

Let m be the number, counted with multiplicity, of nontrivial simple A-composition factors of V/C. The reviewed core-reduced module bound gives m≥1 and


```math
\dim(V/C)\le[1+2^e(3e+1)]\,|A|\,m.
```


Together with (4),


```math
\dim V\le R+[1+2^e(3e+1)]\,|A|\,m.\tag{5}
```


Thus the Frattini rank is bounded for a fixed quotient A and fixed m. This does not bound the number or orders of quotient factors, m itself, or radicals that are not extraspecial. Since the image of every k∈K in A is an involution or identity, k²∈P. An extraspecial 2-group has exponent at most 4, so k⁸=1 and X has exponent dividing 8. No loop construction, classification, elementary-radical theorem, or general bound on all simple Bruck loops is asserted.
