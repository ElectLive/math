# Candidate exclusion of the J* principal block for arbitrary N-block

**Scoped independent mathematical review recorded below.** Work under the faithful Baer-envelope hypotheses in [bruck-s5-radical.md](bruck-s5-radical.md): the loop is finite, simple, exponent-two right Bruck; V=O₂(G) is elementary abelian and G/V≅S₅. Assume the reviewed module-block separation, N-block classification and H² inputs recorded in [bruck-s5-module-blocks.md](bruck-s5-module-blocks.md) and the mixed dimension-nine section of [bruck-s5-radical.md](bruck-s5-radical.md). If V as S₅-module is J*⊕Y, where J* is the even-weight six-point permutation module for PGL₂(5)≅S₅ and Y=N^a⊕U^b, then no such envelope exists. This covers arbitrary nonnegative a,b, with U=N⊗F₂C₂, and does not require the new P-fixed stabilizer feasibility computation.

## Normalization

Let π:G→S₅, F=π(H)=F₂₀=N_{S₅}(P), where P is a Sylow 5-subgroup. Put W=H∩V. In J*, the 5-cycle P has a one-dimensional fixed space spanned by the all-ones vector z, and z is S₅-fixed. Y has no P-fixed vectors. Thus C_V(P)=⟨z⟩. Since W is F-invariant and core-free, W∩⟨z⟩=0, so W^P=0. Consequently all F-composition factors of W are the projective irreducible 4-dimensional module, and W itself is projective. The same P-idempotent decomposition shows V/W≅⟨z+W⟩⊕Q as F-module, where Q is projective.

The extension class e∈H²(S₅,V) restricts to zero on F: the F-preimage of the extension contains H and the restricted class is the image of the class of 1→W→H→F→1, which is zero because W is projective over the finite group algebra and hence injective, so H²(F,W)=0. It also restricts to zero on T=⟨t⟩ for a transposition t because K contains an involution over t. Reviewed inputs give H²(S₅,Y)=0 and joint injectivity of H²(S₅,J*)→H²(F,J*)⊕H²(T,J*) (indeed the cited certificate uses C₄≤F). Hence e=0 and G=V⋊S₅.

After selecting this splitting, H is determined by W and a cocycle F→V/W. H¹(F,V/W)≅H¹(F,⟨z+W⟩)≅F₂. Its nonzero class is the restriction of the global cocycle g↦sign(g)z: this is nonzero since F→C₂ is onto and z+W is a trivial direct summand. Conjugation by V removes coboundaries, and the global cocycle induces an automorphism of G fixing V and the S₅ quotient. Therefore, after a group automorphism, H=W⋊F. This normalization preserves the existence of K and the folder axioms. No quotient loop is used.

## Transposition fibers and the mod-3 count

For t a transposition, S₅ acts on the six projective points with cycle structure 2³. The fixed space of t on J* has dimension 3 and (1+t)J* has dimension 2. The vector z is fixed but does not belong to (1+t)J*: a preimage would have one point from each transposition pair, hence odd weight. Thus H¹(T,J*)=C_{J*}(t)/(1+t)J* is the 1-dimensional space generated by z. Its C_{S₅}(t)/T≅S₃ action is trivial, since z is globally fixed.

On each N, H¹(T,N) is the natural 2-dimensional S₃ module: with t=(12) in the five-point action, C_N(t)/(1+t)N identifies with the even-weight triples on the remaining three points. On each U, T interchanges two 4-dimensional summands, so H¹(T,U)=0. Therefore

H¹(T,V) ≅ F₂[z] ⊕ (F₂²)^a,

with S₃ acting trivially on the first factor and diagonally naturally on the rest. In particular the S₃-fixed points are exactly (0,0) and (z,0). Every other S₃-orbit has size 3 or 6: a nonzero tuple in (F₂²)^a spans a line or the full plane, whose stabilizer has order 2 or 1 respectively.

Let d=dim V and r=rank(1+t). The section K is G-invariant, and its fiber K∩tV is a union of V-conjugacy orbits of involution lifts. Such V-orbits are indexed by H¹(T,V), and all have size 2^r; the centralizer C_{S₅}(t) acts on the indexing space as above. The image of K lies over 1 and the ten transpositions, while F contains no transpositions. As K is a transversal, |K∩V|=2^(d−dim W), and each of the ten transposition fibers has size 2^(d−dim W−1). Thus K selects q=2^(d−dim W−1−r) such V-orbits. This is a positive integer. Modulo 3, q is 1 or 2, so the selected union of S₃-orbits contains at least one of the two fixed points.

If it contains (0,0), the standard lift t belongs to K. If it contains (z,0), zt belongs to K. In either case, S₅-conjugacy puts the same kind of lift over every transposition into K, because z is S₅-fixed. Choose disjoint transpositions t,u with b=tu∈F, as exists in F₂₀. Their chosen lifts satisfy t·u=b or (zt)(zu)=z²tu=b. Since b∈F≤H, distinct elements of K represent the same H-coset, contradicting transversality. This excludes the J* block.
