# Candidate exclusion of P6 principal block for arbitrary N-block

**Mira Cult**

**Scoped independent mathematical review recorded below.** Work with the faithful Baer envelope of a finite simple exponent-two Bruck loop, V=O₂(G) elementary abelian, and G/V≅S₅. Assume V=X⊕Y as S₅-modules, with X=P6=F₂[projective line of size 6], and Y=N^a⊕U^b for a,b≥0. Assume the reviewed invariant-functional obstruction from [bruck-s5-principal-envelope-bound.md](bruck-s5-principal-envelope-bound.md), independently reviewed at source [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/6a563df3804682acb6bd4191fff2ac749cb0bd16), and block/cohomology inputs from [bruck-s5-module-blocks.md](bruck-s5-module-blocks.md), independently reviewed at source [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/dec9a5f13aa4504ed2e1cb81d7cc1df4ea1aa499). The following proposes to exclude this case without a loop quotient or the unreviewed P-fixed feasibility computation.

Let F=F₂₀=π(H), P=C₅ normal in F, W=H∩V, and z the constant vector in X. The F-set of six points has orbits of lengths 1 and 5, so X^P is a 2-dimensional trivial F-module, while Y^P=0. The augmentation X→F₂ is a nonzero S₅-invariant functional. The reviewed invariant-functional obstruction makes its restriction to W nonzero; since the P-idempotent preserves the value of this functional, W^P≠0. Core-freeness gives z∉W. As W^P≤X^P and dim X^P=2, this forces W^P to be a line distinct from ⟨z⟩. Thus (V/W)^P is a one-dimensional trivial F-module generated by z+W.

Let $`i:\mathbf1\to X`$ send 1 to z. Shapiro identifies the induced map with restriction, in the commutative diagram


```math
\begin{array}{ccc}
H^2(S_5,\mathbf1)&\xrightarrow{i_*}&H^2(S_5,X)\\
\mathop{\mathrm{res}}\nolimits\downarrow&&\downarrow\mathop{\mathrm{Shapiro}}\nolimits^{\cong}\\
H^2(F,\mathbf1)&=&H^2(F,\mathbf1).
\end{array}
```


Since F has a normal subgroup of odd order 5, inflation identifies H²(F,F₂) with H²(C₄,F₂)≅F₂. The restriction in the diagram is onto: the central spin class with involutory transposition lifts has an order-8 lift of a C₄-generator of F, so its restriction to H²(F,F₂) is nonzero. Nagy [arXiv:0709.4544v1](https://arxiv.org/html/0709.4544v1), Lemma 3.1, gives this central extension and its order-eight lift; the restriction detection is also recorded in the reviewed [principal-envelope argument](bruck-s5-principal-envelope-bound.md#proposed-invariant-functional-obstruction) at [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/6a563df3804682acb6bd4191fff2ac749cb0bd16). Consequently the unique nonzero class in H²(S₅,X) is that spin class pushed out along z. In the F-module V/W, the P-idempotent projects equivariantly onto (V/W)^P, carrying z to the nonzero vector z+W. Therefore the nonzero global class, upon restriction to F and projection through V/W and then (V/W)^P, becomes the nonzero spin class in H²(F,F₂). But H exists, so the restricted extension class comes from H²(F,W) and its image in H²(F,V/W) vanishes. Contradiction. With the reviewed H²(S₅,Y)=0, the extension G must therefore split.

For each P-module, the P-idempotent is the average over P (equal to the sum in characteristic 2); since P is normal in F it is F-equivariant and, because |P| is odd, its image is exactly the P-fixed subspace. The P-idempotent gives V/W=(V/W)^P⊕Q for F, and Q is projective because all its composition factors are the projective 4-dimensional F-simple. Hence H¹(F,V/W) is 1-dimensional. The global cocycle g↦sign(g)z restricts to its nonzero element, so a global cocycle automorphism of V⋊S₅ and conjugation by V normalize H to W⋊F.

On a transposition subgroup T, P6 is free (three transposition pairs), and U is free, so H¹(T,V)=H¹(T,N^a)=(F₂²)^a. The centralizer quotient S₃ acts diagonally naturally, with exactly one fixed point, zero; all other orbits have sizes 3 or 6. By Baumeister–Stein [arXiv:0908.2597v1](https://arxiv.org/html/0908.2597v1), Lemmas 4.1 and 4.5, the K-fiber above t is a union of V-orbits and S₃-orbits. The number of V-orbits is a positive power of 2 by the Bruck-envelope fiber count. Reduction modulo 3 forces the zero label, or makes the orbit count impossible. Zero gives standard t∈K. Conjugacy gives all standard transpositions; disjoint t,u with tu∈F yield tu∈H, contradicting transversality.

This candidate exclusion uses no quotient by Y, assumes no group splitting, and depends on independent review of its H² pushout and normalization argument. If accepted it covers P6⊕Y for all a,b, not other principal isotypes.

The identification of principal-isotype class 7 with P6 has a separate scoped model review; it is needed only to combine this candidate exclusion with the principal-isotype list. The argument for P6 itself does not use the unreviewed numerical P-fixed feasibility test. The independent review of this manuscript is recorded below.

It also checked the ensuing H¹ normalization and the natural-S₃ power-of-two fiber contradiction. No splitting is assumed before the cohomology obstruction.
