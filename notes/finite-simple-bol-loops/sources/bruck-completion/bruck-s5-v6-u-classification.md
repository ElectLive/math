## Normalization and outer fibers

The V₆ fiber bounds yield W₀=W^P=C_V(P) of dimension 2, W₁=[P,W] dimension 4, and q=1. Therefore K contains all involution lifts over all transpositions. The preimage of P in H has normal 2-subgroup W and complements P conjugate by W. Conjugating in V arranges a fixed order-five P in H. Since P is normal in F=N_{S₅}(P), the same conjugacy argument makes N_H(P)→F onto. Both N_G(P) and N_H(P) have order 4·20, so N_H(P)=N_G(P) and H=W N_G(P). This does not quotient the loop.

If the extension splits, normalize P to the standard pure subgroup. Then N_G(P)=W₀⋊F contains pure F. Distinct pure disjoint transpositions t,u with tu∈F both lie in K and satisfy tu∈H, contradicting transversality. In the nonsplit case, H²(S₅,V₆) has the unique nonzero class and H²(S₅,U)=0. The reviewed global cocycle in `verify_s5_v6_h2.py` constructs it; its the recorded source revision JSON matches byte-for-byte the generator at the recorded source revision (SHA-256 `4fc0026d4f7e0eb74bcc7d7501ebacbf962a33edcbf7767a1d4a102b02ea470a`). The certificate regenerates the latter and checks both C,D matrices against the V₆ module, rather than trusting a preexisting file.

On F=F₂₀, [P,V]≅S³ for the four-dimensional simple projective S with End_F(S)=F₂. Thus W₁ is one of seven lines in the 3-dimensional multiplicity space. The certificate explicitly computes all seven Hom maps, checks rank four, and evaluates W∩(1+t)V: dimensions 3,3,2,0,0,0,0. Since all involution lifts in the t fiber belong to K, nonzero intersection violates transversality. For each of the four remaining W, H is core-free by direct tests against every nonzero S₅-submodule of V.

Same-outer-fiber collisions are impossible because W∩(1+t)V=0. Products of transpositions are identity, a 3-cycle, or a double transposition; F has no 3-cycles. Mixed radical/outer pairs cannot collide, as F has no transpositions. The certificate checks all five unordered distinct transposition pairs whose product is in F, using the actual nonsplit cocycle and normalized N_G(P), and finds no collision for all four W. Hence all remaining collisions are radical–radical, exactly the W-transversal condition on D=K∩V. For any such invariant transversal containing 0, the union K=C∪D consists of the identity and involutions, is G-invariant and therefore closed under xyx. Its size is 1280+256=1536=[G:H]. Absence of collisions makes K a transversal to H, and G-invariance transports this to every conjugate of H. With the verified core-freeness and the generation condition below, these are faithful exponent-two Bol (hence Bruck) folders.

## Exact covers, generation and simplicity

The program enumerates all 196 S₅-vector orbits of V, uses all W-cosets and every eligible orbit as an exact-cover row, and runs an uncapped deterministic backtracking enumeration. Requiring the identity orbit {0}, the normalized types 4 and 5 give respectively 56 and 21 covers inside J⊕U, and 362 and 123 covers meeting its complement. Every completed cover is independently checked to contain 0, have 256 vectors in distinct W-cosets, and be invariant under the S₅ generators. The nontrivial S₅-automorphism of U interchanges W types 4↔6 and 5↔7; no candidates are lost by retaining 4 and 5.

Let C be the full class of involution lifts above the transpositions and A=⟨C⟩. A is normal, maps onto S₅, and contains [V,A]=[V,S₅]=J⊕U. The reviewed dimension-six certificate gives the index-two subgroup in the V₆ extension containing every such lift and meeting V₆ in J; after adjoining U it has radical J⊕U. Therefore A is precisely that index-two subgroup. It follows that ⟨C∪D⟩=G exactly when D meets V\(J⊕U).

The exact module lattices are 0<M<J<V₆ and 0<N<U; no mixed submodule occurs by the reviewed block separation, giving 11 nonzero sums S. If R◁G projects nontrivially to S₅, its image contains A₅ and [V,R] contains [V,A₅]=M⊕U. W₀ complements M in V₆, and FA₅=S₅; hence HR=G. Thus the C0001 normal-subloop criterion reduces proper quotients to these S≤V. For each S the certificate checks

 |KS/S| = |(D+S)/S| + 10·128/|C_V(t)∩S|,
 [G:HS] = 6·2^(14−dim(W+S)).

For S=V these are 11 and 6, respectively; this case is included. All 362+123 generating identity-containing covers pass all 11 tests. Subject to independent verification of the criterion and its hypotheses, these folders are simple.

## Isomorphism reduction and scope

Aut(S₅) is inner and N_{S₅}(F)=F. A folder isomorphism between the normalized candidates can therefore be adjusted by a target H-element to act trivially on S₅. The certificate checks End(V₆)=F₂, both cross-block Hom spaces zero, and dim End(U)=2; the two units of End(U)=F₂[ε]/ε² are identity and the swap. The swap takes W4 to W6 and W5 to W7, so an adjusted isomorphism preserving either normalized W fixes V pointwise, hence fixes D. Distinct covers at a fixed W therefore give distinct folder and loop isomorphism types, conditional on the canonical-folder equivalence. Together with the finite certificate and the review scopes below, this gives 362+123=485.
