# Odd-characteristic E6 graph-only full-socle exclusion

Continuation of [0323:C0010](../foundations/research-C0010.md), with independent exact-source review recorded below. Subject to the exact source inputs below, the actual full multiplication socle of a finite simple non-Moufang left Bol loop cannot be ²E6(q) with q odd, or E6(p^f) with p and f odd. The proof establishes the graph-only cases G/S=1 or C3 uniformly for either rational form; it does not address split E6(p^f) with f even. The original canonical actual folder is used throughout; no quotient folder is formed. The exact-source mathematical reviews below retain all stated classification and rational-form inputs.

## Reduction to the graph labels

C0008 gives almost simple M=Mlt(Q)=GI with socle S, G=LMlt(Q) and I the point stabilizer. If S is not in G, it is not in I since the primitive action is faithful; this contradicts the exceptional proper-factorization list in Kazarin–Martínez-Pastor–Pérez-Ramos (2015), Lemma 11 pp.56–57. Hence S≤G≤Aut(S). By the actual canonical interface at 932ead5db5f39045e2085caa6b062f6b981b2813, Λ=τK consists of outside involutions, is G-invariant, contains τ, has |Λ|=[G:H], and satisfies H∩Λ²=1, while K generates G. The inner-inversion theorem 84eba0ffd4dfab4b6d64853a3131010ad2b945b8 implies the order-two label r of τ is not in L=G/S.

Let d=(3,q−1) for the split form or d=(3,q+1) for the twisted form. Outer-group structures are retained from Burness–Guralnick–Harper arXiv:2006.01421v2, §3.2 Table 2 and Lemmas 3.10–3.12, with field generators normalized as there. When d=1, the graph-field outer group is cyclic of order 2f with unique involution r0 the graph label. Every generator of L is inverted by r, whose action is trivial on the outer group; thus L has exponent two. If L is nontrivial, it contains r0=r, contradicting r∉L. Therefore L=1 and r=r0. For split E6 with f odd and d=3, p≡1 mod3 and Out(S)≅S3×C_f; for twisted ²E6 with d=3, p≡−1 mod3 and f is odd, with the normalized outer group again S3×C_f. The projection of every generating element inverted by r to the central odd C_f factor is trivial; r has trivial C_f coordinate as well. Hence L≤S3 and r is a reflection normalizing L but outside it. The only possibilities are L=1 or its normal C3. This covers every twisted odd q, and split odd-characteristic fields of odd extension degree. The same argument applies whenever the original outer action is graph-only with L=1 or C3, irrespective of the parity of f.

Thus all 24 σ-fixed root spaces must have sign +1 under σ: 4+24+24=52 and each contributes at most one. Therefore dim C_G(σt)=28+# of fixed roots on which t acts as +1. The certificate finds that number 8 for exactly 12 points and 24 for the other four; the two graph-involution algebraic centralizer types are C4 dimension 36 and F4 dimension 52. For u,v among these 12 candidates, (σu)(σv)=uv because u and v are σ-fixed and commute. ## Nonidentity square witness in the F4 graph class

The checker asserts that the 24-fixed-root set has four torus points, including nonidentity t=20, whose internal fixed-root count is 40. Thus σ and σt have algebraic F4 centralizer, while t is a nonidentity involution in C_S(σ) (the unique odd-kernel torus lift makes t rational in S for both Frobenius forms). ## Both graph types selected

In either G case the class-size factor cancels and |H|=F/(1+A). Put

 D0=1+A=q^16+q^12+q^8+1.

For odd q, q^4≡1 mod16, so D0≡4 mod16. Thus D0/4 is odd and greater than 1. It is coprime to q and to every q^j−1 for j=2,6,8,12:

* For j=2, D0≡4 mod q²−1.
* For j=8, put y=q^4. Modulo q^8−1 one has y²=1 and D0=y+3. Its gcd with y²−1 divides 8, since evaluating y²−1 at y=−3 gives 8.
* For j=6, put y=q². Modulo q^6−1 one has y³=1 and D0=y²+y+2. Multiplication by y−1 gives (y−1)D0≡y−1. Therefore their gcd divides y−1 and hence divides D0 evaluated at y=1, namely 4.
* For j=12, the identical argument with y=q^4 applies.

Hence D0/4 is coprime to F, making F/D0 nonintegral. This excludes the union without a class-square assertion.
