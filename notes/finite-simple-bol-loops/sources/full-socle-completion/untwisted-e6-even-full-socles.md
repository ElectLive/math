## Actual outer labels and pure graph reduction

The actual full multiplication group M is almost simple and factorizes M=GI, with G=LMlt(Q). By the exceptional proper-factorization list quoted in Kazarin–Martínez-Pastor–Pérez-Ramos (2015), Lemma 11 pp.56–57, S≤G: otherwise neither G nor I contains S, contradicting that list. The canonical inversion τ restricts faithfully to Aut(S), since C_Aut(G)(S)=1, and its label r lies outside L=G/S by the inner-inversion obstruction at 84eba0ffd4dfab4b6d64853a3131010ad2b945b8. Put q=2^f and Out(S)=S3×C_f, with D=C3 the diagonal subgroup, s a graph reflection, and z the unique involution of C_f. This structure is in Burness–Guralnick–Harper, arXiv:2006.01421v2, §3.2 Table 2 and Lemma 3.10. The actual generating set K consists of elements inverted by τ. Projection to the central C_f therefore has exponent at most 2. Hence L≤S3×<z>. An order-two r has field coordinate 1 or z; its S3 coordinate is 1 or a reflection. Conjugate the reflection to s when present.

The marks τK generate G after multiplication by τ, so their outer labels generate L in that sense. In the S3×<z> outer subgroup, outside involution labels have either field coordinate z (semilinear) or field coordinate 1 (graph reflection). The identity label cannot occur, since it would put r in L. If there is no selected semilinear label, τ itself has graph label, say r=s, and every label in Λ is a reflection in S3. Products of any two graph reflections lie in D, so the generation of K gives L≤D. Thus the graph-only cases are exactly L=1 or D. This argument never assumes a group generated by involutions is elementary abelian; in particular no erroneous restriction on the r=z cases is used.

## Proposed semilinear-class elimination (new; independently unreviewed)

This section remains conditional on its stated Lang, fixed-point and class-correspondence interfaces. It concerns the original actual group and marked set only. Put s=2^(f/2), q=s², and let α be either the physical field involution F_s or the physical graph-field involution γF_s on the simply connected split algebraic E6. These commute with F_q and α²=F_q. Their rational fixed groups are, respectively, the simply connected groups E6(s) and ²E6(s). Passing to the centerless finite socle gives centralizers of the corresponding simple-group orders. The finite simply connected center has order3, and H^1(<alpha>,Z)=1 because the acting group has order2. Thus taking alpha-fixed points of G_sc(q) onto S is surjective, with kernel Z^alpha. Its order is gcd(3,s-1) for F_s and gcd(3,s+1) for gamma F_s, giving exactly the simple fixed-group orders stated here.

**Actual-class square.** Every involution u in C_S(α) has a unique involutory lift to the simply connected finite group: the odd central kernel makes the lift unique and α-invariant. Write uhat for this lift. Lang's theorem for α supplies x with x^(-1)α(x)=uhat. As α(uhat)=uhat and uhat^2=1, α²(x)=x, so x lies in the F_q-fixed simply connected group. Thus αu is S-conjugate to α, and u∈(α^S)². The two fixed finite groups contain representatives of all three involution classes of S: each has all three algebraic involution types, and the finite/algebraic class bijection of Burness–Thomas, Remark 2.2 and Proposition 2.3, identifies these types with the three S-classes. Consequently (α^S)² contains all S-involutions. This is a statement about actual S-conjugates, not an image of a quotient folder.

For each outer label of an involution of physical field or graph-field type there is one S-class. Indeed, an arbitrary involution β in the same label as α equals xα with x∈S. Its involutory lift to the odd-center extension exists by Sylow conjugacy/Schur–Zassenhaus and has form x̃α with x̃∈G_sc(q), and x̃α(x̃)=1. Lang again gives x̃=y^(-1)α(y), with y∈G_sc(q) since α²(y)=y. Graph-diagonal transport handles the other reflection labels. The diagonal outer group cyclically permutes those three labels; the central label has just one label. This also shows no field-class splitting is being silently ignored.

The physical field form is the central outer label when s≡1 mod 3 and a graph-reflection label when s≡−1 mod 3; the physical graph-field form has the opposite label type. This is because the field involution acts on the diagonal C3 by s-powering. Consequently at most one physical-form class of one kind and at most three of the other kind can occur. It is enough below to use the weaker uniform bounds n,m≤3. An S-invariant marked set is an actual union of these S-classes and the graph classes.

If any semilinear S-class is selected, the square result forces B=H∩S to have odd order. Since L is contained in S3 times <z> and hence has 2-part at most4, |H|_2≤4, whereas |S|_2=q^36. Therefore

    v2(|Λ|)=v2([G:H])≥36f−2.                         (1)

We now show this contradicts the actual union size, without assuming Λ is a single class. The graph involution classes have sizes c and cA, where c_2-part=q^12 and A=(q^12−1)(q^4+1). For each of the three graph labels there is at most one S-class of each type, by AS §19 and the centralizer projection argument in the prior section. If a,b∈{0,1,2,3} are their selected numbers, the graph contribution is c(a+bA).

The field and graph-field centralizer orders are |E6(s)| and |²E6(s)|, and each has 2-part s^36=q^18. Thus each semilinear class size has 2-part q^18. If a≠b, A≡−1 mod q^4 and 0<|a−b|≤3, giving v2 of the graph contribution at most 12f+1. If a=b>0, then 1+A=q^4(q^12+q^8−1), giving valuation 16f+v2(a)≤16f+1. Both are strictly less than 18f, so adding arbitrary selected semilinear classes cannot alter that valuation. In either case (1) is impossible.

It remains to treat a=b=0. Set d_-=gcd(3,s−1), d_+=gcd(3,s+1), and let n,m be the numbers of selected classes with field, respectively graph-field, centralizer. The simple-group order formulas yield

 |E6(s)|_odd = ((s^12−1)(s^9−1)(s^8−1)(s^6−1)(s^5−1)(s²−1))/d_-,
 |²E6(s)|_odd = ((s^12−1)(s^9+1)(s^8−1)(s^6−1)(s^5+1)(s²−1))/d_+.

All divisions here are exact odd integers. After dividing the semilinear class sum by q^18, the two class-size terms have a common odd unit, and the quotient is congruent modulo 2s^5 to that unit times n d_-+m d_+. Each ratio (s^5+1)/(s^5−1) and (s^9+1)/(s^9−1) is congruent to −1 modulo 2s^5, so their product is congruent to 1; all denominators are odd and hence invertible 2-adically. Since (n,m)≠(0,0), n,m≤3, and {d_-,d_+}={1,3}, the integer n d_-+m d_+ lies between 1 and 12 and has 2-adic valuation at most 3. As 2s^5 is divisible by 2^6, the semilinear class sum has valuation at most 18f+3, again contradicting (1) for f≥2.

Thus, under the separately stated fixed-group, Lang, conjugacy and three-class inputs, every actual Λ containing a semilinear involution is excluded. The initial graph-only reduction shows every remaining case has a selected semilinear involution. Together with the earlier proposed pure-graph maximal filter, this gives a proposed full exclusion of untwisted E6(2^f) with f even as the full multiplication socle.
