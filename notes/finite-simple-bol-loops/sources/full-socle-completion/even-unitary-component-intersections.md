## Reusable rank-two square lemma

Let L=SU_d(q), d odd≥5, q even. Every nonidentity involutory automorphism of L/Z(L) has a component-conjugacy-class square containing the rank-two involution class. For an outer automorphism this is the reviewed finite form/descent and fixed-representative argument in [radical-free-unitary-inversion](../primitive-completion/radical-free-unitary-inversion.md), exact source fa462cdffd3082b37b0f100ea7e0364a97832473: the required outside conjugates lie in one actual component class, not merely a fused full-automorphism class. Transporting diagonal labels and the actual odd-central lift use the reviewed interfaces named there and at 1d64. The standard automorphism input is Steinberg, *Automorphisms of finite linear groups*, Theorem 3.2 and statements 3.3–3.6, printed p.608, as cited in that source.

For an inner involution, its rank r is at most (d−1)/2. If r=1, two commuting transvections on independent orthogonal isotropic directions multiply to rank 2. For r≥2, use a hyperbolic 2r-space and commuting u(A)=[[I,A],[0,I]], u(A+D), where A,D are Hermitian. Choose ω∈F_{q²} outside F_q, let A=diag([[1,ω],[barω,0]],I_{r−2}) and D=diag([[0,1],[1,0]],0). Both A and A+D are nonsingular: their 2×2 determinants are N(ω) and N(ω+1), both nonzero also when q=2. The involutions have the same rank r and are SU-conjugate; their product u(D) has rank 2. The positive-dimensional 1-block unitary centralizer realizes every norm-one determinant, so the U-class does not split in SU. Odd-center lifting and component conjugacy then give the actual marked collision whenever H∩L contains such a rank-two involution.
