In the opposite orientation, suppose the actual maximal point stabilizer I tightly contains 3.A7. We show that I normalizes a nondegenerate one-space, contradicting its stated solvable residual. Work in the SU4(5) lift over F25. The derived subgroup Ltilde of the full preimage of 3.A7 is perfect and maps onto 3.A7 with central kernel of order at most 2. Lift a central element of order 3 to an order-3 element z. Its commutator with Ltilde takes values in the central kernel; perfectness forces that homomorphism to vanish. The element z is not scalar, since the SU4(5) scalar center has order 2. Its distinct eigenvalues lie in μ3⊂F25, have unitary norm 1, and their eigenspaces are nondegenerate, orthogonal, and Ltilde-invariant.

At least one eigenspace E has dimension at most 2. On E, the central 3-subgroup acts by scalars, so the projective action factors through A7; the extra central kernel also acts by scalars. Any nontrivial projective image would embed A7 into PGL2(25), impossible since |A7|=2520 does not divide |PGL2(25)|=15600. The same holds in dimension 1. Thus the action on E is scalar, and perfectness makes it trivial. If dim E=2, its orthogonal complement has dimension 2 and is a direct sum of the other z-eigenspaces; applying the same argument separately to each of those eigenspaces makes the whole action trivial, impossible. Hence E is a fixed nonsingular line. The only possible nontrivial eigenspace decomposition is now 1+3: if all eigenspaces had dimension at most 2, the preceding argument would make the whole action trivial. On the fixed line z acts as 1, and on the remaining three-space it acts by a distinct eigenvalue. Thus no vector in that complement is fixed by the group, proving uniqueness of the fixed line.

The perfect-derived lift is intrinsic to 3.A7. A projective semilinear normalizer of that subgroup normalizes this lift: any scalar discrepancy defines a homomorphism from the perfect lift to the scalar group and therefore vanishes. It preserves the unique fixed line. Thus actual I≤N_M(line), and maximality gives equality. The nondegenerate line normalizer has residual SU3(5), up to its projective central kernel, strictly larger than 3.A7; this contradicts I^(∞)=3.A7.
