# Proposed exclusion of pure diagonal canonical inversion in odd E7

Continuation of [0323:C0010](../foundations/research-C0010.md), with independent exact-source review recorded below. Suppose a finite simple non-Moufang left Bol loop has actual canonical G=S=E7(q), q odd, and canonical inversion τ induces an outer diagonal involution. Under the cited canonical, algebraic-group, and finite 2-group inputs, no selected diagonal class is possible. Independent exact-revision review is recorded below; it makes no assertion for field or mixed outer labels, larger canonical overgroups, or all E7 full socles.

Let D=Inndiag(S), |D:S|=2. Burness–Thomas, Remark 2.4 p.8, https://seis.bristol.ac.uk/~tb13602/docs/BTh_revised_2.pdf, gives exactly two D-classes of outside involutions, algebraic centralizer types A7.2 and T1E6.2, and three S-classes of inner involutions. Each outside D-class is a single S-class because an outside involution belongs to its own centralizer. The nonempty actual selected set Λ is therefore one or both of these classes. The independently reviewed [two-class union exclusion](e7-odd-diagonal-union.md) at exact source `afd94837b5afafb3316a865911fd25fbcd6bc775` is used only for the both-class alternative. Its connected-centralizer order calculation is also retained below. Now assume one class C is selected.

Let d:G_sc→G_ad be the simply-connected central isogeny of split algebraic E7. On maximal torus 2-torsion, the simply-connected cocharacter lattice is the coroot lattice and the adjoint character lattice is the root lattice; hence the map, in simple-root value coordinates, is the Cartan matrix mod 2. The [exact root-datum certificate](../code/verify_e7_diagonal_torus.py) checks that this map on all 128 inputs has kernel size 2 and image exactly {0} together with the 63-element Weyl orbit. It also checks 126 norm-two roots, the complete four torus orbits of sizes 1,63,36,28 and fixed-root counts 126,62,56,72, and that the product of any two distinct elements within either the 36- or 28-orbit is in the 63-orbit. Noncentral semisimple involutions lie in an algebraic maximal torus, and all such tori are conjugate, so every noncentral algebraic involution in G_sc maps to the algebraic 63-orbit, of type A1D6. Conversely the 36- and 28-orbit involutions have no involution lift: their two lifts have order four and central square. Root classification and rational torus facts are the stated external Lie-theoretic inputs, not proved by the finite checker.

The longest Weyl element acts as -1: it sends the simple roots to their negatives followed by a diagram automorphism, and the E7 diagram has no nontrivial automorphism (its three arms have distinct lengths). For q≡3 mod4 take an F-stable split adjoint torus; for q≡1 mod4 take a torus of Weyl type w0=−1. Its 2-torsion is rational, and all Weyl elements have rational normalizer representatives: w0 is central and Lang on the connected torus makes the normalizer map onto the full rational Weyl group (Dudas-Michel2015 Proposition7.10 p.26 and Proposition7.12(i) p.27, https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~jean.michel/papiers/lectures_beijing_2015.pdf). Frobenius on the corresponding sc torus is q or −q, both congruent to −1 modulo 4. Thus the order-four lifts of its 36- and 28-orbit involutions are interchanged, not rational; the adjoint representatives are outside S by the rational central-isogeny exact sequence. Any two distinct elements in either orbit are rational D-conjugate, hence S-conjugate because an outside element centralizes itself. Their product lies in the 63-orbit, is an S-involution of algebraic type A1D6, and lies in C². As BT states there is one S-class of this algebraic type, C² contains the entire t1 class.

Let H≤S be the actual stabilizer, and suppose it avoids t1. For any Sylow 2-subgroup P of H, its full preimage P̂ in the rational simply-connected cover 2.S has exactly one involution, the central kernel element: any other involution would project to a noncentral algebraic sc involution, hence to t1. Keith Conrad, *Generalized quaternions*, Theorem4.10 p.8 and its Appendix A proof pp.12-15, https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/grouptheory/genquat.pdf, therefore makes P̂ cyclic or generalized quaternion; it contains an element of order at least |P̂|/2=|P|. Its image in S has order at least |P|/2.

The simple S acts faithfully on its 133-dimensional adjoint Lie algebra in odd characteristic: its kernel is normal and S is simple, while the action is nontrivial: a t1 torus representative fixes62 roots and negates the other64 root spaces in odd characteristic. The dimension is126 root spaces plus rank7. Thus no faithfulness claim about a minuscule module is needed. A 2-element in S is semisimple and its eigenvalue orders divide 2-parts of q^i−1 for i≤133. Put B=(q²−1)_2≥8. The lifting-the-exponent formula gives (q^i−1)_2≤64B for all i≤133: if i is odd it is at most B, and if even its 2-valuation is v2(q²−1)+v2(i)−1≤v2(B)+6. Thus every 2-element of S has order ≤64B, and |P|≤128B.

The original section count gives |H|=|C_S(t)|. The rational connected-centralizer calculation in the reviewed two-class union source writes it as either |SL8^ε(q)| or (q−η)|E6,sc^δ(q)|, with signs as there. In the first polynomial, the four independent even-degree factors i=2,4,6,8 contribute at least B^4 to |H|_2; in the second, i=2,6,8,12 do so. Therefore |P|=|H|_2≥B^4>128B, since B≥8. This contradiction forces H to meet t1, and hence H∩C²≠1, contradicting the original canonical transversal.
