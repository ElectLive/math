# Even-dimensional unitary line and parabolic factors

Independently reviewed continuation of [0323:C0010](../foundations/research-C0010.md), conditional on the stated inputs. Let Q be a finite simple proper left Bol loop, M=Mlt(Q) almost simple with socle S=PSU_(2m)(q), m≥2, G=LMlt(Q), I=M_1 and H=G∩I. Assume the two actual factors G,I are nonsolvable and do not contain S. The following two exclusions use their actual embeddings and the stated canonical/source inputs:

1. If I^(∞) is the natural embedded line component L=SU_(2m−1)(q), then no such Q exists, for every q.
2. If q is even, G^(∞)=L and I^(∞) is the source parabolic residual with nontrivial unipotent part P≤R, then no such Q exists, including proper P<R. Here R=q^(m²) is the Siegel unipotent radical, and the residual quotient is a central quotient of SL_a(q^(2b)), Sp_a(q^(2b)), or G2(q^(2b)) in its source extension-field action, with m=ab or m=6b respectively.

The first statement covers the line-stabilizer orientation of Li–Wang–Xia unitary Table1.1 rows1–8,14,16–18 and any actual partial-radical pair satisfying the displayed residual hypothesis. The second covers the opposite orientation of rows1–3 in even characteristic and the corresponding partial-radical alternative. It does not settle the other opposite orientations, odd-characteristic parabolic cases, finite rows9–13, or socle-containing canonical groups.

## The actual full stabilizer is on the line side

Suppose I^(∞)=L. Then I≤N_M(L)<M. The normalizer is proper: otherwise L would be normal in S, contrary to 1<L<S and simplicity of S. Maximality of the original I gives I=N_M(L).

Here N_S(L) is exactly the nonsingular-line stabilizer. To see this in the covering group, the inverse image of L is LZ, where Z is the ambient scalar center, and (LZ)'=L. A projective normalizer therefore normalizes the genuine embedded L. The natural L-module has its unique fixed line ⟨v⟩ and an irreducible complement v-perp, so every normalizer preserves that line; conversely its line stabilizer normalizes L. This is the [reviewed line-normalizer argument](../odd-completion/odd-envelope-parameterization.md), exact source `a1eb46e7c4dcaa3200f1160e45cf464784d1746b`, used only for its embedding/normalizer statement. It follows that the original S-set is its nonsingular-point action.

The full original M-action is the corresponding semilinear action. One can identify it directly using M/I and the semilinear representatives just cited: N_M(L) is the line stabilizer, S is transitive on nonsingular lines by Witt extension and determinant adjustment, and the two coset actions agree. Thus no hypothetical change of the point stabilizer is involved.

For q≥3 the [reviewed nonorthogonal invariant theorem](../primitive-completion/unitary-nonorthogonal.md), source `13e21094894aed868a16d7859db036ca7cfc1a69`, excludes every loop with multiplication group in this natural action. For q=2 the ambient dimension is at least6, and the even-unitary nonsingular-point subproof of [C0016(c)](../../claims/0016.md), independently reviewed at `15ec5fb55fba8e614b180cff75e95516031285b6`, supplies the exclusion. This uses that scoped subproof, not the unproved aggregate parts of C0016. The excluded dimension4 binary line factor was addressed by solvability above.

## A nonzero parabolic radical forces the whole radical into I

Now let I be on the parabolic side. We prove the following embedding step for the displayed nonzero P in any characteristic, before using even characteristic for the collision. Assume I^(∞) is the actual projective residual P.K in the source model, where 1<P≤R and the quotient K is one of the displayed natural extension-field classical/exceptional groups up to its central scalar quotient.

Each permitted nonsolvable K has O_p(K)=1, where q has characteristic p: the linear and symplectic groups over q^(2b) are quasisimple with p-prime center, and G2(q^(2b)) in its permitted even characteristic is simple since q^(2b)≥4. A trivial SL1 factor cannot give a nonsolvable residual. Hence

    P=O_p(I^(∞)),

so P is characteristic in I. The quotient action on U is irreducible over F_(q²). For SL_a and Sp_a this follows from transitivity on nonzero vectors of their extension-field natural module; for G2 the same nonzero-vector transitivity is the cited natural-module input retained at `4169b5a5219bf8872dcf8723a2dbea6e07ddb111`. Restricting scalars preserves irreducibility here precisely because that transitivity persists. The dual, possibly field-twisted action on W is also irreducible.

Elements of R have block form 1+N(B), with N(B) mapping W into U and killing U. For P their common kernel on W is invariant under the quotient K: conjugation by the residual induces the natural action on these blocks, irrespective of whether its extension by P splits. Since P is nontrivial, that kernel is not all W. Irreducibility makes it zero. Therefore the common fixed space of the actual unipotent lifts is intrinsically

    C_V(P)=U.                                        (1)

Projectivization introduces no ambiguity in (1). The scalar kernel has order prime to p, and the inverse image of the embedded P is the direct product of its unipotent lift with that central scalar group. Its unique Sylow p-subgroup is that lift. A semilinear normalizer of projective P consequently normalizes the lift and preserves its common fixed space. Since I normalizes P, it preserves U. Its normalizer in M is proper, because S does not preserve a fixed maximal isotropic subspace. Maximality of the actual point stabilizer now forces

    I=N_M(U),   and consequently R≤I.                (2)

This uses the original primitive action and the exact residual equality. It does not claim that every invariant nonzero subgroup P equals R or contains every rank. In particular the source's product condition GI⊇R is not used as if it implied I≥R; equation(2) is a separate consequence of maximality.

## The even-characteristic intersection meets every involution class

Assume now q is even and G^(∞)=L. Its center has odd order, L is characteristic and perfect, and L/Z(L) is nonabelian simple. By (2), actual H contains L∩R in the source's common model. Lemma4.1(b) identifies this covering intersection as q^((m−1)²): it fixes the first hyperbolic plane pointwise and is the Hermitian-block group on the remaining m−1 planes. Equivalently, for every Hermitian (m−1)-square matrix B over F_(q²), it contains

    u_B=1+N(B),   N(B)²=0,   rank(N(B))=rank(B).

For each r=1,...,m−1 choose B=diag(I_r,0). The resulting element is an actual involution of rank r on v-perp, with determinant1, lying in H∩L. The [reviewed unitary involution-class argument](../primitive-completion/radical-free-unitary-inversion.md#unitary-involution-classes-and-their-2-parts), source `fa462cdffd3082b37b0f100ea7e0364a97832473`, gives exactly one SU_(2m−1)(q)-class per such rank. Its determinant correction uses the nonzero residual one-block, and the odd central kernel does not identify distinct involutory lifts. Thus H∩L meets every nonidentity L-involution class.

The [reviewed odd-central characteristic-component obstruction](marked-involution-vector-obstruction.md), exact source `1d64c9301e1fa3a8aae1a86c9a416d731cc419f8`, now applies to the original selected marks. It forces a nontrivial intersection H∩Λ², a contradiction. No faithful restriction of G to Aut(L), scalar-free lift assumption, or quotient-folder assertion is required.

## Odd-characteristic completion using the full parabolic

Independently reviewed continuation of [0323:C0010](../foundations/research-C0010.md), conditional on the stated inputs. Let a finite simple nonassociative Bol loop have primitive almost-simple full multiplication group M with socle PSU_{2m}(q), q odd, m≥2, and actual factors G=LMlt(Q), I=M₁. Suppose the actual line-side canonical component is the image L of SU_{2m−1}(q), while I has a nonzero characteristic parabolic radical P and satisfies all fixed-space/residual hypotheses of the reviewed parabolic-maximality lemma above, exact source b4318157e60b854d0ff136faf496d872b7065dce. In particular, this is the source's actual common model and its explicit partial-radical assumptions, including the recorded source subscript caveat; no conclusion is inferred for arbitrary nonzero subgroups P. Then the original marked-folder obstruction excludes this configuration, subject to the stated reviewed inputs.

The b431 fixed-space lemma is all-characteristic up to and including I=N_M(U), even though its earlier involution-collision conclusion was restricted to characteristic two. Here U is the fixed maximal totally isotropic m-space and I is the actual maximal point stabilizer. Thus I contains the entire intersection S∩N_M(U), i.e. the projective full parabolic P_m(S), not merely its unipotent radical R. This follows directly from maximality and the actual normalizer equality; it makes no assertion that P itself contains an involution.

In the common covering Hermitian space choose hyperbolic basis e1,…,em,f1,…,fm, U=⟨e1,…,em⟩, and source nonsingular line ⟨v⟩ with v=e1+λf1 and λ+barλ≠0. The embedded line-side SU_{2m−1}(q) fixes v pointwise and acts on v^⊥. For each 1≤r≤m−1, define a_r to be −I on any r of the hyperbolic pairs ⟨ei,fi⟩ with i≥2 and +I on every other vector. It fixes v, is unitary, has determinant 1, preserves U, and its negative eigenspace on v^⊥ has dimension 2r. Hence its actual image lies both in L and in the full parabolic contained in I; therefore a_r∈H∩L. The embedding of SU(v^⊥) into the ambient projective group has no scalar kernel: a scalar matrix fixing the nonzero vector v is 1. In particular the displayed involutions are actual, not merely projected elements. The component's own center can be nontrivial, but has odd order dividing (2m−1,q+1).

Every involution of SU_{2m−1}(q) is semisimple, and determinant one makes the −1 eigenspace dimension even, necessarily 2r with 1≤r≤m−1. Its positive and negative spaces are nondegenerate; finite Hermitian spaces of each dimension are isometric, so the dimension 2r determines its U-conjugacy class. The positive eigenspace is nonzero and its unitary group has surjective determinant onto μ_{q+1}; multiplying a U-conjugator by an appropriate centralizer element makes it an SU-conjugator. Thus each actual L-involution class meets H in one of the displayed a_r. No assumption is made about quotient folders or simplicity of a projected loop.

Apply the reviewed original marked-involution obstruction in [the marked-component theorem](marked-involution-vector-obstruction.md), exact source 1d64c9301e1fa3a8aae1a86c9a416d731cc419f8: for a characteristic quasisimple component with odd center, at least one original selected mark has nontrivial induced action, and its component-conjugate square supplies a nonidentity actual component involution. Since every such class meets H, actual conjugacy invariance gives H∩Λ²≠1, contradiction. The scalar/semilinear ambient overgroup and any partial radical remain actual throughout.
