# Uniform binary rank-two tensor obstruction

**Independently reviewed scoped continuation of [0323:C0010](../foundations/research-C0010.md).** Let R=2^b>=4 and let V=Mat2(F_R) viewed as a 4b-dimensional F2-space. Let N=SL2(R)×SL2(R) act by left and right multiplication. Give V the alternating form B(x,y)=Tr_F_R/F2(det(x+y)-det x-det y). Suppose the actual full multiplication group M is a subgroup of Sp(V,B) acting naturally on the entire set of minus-type quadratic refinements of B, and its actual canonical G normalizes and contains N. Retain the canonical identity G=HG' at 030ebb1d681efd8b12139efc7d0e3d4bdc9173ce. Then this cannot be the action of a finite simple nonassociative Bol loop. The domain is the entire set of minus refinements, with its specified natural symplectic action. No claim that all source-family embeddings have been identified is made. The normal subgroup N acts faithfully because the scalar centers of the two SL2(R) factors are trivial.

Put Q0(x)=Tr(det x), a quadratic refinement of B. The determinant polar form is nondegenerate over F_R, and nondegeneracy of the trace pairing makes B nondegenerate over F2. The subspace of matrices with second row zero is a totally Q0-singular half-dimensional F2-space, also directly showing that Q0 is plus type. The number of singular 2×2 matrices over F_R is R^3+R^2-R, while every nonzero determinant fiber has |SL2(R)|=R(R^2-1) elements. Therefore the number of zeros of Q0 is

 R^3+R^2-R+(R/2-1)R(R^2-1) = R^2(R^2+1)/2.

Thus Q0 is plus type. Every quadratic refinement is uniquely Q_v(x)=Q0(x)+B(v,x), v in V, and its Arf invariant differs from that of Q0 by Q0(v). Indeed Q_v(x)=Q0(x+v)+Q0(v), so the signed quadratic sum changes by the factor (-1)^Q0(v). The N-invariance of Q0 and B identifies its action on these labels with its original linear action on v. Consequently the minus refinements correspond exactly to the matrices v with Tr(det v)=1. These are invertible. N preserves determinant, and the left SL2(R) factor is transitive on the matrices of each fixed nonzero determinant: if det v=det w, then wv^-1 lies in SL2(R). Hence there are exactly R/2 N-orbits on the minus forms, one for each c with Tr(c)=1, each of size R(R^2-1). Since R>=4, N is intransitive.

## The symplectic normalizer

The normalizer quotient N_Sp(V,B)(N)/N is abelian, in fact contained in C_b×C2. To see this, the F2-algebra generated by each SL2(R) contains R-scalars: for g_t=[[0,1],[1,t]] one has g_t+g_t^-1=tI. The elementary root matrices then generate the full Mat2(R) factor algebra, and together the two factors generate End_R(V)=Mat4(R). Thus their commutant in End_F2(V) is precisely R. A symplectic normalizer of N preserves this commutant and induces a field automorphism. Remove the corresponding Frobenius map. Its induced automorphism of N either preserves or swaps its two simple factors; remove the tensor-factor swap if needed. The resulting R-linear map normalizes each factor algebra. An R-algebra automorphism of Mat2(R) is inner: a basis adapted to its two diagonal matrix units and the off-diagonal matrix units realizes it by a matrix in GL2(R). Applying this first to one factor algebra and then using its full commuting second factor shows that the resulting map is A tensor B for A,B in GL2(R), up to the harmless tensor scalar identification. Its multiplier on the R-valued tensor polar form is det(A)det(B). Preservation of the F2 trace form forces that multiplier to be 1, by nondegeneracy of the trace pairing. In characteristic two, rescale the two factors inversely using a square root of det(A), obtaining representatives in SL2(R)×SL2(R). The Frobenius and factor swap commute and normalize N, establishing the claimed abelian quotient.

If instead the given invariant alternating form is Tr(c times the R-valued tensor polar form), c in R*, a scalar change of variables with square equal to c identifies it with B, so the same conclusion holds. For the stated actual G, G/N is abelian and hence G'<=N. The [original G=HG′ identity](sp8-a5-square-minus-action.md), independently reviewed there at `b8d09f1800271592c97d9e707dc08535994b22ce` and originally at `030ebb1d681efd8b12139efc7d0e3d4bdc9173ce`, gives G=HN, so N must be transitive on M/I. This contradicts the determinant orbit decomposition. No quotient folder or loop census is used.
