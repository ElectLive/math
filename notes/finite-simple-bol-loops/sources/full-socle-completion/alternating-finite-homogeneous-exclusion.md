## The k=2 branch

If I is 2-homogeneous but not 2-transitive, Kantor's 1972 Theorem 1(i), with its degree hypotheses, makes I a solvable subgroup of a one-dimensional semilinear affine group. Thus the separately reviewed solvable-I theorem applies. This is distinct from Kantor's 1985 2-transitive roster used below. Hence assume I 2-transitive and nonsolvable. I is maximal and core-free in the primitive M-action on Q.

If M=A_m, the parity condition in (S_(m−2)×S₂)∩A_m makes C_G(T)=1 for T=A_(m−2). T is characteristic (its centralizer in this product is trivial, and G/T embeds in C2). Consequently G is an almost-simple group with socle T, excluded by the reviewed all-alternating canonical result b03fdd414954bf1fc12186e13c8e3da404e87f95 (with the A6 exceptional automorphism case covered by the reviewed all-PSL₂(9) overgroup result). This includes m=7,8. If M=S_m, actual maximality and core-freeness of I imply I is not contained in A_m: otherwise I=A_m, contradicting core-freeness. Thus I contains an odd natural permutation.

For m≥9, T=A_(m−2) is characteristic in G and |C_G(T)|≤2. On the original formal mark coset, each restriction fiber to Aut(T)=S_(m−2) has size at most 2. Thus

 |Q|≤2j(S_(m−2)),       |Q|=m!/|I|,

where j counts elements of order ≤2. This inequality keeps the actual outside marks and needs no quotient-folder inference.

For the finite roster use Kantor 1985, “Homogeneous designs and geometric lattices”, §2 pp.67–68, https://gladstone.uoregon.edu/~kantor/PAPERS/homogeneousdes.pdf. The primary list includes almost-simple projective, unitary, Suzuki, Ree, symplectic and sporadic actions, and the affine alternative. In the affine case it is enough to bound I by the entire AGL_e(p) at m=p^e, so no classification of affine complements is used. The standard-library certificate [k=2 arithmetic verifier](../code/verify_alternating_k2.py) explicitly constructs full automorphism overbounds for the listed actions of degrees 9–53 and checks, using exact integers, m!>4U_m j(S_(m−2)) except for m=9,10,11,12. It explicitly retains the degree-28 small-Ree q=3 action PΓL₂(8) of order 1512: this is a distinct action, whose order bound happens to be dominated by the degree-28 Sp₆(2) bound. At degree 15 the A7 exceptional action is overbounded by 5040; Kantor states the two degree-15 representations are exchanged by an outer automorphism. At degree 12 the M12 outer involution exchanges the two actions, so 95040 is the valid full action-normalizer bound, not 190080. The associated primary-source and all exception/normalizer identifications must receive independent audit; the arithmetic certificate by itself does not establish roster completeness.

At m=9, PSL₂(8):3 is contained in A9 (the simple part and order-three outer part are even), while the affine case is bounded by |AGL₂(3)|=432. Thus |Q|≥9!/432=840>2j(S7)=464. At m=10, the full PΓL₂(9) bound is 1440, so |Q|≥10!/1440=2520>2j(S8)=1528. At m=11, M11 is even, and PSL₂(11) is even in either degree-11 action; Kantor's outer-interchange statement prevents its outer involution normalizing either action. The remaining affine case is solvable. At m=12, M12 and M11 are even in their respective degree-12 actions; M12's outer involution exchanges its two actions. The remaining PSL₂(11)-type normalizer has order at most 1320, so |Q|≥12!/1320=362880>2j(S10)=18992. The m=11,12 parity claims concern actual action normalizers, not just the simple socles.

At m=8, the affine AGL₃(2) is even: translations are four disjoint transpositions and its linear part GL₃(2) is simple. The remaining nonsolvable proper 2-transitive degree-8 case has order at most 336, so |Q|≥8!/336=120. If C_G(A6)=1 the reviewed all-PSL₂(9) canonical theorem excludes G; otherwise G=A6×C₂ or S6×C₂. Reviewed source 01e1be0bc7331170f94a3b1ef86abac077c81336 gives at most 72 original marks for those central extensions, contradiction. At m=7, the only nonsolvable proper 2-transitive possibility PSL₃(2) is simple and hence even, so odd I must be solvable, already handled. This completes the conditional proof; it does not claim a classification outside this particular k=2 actual-factor orientation.

## The k=3 branch

Sources: Kantor, *k-Homogeneous Groups*, 1972, Theorem 1 (n≥2k), https://darkwing.uoregon.edu/~kantor/PAPERS/k-Homogeneous.pdf, gives non-3-transitive projective groups PSL2(q)≤I≤PΓL2(q) for q≡3 mod 4 and the solvable affine exceptions; these projective cases must not be omitted. Jones, *Primitive permutation groups containing a cycle*, 2014, §2 printed p.161, https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/BBF3021D8434AD2C0BB0D3D6CE637008/S000497271300049Xa.pdf/primitive-permutation-groups-containing-a-cycle.pdf , explicitly lists all 3-transitive groups not containing A_m: natural projective semilinear groups, subgroups of AGL_e(2), and M11 in degrees 11,12; M12, M22, Aut(M22), M23, M24 in their stated degrees. This list imports CFSG. The two sources are classification inputs, not reproved here. M22.2 order is 887040; AGL order and PΓL2(q) upper order are given in the verifier.

For d=m−3≥7, T=A_d is characteristic in G as G^(∞), and C_G(T)≤S3 has order at most 6. An original selected outside mark acts on T by an involution of Aut(T)=S_d, and each image has at most |C_G(T)|≤6 actual marked lifts, so n=|M:I|=|Λ|≤6 i(d), i(d) the total involutions including identity. Since M≥A_m, n≥m!/(2|I|). The exact script [k=3 arithmetic verifier](../code/verify_alternating_k3.py) checks all classified upper groups for 10≤m≤53, after allowing all possible subgroups and overestimating n capacity. The only coarse survivors are (m=10, projective), (m=11, M11), (m=12, M11 or M12). For d≥49, apply the reviewed bounded-component theorem (the script covers through d=50 independently); m=54..∞ follows there. Solvable affine exceptions are covered by the separately cited input.

At m=10, PΓL2(9) has order 1440 and contains an odd permutation: Frobenius x↦x³ fixes P¹(F3) and exchanges the six remaining points in three pairs. If M=A10 then |I|≤720, and if M=S10 then |I|≤1440. Both cases give n≥2520>6 i(7)=1392. Thus this coarse survivor disappears.

At m=11 or 12 the only residual actual I is M11 or M12 in the stated action; simple I lies in A_m, and maximality in M rules out M=S_m. For M=A_m the possible n values are 2520 (M11 at 11, M12 at 12) and 30240 (M11 at 12). T=A8 or A9 has |T|_2=64 in both cases, whereas n_2 is 8 or 32. Since [T:T∩H] divides [G:H]=n, H∩T has even order. Here C_G(T)≤A3 has order at most 3. The pinned original-mark class-square lemma implies that if any projected selected involution has at least floor(d/4)=2 transpositions, every T-involution occurs in actual Λ², contradicting the even H∩T. Otherwise every projected mark is identity or a transposition, giving n≤3(1+binom(d,2))≤111, contrary to n≥2520. This relies on the actual-mark lemma, not merely on a projected class-square calculation.

At m=9, nonsolvable 3-homogeneous I is contained in PΓL2(8), and maximality and evenness of that group give I=PΓL2(8), M=A9, n=120. The special abstract group T=A6 is characteristic in G; C=C_G(T)≤A3. If C=1 then G embeds into Aut(A6), so the reviewed full canonical PSL2(9) exclusion applies. If C=A3 and the projection to S6 is A6, then G=A6×C3. The reviewed center-fixing property makes τ fix C3; every τ-inverted element has trivial C3-coordinate, contradicting generation. If the projection is S6, then G=(S6×S3)∩A9. The exact GAP 4.15.1 source [A9 parity-group verifier](../code/verify_a9_k3_parity_group.g), authored by audit_c16_full, checks full Aut(G), its inner image and every noninner coset: outer involution G-class sizes are [3,15,15,135], [36], [108]; no union has size 120. ## The k=4 and k=5 branches

Kantor, *k-Homogeneous Groups*, Math. Z. 124 (1972), Theorem 1(iv), printed p.261, https://darkwing.uoregon.edu/~kantor/PAPERS/k-Homogeneous.pdf, classifies every non-k-transitive exception for m≥2k. For k=4 these are PSL2(8), PΓL2(8), PΓL2(32), of natural degrees 9,9,33; there are no nontransitive exceptions for k=5. PSL2(8) is not maximal in A9 because it lies properly in PΓL2(8), and the latter lies in A9. The 4-transitive list consists of A_m,S_m and the natural M11,M12,M23,M24; the 5-transitive list consists of A_m,S_m and M12,M24. The four Mathieu groups are simple and therefore even, while PΓL2(8) and PΓL2(32) have a simple even socle and odd-order outer quotient, so all these exceptional I lie in A_m. Maximality rules out M=S_m. Thus M=A_m in every remaining case.

Write T=A_d with d=m−k. For all surviving degrees d≥5, T is characteristic in G: an automorphic image either equals T or injects into G/T≤C2×S_k, and |A5|>2·4! in the only d=5 case, while |A7|>2·5! for d≥7. Also C=C_G(T)≤A_k and every mark in the actual outside set Λ has a restriction to Aut(T)=S_d, with fibers at most |C|. No quotient is assumed to be a loop folder.

For the large Mathieu and degree-33 cases, n=|A_m:I| exceeds k!·j(S_d), where j includes the identity. Exact pairs (n, k!·j(S_d)) are:

* (m,k)=(23,4): (1267136462592000,115432834560).
* (24,4): (1267136462592000,570207938304).
* (24,5): (1267136462592000,577164172800).
* (33,4): (26525285981219105863630848000000,2447765430112957440).

Here j(S_d) follows from j₀=j₁=1 and j_d=j_(d−1)+(d−1)j_(d−2). Thus the original mark-fiber injection rules out all four.

The other cases are (m,k,d,n)=(9,4,5,120),(11,4,7,2520),(12,4,8,2520),(12,5,7,2520). In (12,4), |A8|₂=64>8=n₂. Since [T:H∩T] divides n, H∩T has even order. Lemma 3.2 forces every selected involution label in S8 to have support below two transpositions; otherwise its class square captures every involution of T. Thus only identity and transposition labels occur, giving n≤4!·(1+28)=696, contradiction.

For the remaining three small cases use the exact finite block normalizer. If d∈{5,7}, restriction to S_d gives at most 26 or 232 involutions respectively; if the image of G is A_d, only one parity coset can occur, whose counts are (16,10) for d=5 and (106,126) for d=7. The centralizer C lies in A4 for k=4 and A5 for k=5. Its order, normality in G and the lower fiber bound leave only the possibilities below. All used automorphism identifications follow by restricting to characteristic commuting factors, noting their centralizer in G is trivial, and observing that every element of the indicated symmetric product normalizes the parity kernel.

* (11,4): 2520≤232|C| forces C=A4. If the S7 projection were A7, the fixed parity of the original mark coset would give n≤12·126=1512. Thus the projection is S7. Then G=(S7×S4)∩A11 and Aut(G)=S7×S4. An inner τ is already forbidden. The unique noninner ambient coset consists of opposite-parity pairs, and has only 106·6+126·4=1140 involutions, fewer than n.
* (12,5): 2520≤232|C|, and the subgroup list of A5 gives C=A4 or A5. If C=A4, it fixes a unique point on the 5-block, so G reduces to the previous full S7×S4 parity kernel and its 1140 bound. If C=A5 and the S7 projection is A7, then G=A7×A5, Aut(G)=S7×S5, and the three noninner cosets contain respectively 126·16=2016, 106·10=1060, or 126·10=1260 involutions. If the S7 projection is S7, G=(S7×S5)∩A12. Its outside involution classes have sizes 10,1050,21,315,105,1575; no subset sums to2520. Each full symmetric-product class is one G-class because an outside involution itself supplies an outside centralizer element. Since Λ is G-conjugacy invariant, no selection is possible.
* (9,4): 120≤26|C| forces C=A4. If the S5 projection is A5, G=A5×A4, Aut(G)=S5×S4, and its three noninner involution cosets have sizes 96,40,60. If the projection is S5, G=(S5×S4)∩A9. Its outside involution classes have sizes 6,90,10,30, whose only subset summing to120 is 90+30. Thus Λ contains the class of pairs (a,b) with a a double transposition in S5 and b a transposition in S4. The square of the double-transposition class in A5 is all A5; taking the same b twice shows A5×1⊆Λ². Consequently H∩A5=1. Since |H|=12, its projection to S4 is an order-12 subgroup, necessarily A4. An element of order three in H projects to a 3-cycle in S4 and to either identity or a 3-cycle in S5. Both occur as products of two double transpositions, and every S4 3-cycle is a product of two transpositions. Hence that H element is in Λ², a contradiction.

In the last case, the A5 class-square claim follows directly by counting cycle types, or by CTblLib/GAP: for the nonidentity involution class, coefficients on the five A5 classes are [15,2,3,5,5] in the order identity, involution, 3-cycle, 5-cycle, 5-cycle. ## Boundary m<2k, m≥7

For k=4 the only boundary degree is m=7, and G≤S3×S4 is solvable. For k=5 the boundary degrees are m=7,8,9, and G≤S_(m−5)×S5. If G is solvable, then either I is solvable, in which case the reviewed solvable-stabilizer reduction at `030ebb1d681efd8b12139efc7d0e3d4bdc9173ce` forces G≥Soc(M), impossible for solvable G; or I is nonsolvable, in which case the reviewed solvable-G alternating-full-action theorem at `4922123613711b1884764a672d0091afabd5d991` forces the natural action, whose loop degree m≤9 contradicts the reviewed minimum order 24. These uses retain the exact hypotheses and inputs of both reviewed results.

If G is nonsolvable in the k=5 boundary, its projection into S5 is nonsolvable. Every proper subgroup of S5 not containing A5 is solvable, so the projection contains A5. The perfect residual G^(∞) projects trivially into the solvable S_(m−5), and projects onto A5 in S5. Thus G^(∞)=1×A5, and G contains A5 on that five-point block. Swapping blocks gives the same actual factor in S5×S_(m−5) with the large alternating factor A5 and new k′=m−5, namely k′=2,3,4 for m=7,8,9. I is also k′-homogeneous because complements identify k-sets and (m−k)-sets. The (m,k′)=(9,4) case is excluded above; the two other cases reduce to the k′=2,3 branches and are excluded in the preceding sections.

Together with the k=2 and k=3 proofs above, this excludes every case in the finite residual from the pinned full-action reduction. Combining that reduction with its already reviewed high-degree and exceptional-clause proofs establishes the proposed all-degree natural-action conclusion under the displayed inputs. Minimum order24 then gives m=|Q|≥24. Natural-action operations, Lie-type full socles and unrestricted C0010 remain open.
