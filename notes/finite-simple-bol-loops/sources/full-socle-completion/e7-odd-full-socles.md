# Odd E7 full multiplication socle: proposed completion

Continuation of [0323:C0010](../foundations/research-C0010.md), with independent exact-source review recorded below. **Proposed theorem:** no finite simple non-Moufang left Bol loop has full multiplication socle E7(q) for odd q, under the explicit inputs below. Let M=Mlt(Q), with socle S=E7(q), q=p^f odd, and G=LMlt(Q), H=G₁. Assume the reviewed canonical inversion, selected marks and inner-inversion interfaces; the KMP exceptional-factor classification; the reviewed pure diagonal E7=S result; the cited standard rational group/outer-automorphism and field-descent facts; and the t1-avoidance bound |U|₂≤128(q²−1)₂ for any U≤S avoiding t1. This note is a proposed proof, not an independent review of those inputs or of this assembly.

KMP2015 Lemma11, printed pp56–57, https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/38522, excludes every factorization of an almost-simple group with E7 socle by two proper core-free factors. As M=GI, S is transitive and I is corefree, so S≤G. Then S is characteristic in G and restriction Aut(G) to Aut(S) is injective: an automorphism fixing S pointwise sends each g to an element differing from g by C_G(S)=1. The outer group is D×F=C2×Cf; see Burness–Guralnick–Harper arXiv:2006.01421v2 Table2. Since S is perfect and Out(S) abelian, G′=S. Conjugation by any automorphism of S is trivial on Out(S), while canonical inversion acts as inversion on G/G′. Hence A=G/S≤Out(S)[2]. Every involutory automorphism of G with outer label in A is inner: match its restriction using an element of G and use injectivity. The reviewed inner-inversion obstruction therefore requires the label of τ outside A.

For odd f, the only nontrivial involution label is diagonal d; if G>S, it lies in A and τ is inner. For even f, put q=s² and write d and σ for the diagonal and order-two field labels. Burness–Guralnick–Harper v2 Example3.22, Lemmas3.20 and6.2, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.01421v2, imply that no involutions exist in S dσ: Shintani sends that coset to the nontrivial diagonal coset in Inndiag(E7(s)), while an order-two lift maps to the identity. The case A=<d,σ> has no outside involution label, and A=<d> or <σ> makes the actual selected outside marks lie in one single S-coset because the alternative is the empty dσ coset; since t∈Λ, K=tΛ⊂S contradicts <K>=G. This leaves A=1 or <dσ>. For A=1, diagonal τ is excluded by the reviewed pure-diagonal canonical theorem; field τ is handled next.

The following direct proof also establishes the empty mixed coset used above. The field involutions in Sσ form exactly two S-classes, fused by a diagonal automorphism, and there are no involutions in Sdσ. This follows directly from Lang. Put D_q=E7,adj(q), S_q=im(E7,sc(q)→D_q), and σ=F_s, q=s². For x∈D_s, choose an algebraic simply connected lift x̂. Since x is F_s-fixed, F_s(x̂)=z x̂ with z in the central kernel C2; F_s(z)=z, so F_s²(x̂)=x̂. Thus D_s≤S_q, and C_{S_q}(σ)=C_{D_q}(σ)=D_s. If xσ is an involution in D_q⋊⟨σ⟩, Lang for connected adjoint E7 writes x=y⁻¹σ(y); the square condition implies σ²(y)=y, so y∈D_q. Since σ acts trivially on D_q/S_q=C2, x∈S_q. Hence Sdσ has no involutions, and all involutions in D_qσ constitute one D_q-class. Its centralizer D_s lies in S_q, so it splits into exactly two S_q-classes, each of size |S|/c with c=|E7,adj(s)|=|E7,sc(s)|, interchanged by d. The equality of finite fixed-point orders follows from the central-isogeny exact sequence and Lang: for a finite central kernel, F−1 has kernel and cokernel of equal size. BGH v2 Example3.22/Lemma6.2 independently corroborate the field-label description.

Each of the two field classes has square containing the full t1=A1D6 involution class of S. In E7,sc(s), a simple-coroot involution h_alpha(-1) maps to t1; denote it by û. This is the reviewed Cartan-mod-2 image calculation at dac5d3d09a126cf9805ae6305a51d0cdda17f02c. Pierro arXiv:2003.12595v2 section4.1 and Table6 p.8 corroborates this lift/fusion; the proof retains the reviewed Cartan calculation directly. Lang in the simply connected algebraic group gives g⁻¹σ(g)=û, and σ²(g)=g because ûσ(û)=1. Thus g∈E7,sc(q), and σû is S-conjugate to σ. Projecting, σ(σu)=u∈(σ^S)²; S-invariance gives the whole t1 class. The second field class is diagonal-conjugate to the first and t1 is Aut(S)-stable. No quotient folder is invoked.

Set P=(q²−1)₂=2(s²−1)₂≥16. The E7 degrees 2,6,8,10,12,14,18 and LTE give |S|₂=4P⁷ and c₂=P⁷/16. If G=S and τ is field, Λ is one or both field classes and |H|₂ is c₂ or c₂/2, at least P⁷/32. But any selected field class forces H to avoid t1, contrary to the t1-avoidance bound because P⁷/32>128P.

It remains A=<dσ>. The actual outside coset tG consists of the pure diagonal and pure field labels. Both must occur in Λ: otherwise tΛ⊂S and cannot generate G. Conjugation by dσ∈G fuses the two field S-classes, so both occur. The diagonal side contains one or both of its two S-classes.

Their rational centralizers have connected finite orders |SU8(q)| and (q+1)|²E6,sc(q)|. For both outside algebraic types, representatives in the rational −q torus are supplied by the independently reviewed Cartan image/63-orbit calculation at dac5d3d09a126cf9805ae6305a51d0cdda17f02c, the [pure-diagonal manuscript](e7-odd-diagonal-canonical.md). Restrict −q Frobenius to the connected centralizer and compose with the longest Weyl element of its root subsystem. Opposition is nontrivial for A7 and E6: the Weyl group acts trivially on P/Q=C8 or C3, while −I does not. Their diagram automorphism groups have order two, so the resulting Frobenius gives the unitary and twisted-E6 forms. In the T1E6 case the subsystem Weyl group fixes the connected central torus pointwise, and −q Frobenius has q+1 fixed points on it. Standard finite-form classification, Dudas-Michel2015 section7.4 pp.28-29, identifies the indicated finite groups; central isogenies preserve connected fixed-point orders by the preceding Lang argument. The algebraic centralizer component group is C2, Frobenius fixes it, and Lang makes the rational map onto C2 surjective. Each outside involution itself maps outside S, so intersecting its rational centralizer with S divides the full rational centralizer order by two. Thus c_A=|SU8(q)| and c_E=(q+1)|²E6,sc(q)| exactly, and each outside diagonal Inndiag class is a single S-class.

Using q≡1 mod8, both diagonal centralizer 2-parts are 64P⁴: their four even-degree factors contribute 8P⁴, and the three odd-degree/toroidal factors each have 2-part2. Hence each diagonal S-class size has 2-part P³/16≥256. Each field S-class has size 2-part |S|₂/c₂=64; both together have 2-part128. Adding one or both diagonal class sizes cannot change that 2-part, so |Λ|₂=128. Since |G|₂=8P⁷, |H|₂=P⁷/16 and |H∩S|₂≥P⁷/32>128P. Yet the field-class square forces H∩S to avoid t1, contradiction.

Therefore, conditional on the stated inputs and the already reviewed pure-diagonal case, there is no such full multiplication socle E7(q) for q odd. This combines genuinely actual factors and marks; it never constructs a quotient loop. The characteristic-two E7 family is already excluded by the [reviewed exceptional field-involution theorem](exceptional-field-involution-exclusions.md) at exact source `cd5bf6a31bc63eb838b3150e51dd7aaec57be838`. Combining that result with the present proposed odd-field proof excludes every E7(q) full multiplication socle under the retained inputs. Other exceptional socles and unrestricted C0010 remain outside this theorem. Independent review of this exact assembly and its rational-centralizer/field splitting derivations is recorded below.
