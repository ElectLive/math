## Statement

With C0008 and C0011 proved, C0017 accepted under CFSG and its scoped dependencies, and the remaining aggregate gaps in C0013 and C0016 still open, the structural reductions, constructive classifications, exact-order uniqueness theorems, and `LMlt` / `Mlt` exclusions proposed in `0323-finite-simple-non-moufang-bol-loops:C0001` through `0323-finite-simple-non-moufang-bol-loops:C0009` and `0323-finite-simple-non-moufang-bol-loops:C0011` through `0323-finite-simple-non-moufang-bol-loops:C0017`, the unrestricted classification problem `0323.CLASSIFY` and the permutation-rank subproblem `#0323/S1` suggest the following open obligations. These are not currently an exhaustive reduction; the unresolved prerequisites must also be settled:

1. **Unrestricted folder exhaustiveness (`0323.CLASSIFY`)**:
   - **Proved exclusion, with review scopes in C0011:** all prime-power orders $`|Q| = p^d`$ ($`\forall p \ge 2, d \ge 1`$) and all orders $`|Q| < 24`$ are empty, and $`Q_{24}`$ is unique at order $`24`$ (`C0011`, `100% CFSG-free`).
   - Every exact-factorization left Bol loop $`Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}`$ has imprimitive $`\operatorname{LMlt}(Q)`$ and is universally a $`G`$-loop (`C0012`), separating all exact-factorization families (`C0002`–`C0005`, plus the odd-order multi-module solvable-`LMlt` family $`Q_{p,q;R}`$ of order $`p^{kp+1}q`$ in `C0013`) from multi-isotope classes (`4` loops on $`N_{96}^{\mathrm{op}}`$ in `C0006`; `20` loops on $`Q_{1536}`$ in `C0012`).
   - **Remaining gap**: Classify all faithful simple proper left Bol folders $`(G, H, K)`$ satisfying `C0001` Theorem 2.2(3) beyond exact factorizations $`X = Y_0 Y_1`$ and `BSS11` simple Bruck loops of $`2`$-power exponent ($`G / O_2(G) \cong \prod_{i=1}^e \operatorname{PGL}_2(q_i)`$) together with their `C0006` shifted-folder isotopes — in particular classifying odd-order simple proper folders outside exact factorizations, whose existence is established by the [reviewed cross-coupled extraspecial construction](../odd-completion/odd-cross-extraspecial.md) (where `C0013` gives an affine-fiber argument but its proposed absorption of all single-factor module deformations remains unproved), and completing the conjugacy census of non-translation regular subgroups $`Y_0 \neq V`$ in `C0004` and wreath-socle factorizations in `C0005`.
   - *Resumable continuation blueprint*: Intrinsic characterization of exact-factorization folders via a normal complement $`N \trianglelefteq G`$ satisfying $`N \cap \tau(N) = \{1_G\}`$, $`[G : N] = |K|`$, and $`H N \cap H \tau(N) = H`$, together with $`p`$-radical imprimitivity ($`O_p(G) \neq \{1_G\} \implies \operatorname{LMlt}(Q)`$ imprimitive) and multi-module radical-algebra deformation tests over $`Q_{3,13;R}`$.

2. **Primitive and simple `LMlt(Q)` existence (`CMUC 2008` Problem 2)**:
   - **Proposed exclusions, conditional on the listed dependencies:** types **`HA`** ($`\forall p \ge 2, d \ge 1`$), **`SD_2`**, and **`HC` Case B** are completely eliminated `CFSG`-free (`C0014`), and **`HS` Case B** is completely eliminated under `CFSG` (`C0015`).
   - Under CFSG and the explicit folder/inversion and external-source inputs, [the reviewed all-socle assembly](../primitive-completion/sd-all-simple-socles.md) excludes **`SD_k` for every k≥3**. This is a conditional manuscript-local result with its own review chain, not a status promotion of C0014/C0015.
   - **Remaining O'Nan–Scott regimes**:
     * **`AS`**: Almost-simple $`G`$ outside the infinite families excluded in `C0007` ($`\operatorname{PSL}_2(q), \operatorname{PGL}_2(q), \operatorname{PSL}_3(p), \operatorname{PSU}_3(p), \operatorname{PSU}_3(2^{2^s}), A_m, S_m`$) and outside degrees $`\le 120`$ / $`|G| \le 190{,}080`$ (`test_wave2_census.py`);
     * **`HS` Case A ($`\tau(T_i) = T_i`$)**: Multi-involution-class Lie-type groups surviving `C0014` Statement 3(b) (`|C_T(\alpha_1)||C_T(\alpha_2)| >= |T|` and $`\mathcal{C}(\alpha_1) \cap \mathcal{C}(\alpha_2) = \{1_T\}`$; note that short-root involutions in $`\operatorname{PSp}_{2m}(q)`$ and anti-diagonal graph involutions in $`\operatorname{PSL}_{2m}^\varepsilon(q)`$ require orbit-counting $`|T| \le \sum |(\alpha_1, \alpha_2)^T|`$ rather than single-class $`Z^*`$-descent);
     * **`HC` Case A** without a shared transposed factor pair (`C0015`), excluding the reviewed prime-factor Out(T)=1 cases linked below, **`PA`** with component degree $`d = [T : R] \ge |T|^{1/2}`$ and $`R \cap \mathcal{I}_0 = \emptyset`$ (`C0014`, `C0015`), **`CD`**, and **`TW`** (where $`B \cong T^k`$ acts regularly on $`Q`$, reducing to a twisted Bol operation on $`T^k`$).

3. **Full `#0323/S1` frontier after the conditional rank-three exclusion**:
   - Under `CFSG` and the reviewed C0017 assembly, every rank-$`3`$ counterexample is eliminated (`C0017`: $`\operatorname{rank}(\operatorname{Mlt}(Q)) = 3`$ for simple nonassociative left Bol $`Q \iff Q \cong M(2)`$ or $`M(3)`$, both Moufang), and every prime-power order $`|Q| = p^a`$ has $`\operatorname{rank}(\operatorname{Mlt}(Q)) = 2`$ (`C0017`, vacuous for simple left Bol loops by `C0011`).
   - **Residual under those assumptions:** determine whether any finite simple proper left Bol loop satisfies $`\operatorname{rank}(\operatorname{Mlt}(Q)) \ge 4`$ in an `AS` action surviving `C0009` and `C0016` — including nondegenerate-subspace actions not covered by the existing Grassmannian/polar-space arguments, higher-rank exceptional Lie-type coset actions, and subspace-free `AS` actions of rank $`\ge 4`$ and degree $`>50`$. The reviewed natural-action continuations now exclude proper loops on even-dimensional orthogonal nonsingular points for all q≥4 (only the eight-dimensional plus-type Paige action can support a loop), unitary nonsingular points for all q≥3 and dimensions at least three, and symplectic minus refinements for q≥4 under CFSG. Their exact ambient groups and extension-interface qualifications are linked below; these are not a classification of all actions of those socles.
