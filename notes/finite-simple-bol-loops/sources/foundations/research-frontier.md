# Open problems and related results

## Established equivalence classes

- [Bruck isotopy and canonical stabilizer conjugacy](../factor-permutation/exact-factorization-criterion.md#bruck-isotopy-as-a-stabilizer-conjugacy-test), for finite simple nonassociative left Bol loops under the cited canonical-folder inputs: two scoped independent acceptances at `ea836a5f328d6bc52913aac8fd1841a87072f5f8`. The former exact-factorization exhaustiveness proposal for the complementary class is refuted by the [cross-coupled extraspecial construction](../odd-completion/odd-cross-extraspecial.md), with three scoped independent acceptances at `62978672415973eb0f8043c6976d06b317a52d42`. General exhaustiveness remains open.

- **Faithful left Bol folders and left Bol loops up to isomorphism ([C0001.md](../../claims/0001.md))**: Restriction $`\Phi \mapsto \Phi|_K`$ is an exact bijection between folder isomorphisms of faithful left Bol folders $`(G, H, K)`$ ($`G = \langle K \rangle`$, $`\operatorname{Core}_G(H) = 1`$) and loop isomorphisms of left Bol loops, under which properness corresponds to $`\exists x, y \in K`$ with $`|K \cap x y H \cap H x y| \neq 1`$, and simplicity ($`|Q| \ge 2`$) corresponds both to primitivity of $`\operatorname{Mlt}(Q)`$ and to the absence of intermediate subgroups $`U = H N`$ ($`1 \neq N = \operatorname{Core}_G(U) \trianglelefteq G`$, $`U \neq G`$) satisfying $`|K N / N| = [G : H N]`$. Left/right Bol conventions likewise correspond under passage to opposite loops (`x *_op y = y * x`) on the identical folder triple $`(G, H, K)`$ when $`K^{-1} = K`$; see [the canonical scope](problem-contract.md#model-and-scope) and [N07 §1](references.md#n07).
- **Triviality of nontrivial loop conjugacy classes and $`2`$-transitivity of $`\operatorname{Mlt}(Q)`$ ([REFERENCES.md](references.md#js-simple-bol-permutation-rank))**: For any finite loop $`Q`$ with identity $`1`$, the loop-character conjugacy classes on $`Q`$ coincide with the orbits of $`\operatorname{Inn}(Q) = \operatorname{Mlt}(Q)_1`$, so $`Q \setminus \{1\}`$ is a single loop conjugacy class iff $`\operatorname{rank}(\operatorname{Mlt}(Q)) = 2`$.

## One-way implications

- **Primitivity of $`\operatorname{LMlt}(Q) \implies`$ loop-simplicity, and imprimitivity / $`G`$-loop property of exact factorizations ([C0012.md](../../claims/0012.md), [C0014.md](../../claims/0014.md))**: Any loop ($`|Q| \ge 2`$) with primitive $`\operatorname{LMlt}(Q)`$ is loop-simple; conversely, every exact-factorization left Bol loop $`Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}`$ ($`|Y_0|, |Y_1| \ge 2`$) has imprimitive $`\operatorname{LMlt}(Q)`$ and is a $`G`$-loop, so no loop whose isotopy class splits into $`\ge 2`$ isomorphism classes (such as the $`4`$ isotopes of $`N_{96}^{\mathrm{op}}`$ in [C0006.md](../../claims/0006.md) or the $`20`$ isotopes of $`Q_{1536}`$ in [C0012.md](../../claims/0012.md)) arises from an exact factorization.
- **Universal `LMlt` inversion and nuclei collapse ([C0001.md](../../claims/0001.md), [C0007.md](research-C0007.md))**: Every simple proper left Bol loop satisfies $`N_\lambda(Q) = N_\mu(Q) = \{1\}`$, $`|N_\rho(Q)| \le |Q|/3`$, and admits a unique involutory $`\tau \in \operatorname{Aut}(\operatorname{LMlt}(Q))`$ inverting $`K`$ with $`H \cap (\tau K)^2 = \{1\}`$.
- A full solution of [0323.CLASSIFY](problem-contract.md#0323classify) would classify every restricted subclass (`Bruck`, `exponent 2`, `G-loops`, `solvable LMlt`), whereas classifications of restricted subclasses are milestones rather than solutions of `0323.CLASSIFY`.

- **Almost-Simple reduction for $`\operatorname{Mlt}(Q)`$ ([C0008.md](../../claims/0008.md))**: The proved reduction states that every finite simple nonassociative left Bol loop $`Q`$ satisfies $`T \trianglelefteq \operatorname{Mlt}(Q) \le \operatorname{Aut}(T)`$ for a unique nonabelian finite simple group $`T`$ (`AS` primitive type).
- **Universal prime-power order nonexistence and minimum order $`24`$ ([C0011.md](../../claims/0011.md))**: The proved claim states that every finite simple nonassociative left Bol loop has non-prime-power order $`|Q| \ge 24`$, with $`Q_{24}`$ unique at $`|Q| = 24`$ (`CFSG`-free).
