# Classification of Finite Simple Non-Moufang Bol Loops

<!-- problem-id: 0323 -->

Stable problem ID **0323**; source key **ALGEBRA.SIMPLE_PROPER_BOL**. Added at Mira's request, 2026-11-13.

## Statement

Classify, up to loop isomorphism, every finite simple **left Bol loop that is not Moufang**, with no restriction on order or exponent.

A loop is a set with a binary operation, identity 1, and unique solutions to ax=b and ya=b. The left Bol identity is

```math
x\cdot(y\cdot(x\cdot z))=(x\cdot(y\cdot x))\cdot z
\qquad(x,y,z\in L).
```

Here simple means that the only normal subloops (kernels of loop homomorphisms) are {1} and L. It does not mean that there are no proper subloops. Non-Moufang means that the loop fails the Moufang identity

```math
(x\cdot y)\cdot(z\cdot x)=(x\cdot(y\cdot z))\cdot x
```

for at least one triple. Literature often calls such Bol loops **proper**.

## Goals

### 0323.CLASSIFY

Give explicit structural construction data and an equivalence relation on those data, with proofs of:
1. **Soundness:** each admissible datum constructs a finite simple proper left Bol loop.
2. **Exhaustiveness:** every loop in the target class arises.
3. **Isomorphism criterion:** two constructed loops are isomorphic exactly when their data are equivalent.

The data must offer structural information beyond a disguised multiplication table or the instruction to enumerate Latin squares and test the identities. A parameterized family or a proved structural reduction with a solved residual classification is acceptable; a finite list is not assumed. The treatment must cover all admissible parameters and state how to recognize their admissibility.

### 0323.KNOWN_FAMILIES

Reconstruct known families, their overlaps and isomorphism criteria, with exact hypotheses. Separate a construction of examples from an exhaustive classification. Reproduce at least one proper simple finite example as a check of conventions.

### 0323.STRUCTURAL_REDUCTION

Find a useful exhaustiveness theorem via Bol folders, multiplication groups, or another invariant. State every residual classification obligation explicitly. A classification under an extra hypothesis is a milestone, not the unrestricted endpoint.

## Model and scope

Use left Bol throughout. The opposite operation x*y=y·x converts right Bol loops to left Bol loops and back. Thus right-sided literature can be transported; this does not identify a loop with its opposite within our isomorphism classification. Isotopy is a different, coarser classification task and is not the requested equivalence.

Include all finite orders and exponents. Groups and nonassociative Moufang loops are excluded by the non-Moufang requirement. Do not silently replace Bol by Bruck, exponent 2, odd order, or an automorphic subclass. Distinguish the left multiplication group, right multiplication group, and full multiplication group; simplicity of the loop is not an assertion that one of these groups is simple.
