### 1. Wreath-Socle Exact-Factorization Dichotomy and Infinite Simple Family on $`T \wr C_2`$

#### Theorem 1.1 (Wreath-Socle Split Failure vs. Transitive-Socle Simplicity)
Let $`T`$ be a finite nonabelian simple group, let $`P \le S_r`$ ($`r \ge 2`$) be a transitive permutation group, put $`B := T^r`$ and $`X := T \wr P = B \rtimes P`$, and let $`X = Y_0 Y_1`$ ($`Y_0 \cap Y_1 = 1`$, $`Y_0, Y_1 \neq 1`$, $`\mathop{\mathrm{Core}}\nolimits_X(Y_i) = 1`$) be a core-free exact factorization.
1. **Split-base failure**: If $`B = (B \cap Y_0)(B \cap Y_1)`$, then $`|B| = |B \cap Y_0| \cdot |B \cap Y_1|`$, so $`\bar{Y}_i := Y_i B / B`$ ($`i = 0, 1`$) satisfy $`|\bar{Y}_0| \cdot |\bar{Y}_1| = |X / B| = |P|`$ and $`\bar{X} := X / B = \bar{Y}_0 \bar{Y}_1`$ with $`\bar{Y}_0 \cap \bar{Y}_1 = 1`$. The quotient projection $`\pi : X \twoheadrightarrow \bar{X}`$ commutes with the unique $`(Y_0, Y_1)`$-factorization in $`Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}`$ (`C0002`), hence defines a surjective loop homomorphism $`Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)} \twoheadrightarrow Q_{(\bar{X}, \bar{Y}_0, \bar{Y}_1)}`$ whose kernel $`B = Q_{(B, B \cap Y_0, B \cap Y_1)}`$ ($`1 < |B| < |X|`$) is a **proper nontrivial normal subloop** (in particular, product-type factorizations $`Y_i = A_i^r \rtimes P_i`$ are never simple).
2. **Transitive-socle simplicity ($`\mathop{\mathrm{Soc}}\nolimits(\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q)) \cong T^{2r}`$, $`2r \ge 4`$)**: If $`B Y_0 = B Y_1 = X`$, then since $`B = B' \le X'`$ and $`C_X(B) = 1`$, we have $`X' Y_0 = X' Y_1 = X`$ and $`\mathop{\mathrm{Soc}}\nolimits(\hat{G}) = B \times B \cong T^{2r}`$ ($`2r \ge 4`$). By `C0002` Proposition 1.1, $`(\hat{G}, \hat{H}, S)`$ is faithful and $`Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}`$ is non-Moufang. Suppose $`P_U \trianglelefteq Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}`$ were a nontrivial proper normal subloop (`C0001` Theorem 2.2), with $`1 \neq N = \mathop{\mathrm{Core}}\nolimits_{\hat{G}}(U) \trianglelefteq \hat{G}`$. As $`\sigma(u, v) = (v, u) \in \mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(\hat{G})`$ satisfies $`\sigma(s_1^{-1} s_2) = s_1(s_1^{-1} s_2)^{-1} s_1^{-1}`$ ($`s_1, s_2 \in S`$), we have $`S \cap N = S \cap \sigma(N)`$, so $`N = \mathop{\mathrm{Core}}\nolimits_{\hat{G}}(U)`$ is $`\sigma`$-invariant. Because $`C_{\hat{G}}(B \times B) = 1`$, $`N \cap (B \times B) \neq 1`$; since $`T`$ is nonabelian simple, $`N \cap (B \times B) = \prod_{j \in I} T_j`$ for a nonempty subset $`I \subseteq \{1, \dots, 2r\}`$ (where $`T_1, \dots, T_r`$ are the factors of $`B \times 1`$ and $`T_{r+1}, \dots, T_{2r}`$ those of $`1 \times B`$). Conjugation by $`S = \{(x, x^{-1}) : x \in X\} \subseteq \hat{G}`$ acts via $`P`$ transitively on $`\{1, \dots, r\}`$ and on $`\{r + 1, \dots, 2r\}`$, while $`\sigma(N) = N`$ swaps the two halves, forcing $`I = \{1, \dots, 2r\}`$ and $`B \times B \le N`$. For any $`(u, v) \in \hat{G}`$, write $`u = b_0 y_0`$ ($`b_0 \in B, y_0 \in Y_0`$) and $`v = b_1 y_1`$ ($`b_1 \in B, y_1 \in Y_1`$); then $`y_0 y_1 \in B u B v \subseteq X'`$, so $`(y_0, y_1) \in \hat{H}`$ and $`(u, v) \in (B \times B)\hat{H} \le N \hat{H}`$, contradicting $`[\hat{G} : \hat{H} N] > 1`$. Hence **$`Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}`$ is a finite simple proper left Bol $`G`$-loop**.
3. **Explicit infinite realization on $`T \wr C_2`$**: Suppose $`T = A_0 A_1`$ ($`A_0 \cap A_1 = 1`$, $`A_0, A_1 \neq T`$) is an exact factorization of a finite nonabelian simple group $`T`$ in which $`A_0`$ admits a subgroup $`M_0 \trianglelefteq A_0`$ of index $`2`$, with quotient map $`\chi : A_0 \twoheadrightarrow \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}`$. In $`X = T \wr C_2 = (T \times T) \rtimes \langle t \rangle`$ ($`t(a, b)t^{-1} = (b, a)`$, $`t^2 = 1`$), put $`W := (A_0 \times A_0) \rtimes \langle t \rangle`$ ($`|W| = 2|A_0|^2`$). The map $`\Phi : W \to \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}`$, $`\Phi((a, b) t^\epsilon) := \chi(a) + \chi(b) + \epsilon \pmod 2`$, is a surjective group homomorphism (both for $`\epsilon_1 = 0`$ and for $`\epsilon_1 = 1`$, where $`t(a_2, b_2) = (b_2, a_2)t`$). Set $`Y_0 := \ker(\Phi) \le W`$ ($`|Y_0| = |A_0|^2`$) and $`Y_1 := (A_1 \times A_1) \rtimes \langle t \rangle`$ ($`|Y_1| = 2|A_1|^2`$). Since $`W \cap Y_1 = \langle t \rangle`$ and $`\Phi(t) = 1 \neq 0`$, we have $`Y_0 \cap Y_1 = 1`$ and $`|Y_0| \cdot |Y_1| = 2|T|^2 = |X|`$. Choosing $`a_0 \in A_0 \setminus M_0`$ gives $`(a_0, 1)t \in Y_0 \setminus B`$, so $`B Y_0 = B Y_1 = X`$, while $`\mathop{\mathrm{Core}}\nolimits_X(Y_i) \cap B \le \mathop{\mathrm{Core}}\nolimits_T(A_i)^2 = 1 \implies \mathop{\mathrm{Core}}\nolimits_X(Y_i) = 1`$. Two infinite simple subfamilies satisfy these hypotheses:
   - **$`T = \mathop{\mathrm{PSL}}\nolimits_2(q)`$ for all prime powers $`q \equiv 3 \pmod 4`$, $`q \ge 7`$**: $`A_1 = \mathbb{F}_q \rtimes C_{(q-1)/2}`$ (order $`\frac{q(q-1)}{2}`$, odd $`\frac{q-1}{2}`$) and $`A_0 \cong D_{q+1}`$ (even dihedral order $`q + 1 \equiv 0 \pmod 4`$, $`\gcd(q + 1, \frac{q(q-1)}{2}) = 1`$, with $`M_0 \cong C_{(q+1)/2}`$ of index $`2`$). At $`q = 7`$ ($`\mathop{\mathrm{PSL}}\nolimits_2(7) \wr C_2`$), this yields a simple proper left Bol $`G`$-loop of order $`56{,}448`$ with $`\mathop{\mathrm{Soc}}\nolimits(\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q)) \cong \mathop{\mathrm{PSL}}\nolimits_2(7)^4`$.
   - **$`T = A_{4k}`$ for all $`k \ge 2`$**: $`A_1 = A_{4k-1}`$ and $`A_0 \cong D_{4k} = \langle r, s \mid r^{2k} = s^2 = 1, s r s = r^{-1} \rangle`$ embedded in $`S_{4k}`$ via its regular Cayley action; $`r`$ acts as two $`2k`$-cycles (sign $`(-1)^{2(2k-1)} = +1`$) and $`s`$ acts as $`2k`$ transpositions (sign $`(-1)^{2k} = +1`$), so $`A_0 \le A_{4k}`$ acts regularly with $`A_0 \cap A_1 = 1`$ and $`M_0 = \langle r \rangle \cong C_{2k}`$ of index $`2`$. $`\square`$

### 2. Universal `LMlt` Evenness for Simple Exact-Factorization Loops

#### Theorem 2.1 (Universal `LMlt` Evenness)
For every finite simple exact-factorization left Bol loop $`Q = Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}`$ with $`|Q| = |X| > 2`$, $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q) \le A_{|Q|}`$.

*Proof*: In Nagy's folder $`(X \times X, Y_0 \times Y_1, S)`$, the map $`(a, b)(Y_0 \times Y_1) \mapsto (a Y_0, b Y_1)`$ is an $`(X \times X)`$-equivariant bijection onto $`\Omega_0 \times \Omega_1 := (X / Y_0) \times (X / Y_1)`$, where $`|\Omega_0| = [X : Y_0] = |Y_1|`$ and $`|\Omega_1| = [X : Y_1] = |Y_0|`$. Restricting to the reduced group $`\hat{G} = \{(u, v) \in X \times X : u v \in X'\} \cong \mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$ (`C0002` Proposition 1.1), $`(u, 1)`$ acts as $`|Y_0|`$ disjoint copies of left multiplication on $`\Omega_0`$ and $`(1, v)`$ acts as $`|Y_1|`$ disjoint copies of left multiplication on $`\Omega_1`$, so
$$\mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\nolimits_Q(u, v) = \mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\nolimits_{\Omega_0}(u)^{|Y_0|} \cdot \mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\nolimits_{\Omega_1}(v)^{|Y_1|} \qquad \forall (u, v) \in \hat{G}.$$
- If both $`|Y_0|`$ and $`|Y_1|`$ are even, $`\mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\nolimits_Q(u, v) = (+1)(+1) = +1`$.
- If both $`|Y_0|`$ and $`|Y_1|`$ are odd, $`|\hat{G}| = |X| \cdot |X'| \mid |X|^2`$ is odd, forcing $`\mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\nolimits_Q(\hat{G}) = \{+1\}`$.
- Suppose one factor — by symmetry $`Y_0`$ — has odd order and $`Y_1`$ has even order, so $`\mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\nolimits_Q(u, v) = \mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\nolimits_{\Omega_0}(u)`$. If $`\mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\nolimits_{\Omega_0} : X \to \{\pm 1\}`$ were surjective, its kernel $`X_+ := \ker(\mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\nolimits_{\Omega_0}) \trianglelefteq X`$ would have index $`2`$. Since $`|Y_0|`$ is odd, $`Y_0 \le X_+`$, and since $`X / X_+ \cong C_2`$ is abelian, $`X' \le X_+`$; thus $`X' Y_0 \le X_+ \lneq X`$ ($`|X_+| = |X|/2 > 1`$ as $`|X| > 2`$). If $`Y_0 \neq 1`$, `C0002` Proposition 1.1(3) gives a proper nontrivial normal subloop $`X' Y_0 \trianglelefteq Q`$; if $`Y_0 = 1`$, $`Q`$ is the group $`X`$ (or $`X^{\mathrm{op}}`$) with proper nontrivial normal subgroup $`X_+`$. Both contradict simplicity of $`Q`$.

Hence $`\mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\nolimits_Q(\hat{G}) = \{+1\}`$, i.e. $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q) \le A_{|Q|}`$. $`\square`$

### 3. Loop-Isotopy Invariance of `LMlt` and Strict Cross-Family Isotopy Separation

#### Theorem 3.1 (Isotopy Invariance and Pairwise Non-Isotopy Across All Five Families)
1. **Loop-isotopy invariance of $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q), \mathop{\mathrm{RMlt}}\nolimits(Q), \mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$**: Let $`(\alpha, \beta, \gamma) : (Q, *) \to (Q', \star)`$ be any loop isotopism ($`\alpha(x) \star \beta(y) = \gamma(x * y)`$). Defining $`u \odot v := \gamma^{-1}(\gamma(u) \star \gamma(v))`$ on $`Q`$ makes $`\gamma : (Q, \odot) \xrightarrow{\sim} (Q', \star)`$ a loop isomorphism (inducing permutation-group conjugacy via $`\gamma`$), while $`(\tilde{\alpha}, \tilde{\beta}, \mathrm{id}_Q) := (\gamma^{-1}\alpha, \gamma^{-1}\beta, \mathrm{id}_Q) : (Q, *) \to (Q, \odot)`$ satisfies $`\tilde{\alpha}(x) \odot \tilde{\beta}(y) = x * y`$. Setting $`y = 1_\odot`$ gives $`\tilde{\alpha} = R_c^{(*)}`$ ($`c = \tilde{\beta}(1_\odot)`$) and setting $`x = 1_\odot`$ gives $`\tilde{\beta} = L_d^{(*)}`$ ($`d = \tilde{\alpha}(1_\odot)`$), so $`u \odot v = R_c^{-1}(u) * L_d^{-1}(v)`$ and $`L_u^{(\odot)} = L_{R_c^{-1}(u)}^{(*)} (L_d^{(*)})^{-1}`$. Evaluating at $`u = 1_\odot`$ gives $`L_{R_c^{-1}(1_\odot)}^{(*)} = L_d^{(*)}`$, so $`\{L_u^{(\odot)} : u \in Q\} = \{L_x^{(*)} : x \in Q\} (L_d^{(*)})^{-1}`$; since $`\mathrm{id}_Q \in \{L_x^{(*)}\}`$, $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q, \odot) = \mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q, *)`$ (and similarly $`\mathop{\mathrm{RMlt}}\nolimits(Q, \odot) = \mathop{\mathrm{RMlt}}\nolimits(Q, *)`$ and $`\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q, \odot) = \mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q, *)`$). Thus $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q), \mathop{\mathrm{RMlt}}\nolimits(Q), \mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q) \le \mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits(Q)`$ are loop-isotopy invariants up to permutation isomorphism.
2. **Strict pairwise non-isotopy across the five constructive families**: Let $`G := \mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$:
   - **Family 1 (`C0003`: solvable-`LMlt` $`Q_{p,q}`$ and $`Q_{1152}`$)**: $`G`$ is **solvable** ($`E(G) = 1`$). *(Note: $`Q_{2,3} = Q_{24}`$ on $`S_4`$ belongs to Family 1 since $`S_4`$ is solvable rather than almost simple.)*
   - **Family 2 (`C0002`: almost-simple $`X = T Y_0 = T Y_1`$)**: $`G`$ is nonsolvable with $`\mathop{\mathrm{Fit}}\nolimits(G) = 1`$ and $`\mathop{\mathrm{Soc}}\nolimits(G) \cong T \times T`$ having **exact simple-factor count $`2`$**.
   - **Family 3 (`C0004`: affine-simple/quasisimple $`V \rtimes G_0`$)**: $`G = (V \rtimes G_0)^2`$ is nonsolvable with $`O_p(G) = V \times V \neq 1`$ and $`G / O_p(G) \cong G_0 \times G_0`$ **perfect** ($`(G / O_p(G))^{\mathrm{ab}} = 1`$).
   - **Family 4 (`C0005` Theorem 1.1(2)–(3): transitive-socle wreath $`T \wr P`$, $`r \ge 2`$)**: $`G`$ is nonsolvable with $`\mathop{\mathrm{Fit}}\nolimits(G) = 1`$ and $`\mathop{\mathrm{Soc}}\nolimits(G) \cong T^{2r}`$ having **$`2r \ge 4`$ simple factors**.
   - **Family 5 (`N07E` $`Q_{96 \cdot 16^k}^{\mathrm{op}}`$ and all `C0006` loop isotopes)**: $`G`$ is nonsolvable with $`O_2(G) \neq 1`$ and $`G / O_2(G) \cong S_5`$, so $`(G / O_2(G))^{\mathrm{ab}} \cong C_2 \neq 1`$.
   These group-theoretic invariants of $`G = \mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$ are incompatible across every distinct pair among Families 1–5, proving **zero cross-family loop isotopies**. $`\square`$
