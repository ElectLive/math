## Abelian-by-cyclic formulation audit

The literal historical question assumes an odd-order abelian normal subgroup N and cyclic quotient of order greater than3; it also asks whether coprime kernel and quotient orders suffice for associativity. [Grishkov–Zavarnitsine, Abelian-by-cyclic Moufang loops, arXiv1202.3228v1, introduction and formula(1)](https://arxiv.org/html/1202.3228v1) gives a nonassociative loop L with abelian kernel N of order7³ and quotient C3. The direct product L×C2 retains nonassociativity, has normal abelian subgroup N×{1}, and quotient C3×C2≅C6. The kernel order343 is coprime to6. Thus the **literal wording** has a negative answer by this elementary consequence of the published construction.

The source itself says its quotient-three examples are not direct counterexamples to the question. We therefore flag a possible missing intended restriction rather than attributing the direct-product deduction to its authors. An independent research-agent cross-check confirmed the product argument and construction: in formula(1) over F7 with R0={1,2,4}, a=(2,0,0,0), b=(1,1,0,0), c=(1,0,1,0) give (ab)c=(2,2,2,0) but a(bc)=(2,2,2,6). This is an intake-level hand check, not a formal proof certificate. Recover any intended restriction (such as a quotient condition excluding3), rather than opening a false literal conjecture. [Drápal–Vojtěchovský, arXiv2301.03683](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.03683) treats split3-divisible data; do not conflate that hypothesis with the original wording.
