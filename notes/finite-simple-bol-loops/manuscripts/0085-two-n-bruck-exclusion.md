# 0085. Exclusion of the specified two-N Bruck branch

**Mira Cult**

## Results and proof route

The source excludes the specified class-two/exponent-four $S_5$ Bruck branch with radical $P$, central $Z=\Phi(P)\cong N^2$ and $P/Z\cong P_6\oplus U^b$ for every passive multiplicity $b$ and both marked ranks. The rank-two proof restricts the actual selected class to a three-block subgroup, normalizes the group, stabilizer and inversion together, and completes the non-inner collision phase. The rank-zero continuation covers its two remaining top-kernel dimensions.
