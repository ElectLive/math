# All-field dimension-four linear and unitary full-socle exclusions

**Mira Cult**

## Results

For every odd prime power q, no finite simple proper left Bol loop has canonical left multiplication group G with

```math
\operatorname{PSL}_4(q)\leq G\leq\operatorname{Aut}(\operatorname{PSL}_4(q))
```

or with PSL replaced by PSU, subject to the canonical and classical inputs in the proofs. Combining these canonical-overgroup results with the separately proved actual proper-factor exclusions and the characteristic-two assemblies excludes PSL₄(q) and PSU₄(q) as full multiplication socles in every characteristic.

The full-socle conclusion is an assembly, not a consequence of proving only G=S. It says nothing against these groups occurring as components or sections in other envelopes.

## From canonical equality to every overgroup

The equality proofs derive actual projective involution-class inventories, retain scalar centers, and test every admissible selected union. They appear in [linear canonical equality](../sources/full-socle-completion/linear-four-odd-canonical.md) and [unitary canonical equality](../sources/full-socle-completion/unitary-four-odd-canonical.md).

For an actual overgroup, let H be its point stabilizer and Λ its original outside marks. The identities |Λ|=[G:H] and H∩Λ²={1} remain decisive. Restriction to the characteristic simple socle is injective because its centralizer is trivial. The canonical inner-inversion obstruction places the inversion label outside G/S. Since the products of that label with the selected marks generate G, odd field coordinates disappear and only a small outer two-group remains. This is a generation argument, not an assumption that the actual outer subgroup is abelian.

In the [linear overgroup proof](../sources/full-socle-completion/linear-four-odd-overgroups.md), class-square witnesses capture the internal projective involution needed to constrain H∩S. For q≡1 modulo four, the Sylow preimage in SL₄(q) has a unique involution and contains the central subgroup of order four. It is therefore cyclic rather than generalized quaternion. Its possible element orders give a bound on the two-part of H∩S and hence a lower bound on the valuation of |Λ|. Actual outer-label selections, semilinear class counts and exact polynomial certificates contradict the required index. The other congruence class has its own outer-label and odd-stabilizer analysis.

The [unitary overgroup proof](../sources/full-socle-completion/unitary-four-odd-overgroups.md) likewise uses the actual outer group: its relevant two-part is an elementary abelian group or a dihedral group, according to q modulo four. Generation rules out some apparent outer subgroups before any count. The remaining selections include the exceptional diagonal classes, their centralizers, and fusion under G. Class-square collisions and exact divisibility then exclude every case; the arithmetic certificate proves a uniform tail as well as checking its finite prefix.

## Completing the full-socle assembly

For M=Mlt(Q), I=M₁ and S=Soc(M), the actual factorization M=GI is split into the socle-containing, solvable-factor and proper nonsolvable-factor branches. The new overgroup theorems close the first branch. The earlier actual-factor arguments close the others, retaining the [A₉/S₉ source repair](../sources/full-socle-completion/alternating-solvable-factor-correction.md). The characteristic-two cases use [0095](0095-characteristic-two-linear-unitary-full-socles.md).

All statements retain the exact classical, finite-data and canonical hypotheses of these sources and their independent model reviews. No bounded field calculation alone proves an all-field claim. Odd-characteristic even dimensions at least six, other classical full actions, and the surviving loop operations remain outside this result.
