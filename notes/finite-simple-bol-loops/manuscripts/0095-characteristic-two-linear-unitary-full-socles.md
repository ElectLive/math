# No characteristic-two linear or unitary full multiplication socle

**Mira Cult**

## Theorem

Under the canonical, primitive almost-simple and actual-factor classification inputs of the linked proofs, a finite simple proper left Bol loop cannot have full multiplication socle PSLₙ(2ᶠ) or PSUₙ(2ᶠ), when the indicated group is nonabelian simple.

This is an exclusion of the socle of Mlt(Q) in its original action. It does not exclude linear or unitary sections of a different canonical envelope, and it is not a classification of operations.

## Exhaustive routing in the actual group

Set M=Mlt(Q), S=Soc(M), G=LMlt(Q), I=M₁, and H=G∩I. The primitive action gives M=GI, with I maximal and core-free. Four cases must be kept distinct.

1. If S≤G, the relevant canonical-overgroup theorem applies to the actual G between S and Aut(S). Odd dimensions use the prior canonical results; even dimensions require the [linear characteristic-two theorem](../sources/full-socle-completion/even-linear-characteristic-two-canonical.md) and the [unitary canonical extension](../sources/primitive-completion/radical-free-unitary-inversion.md).
2. If G is solvable, the full-action theorem forces the natural alternating or symmetric action of degree at least 24. The low-degree linear/alternating coincidences do not give such a case.
3. If I is solvable, the solvable-stabilizer reduction forces S≤G, returning to the first case.
4. Otherwise G and I are nonsolvable proper factors. Apply the appropriate linear or unitary factorization classification to this actual pair in both orientations.

The second case is essential: the nonsolvable-factor tables alone do not exhaust the both-solvable alternative. Moreover “tightly contains” a displayed factor means inclusion together with equality of solvable residuals. It does not identify the actual group with the table entry.

## Eliminating the factor orientations

The [linear assembly](../sources/full-socle-completion/characteristic-two-linear-full-socles.md) routes every relevant row through vector-parabolic, component and antiflag obstructions. The mechanisms include weighted original marked fibers, involution-square collisions, transvection intersections, and exact capacity bounds. The [extension-field antiflag theorem](../sources/full-socle-completion/extension-antiflag-pairs.md), [G₂ antiflag theorem](../sources/full-socle-completion/g2-antiflag-pair.md), and [finite linear pairs](../sources/full-socle-completion/even-linear-finite-factor-pairs.md) supply the remaining orientations and endpoints. Equal group orders do not justify identifying PSL₃(4) with A₈.

The [unitary assembly](../sources/full-socle-completion/characteristic-two-unitary-full-socles.md) handles both line-side orientations and the nonzero proper-radical alternative. [Line-normalizer and parabolic recovery](../sources/full-socle-completion/even-unitary-line-parabolic-factors.md) use maximality in M to recover the actual full stabilizer. [Component intersections](../sources/full-socle-completion/even-unitary-component-intersections.md) then supply the required involution classes. The printed subscript in the unitary source's partial-radical alternative is corrected using its proof and common natural model; the conclusion is explicitly conditional on that correction.

The [A₉/S₉ repair](../sources/full-socle-completion/alternating-solvable-factor-correction.md) supplies two cases omitted from an older solvable-factor source. Both stabilizer classes and factor enlargements are retained in downstream uses.

## Boundary

The assemblies and component results have scoped independent model mathematical reviews. The exact certificates establish their stated arithmetic and character-table assertions, not the factorization classifications. The odd-characteristic even-dimensional canonical branch is not settled by this theorem. Dimension four is treated separately in [0096](0096-rank-four-full-socles.md); dimensions at least six remain open in the stated socle-containing branch.
