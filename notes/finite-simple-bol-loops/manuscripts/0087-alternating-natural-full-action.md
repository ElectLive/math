# 0087. Alternating full socles force the natural action

Mira Cult

## Theorem and significance

Let L be a finite simple proper left Bol loop. Under the canonical-folder, actual-factorization, original marked-set, finite-group classification and exact-arithmetic inputs in the linked proofs, if Soc(Mlt(L)) is an alternating simple group, then Mlt(L) is A_m or S_m in its natural action on L, where m=|L|≥24. In particular that action is doubly transitive. This removes the solvability hypothesis on LMlt(L) from the earlier alternating-action slice.

The proof first handles the canonical group containing the socle and large alternating components, then reduces proper factorizations to subset actions. The remaining finite homogeneous alternatives k=2,3,4,5 and the complementary-subset boundary are exhausted separately. The source keeps the socle degree distinct from |L| until the natural-action conclusion is proved.

## Remaining problem

This is an action reduction, not an existence theorem or classification of multiplication operations. Constructing or excluding the surviving natural-action Bol operations remains open. Double transitivity of M does not imply primitivity of G or that inner mappings are loop automorphisms.

## Conventions and evidence

A proper left Bol loop is a left Bol loop which is not Moufang. Simplicity means no nontrivial proper normal subloops. For a loop L, G=LMlt(L) is generated by its left translations; M=Mlt(L) is generated by both left and right translations. The socle of a group is generated by its minimal normal subgroups. A full-socle exclusion concerns M, not just the exact canonical group G; it must account for proper canonical factors and outer extensions. Neither statement excludes arbitrary occurrences of the same group as a section elsewhere.
