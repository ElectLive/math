# 0086. Frobenius models and proper-coordinate residuals

**Mira Cult**

## Results and proof route

The exact fixed Frobenius-147 canonical model has 15 loop classes, with a complete proof and recorded scoped automated reviews. This does not classify arbitrary order-147 quotients or all loops of that order. A distinct singleton-strip construction works over arbitrary odd Frobenius kernels, preserves exact within-family isomorphism data and checks every original normal cut; its full socle projections distinguish it from the earlier proper-coordinate construction.

The proper $A_5$ residual normal form expresses the actual embedded residual using primewise invariant codes and a cocycle. Its cohomology has two fixed-space linear-map terms under full projection. Actual coboundaries are retained until a whole-folder gauge is justified; top lifts, collisions and loop isomorphism remain separate obligations.

The stable-action source gives reviewed finite and prime-uniform exclusions, keeping analytic arguments separate from finite certificates. It retains the corrected $p=11$ proof and finite $p=31,41$ searches without extrapolating those searches to all primes.
