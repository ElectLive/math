# 0090. Exceptional full multiplication socle exclusions

Mira Cult

## Theorem

Let L be a finite simple proper left Bol loop. Subject to the canonical, automorphism, factorization, subgroup-classification and class-fusion inputs stated in the linked proofs, Soc(Mlt(L)) is none of the following nonabelian simple groups:

- Suzuki Sz(2^f) and small Ree ²G₂(3^f), for odd f≥3;
- G₂(q), for every prime power q>2;
- F₄(q), for every prime power q;
- E₈(q) and ³D₄(q), for all their simple-group parameters;
- E₇(2^f), f≥1;
- large Ree ²F₄(2^f), for odd f≥3;
- the Tits group ²F₄(2)′.

All are full multiplication socle exclusions, not just exclusions of a canonical left multiplication group. The nonsimple small-parameter Suzuki/Ree groups are not additional simple socles. Their derived simple groups, where relevant, use separately identified arguments.

## Proof architecture and complete sources

[Suzuki and small Ree](../sources/full-socle-completion/suzuki-small-ree-full-socles.md) combine odd cyclic outer groups with uniform subgroup-order bounds. The [field-involution collision theorem](../sources/full-socle-completion/exceptional-field-involution-exclusions.md) uses a fixed subgroup meeting every required involution class and lifts the collision to the original marked set. It verifies subfield fusion and triality interfaces explicitly rather than assuming them.

The [G₂ assembly](../sources/full-socle-completion/g2-full-socles.md) handles both actual proper-factor placements, including nonsimple Ree(3) through its derived group; [G₂(4)](../sources/full-socle-completion/g24-full-socle.md) has its own pinned finite certificates. The [even-characteristic F₄ proof](../sources/full-socle-completion/f4-even-full-socles.md) completes the odd-characteristic field-involution result by a socle-containing argument and separate symplectic/triality orientations. The [Tits proof](../sources/full-socle-completion/tits-full-socle.md) uses a necessary-order sieve without assuming that intersections of the actual factors multiply to the socle.

## Still open

E₆/²E₆, odd-characteristic E₇ and remaining classical full socles are not closed by this checkpoint. Later exploratory continuations are intentionally excluded from this publication. Even successful exhaustion of full-group families would still leave the operation-level classification.

## Conventions and evidence

A proper left Bol loop is a left Bol loop which is not Moufang. Simplicity means no nontrivial proper normal subloops. For a loop L, G=LMlt(L) is generated by its left translations; M=Mlt(L) is generated by both left and right translations. The socle of a group is generated by its minimal normal subgroups. A full-socle exclusion concerns M, not just the exact canonical group G; it must account for proper canonical factors and outer extensions. Neither statement excludes arbitrary occurrences of the same group as a section elsewhere.

These are conditional research theorems with complete written arguments and recorded independent automated-model reviews, not human refereeing, formal verification or a novelty claim. Each linked argument retains its canonical-folder, finite-group classification, embedding and computational inputs. This publication does not reprove those inputs. Exact source and review revisions, including corrections and inspection limits, are retained with the proofs and in the [publication record](../PUBLICATION-2026-11-17.md).
