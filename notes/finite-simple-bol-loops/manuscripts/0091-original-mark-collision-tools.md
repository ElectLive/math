# 0091. Original-mark collision lifting and canonical center constraints

Mira Cult

## Odd-kernel lifting theorem

Let E be a finite group, G a normal subgroup of index two, and N a normal subgroup of E contained in G and of odd order. Let Λ be a G-conjugacy-invariant set of involutions in E outside G. Write π:E→E/N. For every involution u in G,

    π(u) ∈ π(Λ)²  implies  u ∈ Λ².

This statement uses no loop, solvability or centrality hypothesis on N. To prove it, choose projected marks whose product is π(u). They generate a Klein four group. In its preimage, take a Sylow 2-subgroup containing u; it maps isomorphically to that four-group. Lift the two marks inside it. Sylow conjugacy in each odd-by-two preimage conjugates the original marks to these lifts by elements of N. Their product is the specified original u. Thus the collision is genuinely in the original marked set, not merely in a quotient.

[Complete proof and local component corollary](../sources/full-socle-completion/odd-normal-involution-collisions.md), independently reviewed by three automated models at source 59338c6eebe69e352d561b68c870ac842d6047e0. Its only group-theoretic input is Sylow's theorem.

## Canonical center constraint

For a finite simple nonassociative left Bol loop with the stated faithful generated canonical folder and inverting automorphism τ, τ fixes Z(G) pointwise and z²∈G′ for every z∈Z(G). The proof shows that the central subgroup {z⁻¹τ(z): z∈Z(G)} is trivial; it does not assert that Z(G) itself is trivial.

The [complete exact-factorization manuscript](../sources/factor-permutation/exact-factorization-criterion.md) proves this all-parity center statement using central saturation of the selected section and a genuine quotient-loop homomorphism with known kernel. The quotient folder is not asserted to be faithful. Three exact-source reviews accepted source e7476945cf987cf1c654a385ac8f78fe2beb320a, conditional on the pinned canonical inputs.

## Why these tools matter

The lifting theorem allows odd central kernels whose involution action is nontrivial; it is not limited to center-fixed lifts. Both tools avoid an unjustified assumption that projected data automatically form a faithful Bol folder. They feed the completed classical exclusions but neither proves a general radical quotient theorem, transports arbitrary odd-order collisions, nor classifies every nonsolvable left multiplication group.

## Conventions and evidence

A proper left Bol loop is a left Bol loop which is not Moufang. Simplicity means no nontrivial proper normal subloops. For a loop L, G=LMlt(L) is generated by its left translations; M=Mlt(L) is generated by both left and right translations. The socle of a group is generated by its minimal normal subgroups. A full-socle exclusion concerns M, not just the exact canonical group G; it must account for proper canonical factors and outer extensions. Neither statement excludes arbitrary occurrences of the same group as a section elsewhere.
