# 0066. Orbit pairing and paths for odd permutation tops

Mira Cult

## Results obtained

Let G = A₅^Ω ⋊ Q be the entire faithful canonical group of a finite simple nonassociative left Bol loop, with Q of odd order acting by pure factor permutations. When Q is nilpotent, canonical inversion exchanges every Q-orbit of factors with a distinct orbit. This includes unequal and nonregular orbits and correlated stabilizers. Original full diagonal strips reduce to two-coordinate strips in paths; every surviving path has opposite A₄ and C₅/D₁₀ endpoint types.

The orbit-pairing conclusion also holds for arbitrary odd Q under the additional hypothesis that the stabilizer maps onto Q. Surjectivity is not asserted for every nonnilpotent top. This note proves the pairing and path reductions; the [all-path affine parameterization](0068-abelian-nilpotent-path-parameters.md) completes the zero-residual-graph branch.

The proof uses actual selected involution classes and actual stabilizer images, not independently chosen coordinate marks. Its foundational folder and normal-subloop interfaces are stated in the linked sources. The two arguments below are retained in full because the path theorem depends on the pairing mechanism.

This contributes to the [classification frontier](../sources/factor-permutation/../../claims/0010.md). Let (G,H,K,τ) be the faithful generated canonical folder of a finite simple nonassociative left Bol loop, with Soc(G)=N=A5^Ω and G=N⋊Q, where Q is any finite odd nilpotent group acting faithfully by pure factor permutations. Then τ preserves no Q-orbit on Ω: every factor orbit is exchanged with a distinct factor orbit. The theorem includes Q=1, arbitrary nonregular and unequal orbit actions, arbitrary original full-strip sizes and arbitrary correlations in the proper residual. It does not classify the remaining paired-orbit folders or arbitrary canonical groups.

The exact inputs are the faithful-folder and actual canonical inversion interfaces C0001 and C0007; [original full-strip separation and the positive tensor argument](../sources/factor-permutation/radical-free-a5-orbit-tensors.md); [actual local stabilizers and pointwise proper-orbit marks](../sources/factor-permutation/radical-free-a5-stable-orbit-local.md); [actual selected-class weights and active-mark parity](../sources/factor-permutation/radical-free-a5-full-strip-matching.md); and the [original strip-orbit forest](../sources/factor-permutation/radical-free-a5-strip-orbit-forest.md). Nilpotence supplies H→Q. The mixed character calculation, rooted-path reduction and chain direct-sum argument below are proved here. No quotient folder, residual product decomposition or invariance τ(H)=H is assumed.

## Mixed symmetric tensors

Let ψ=sgn⊗(permutation on six Sylow-5 groups−1), χ the natural deleted five-point S5 representation. Both restrict irreducibly to A5 and are nonisomorphic, real and self-dual.

On the seven S5 classes of types 1,2,2²,3,3·2,4,5, class sizes are (1,10,15,20,20,30,24); χ values are (4,2,0,1,−1,0,−1), ψ values (5,1,1,−1,1,−1,0). Applying Sym² character formula gives

  χ_Sym²χ · ψ = (50,4,2,−1,1,0,0),
  χ_Sym²ψ · χ = (60,6,0,0,0,0,0).

Each weighted class sum is 120. Therefore both (Sym²χ⊗ψ)^S5 and (Sym²ψ⊗χ)^S5 have dimension 1. The cubic mixed products have class vectors (100,6,0,−2,0,0,0) for Sym³χ⊗ψ and (140,6,0,2,0,0,0) for Sym³ψ⊗χ, with invariant dimensions 1 and 2 respectively. Each representation also has invariant symmetric polynomials of degrees 2 and 3: ψ is in the pinned positive tensor source, while for χ these are the quadratic and cubic power sums on the sum-zero natural five-space. The invariant polynomial ring on ψ⊕χ is an integral domain. Multiplying the eight nonzero invariant polynomials of bidegrees (2,0),(3,0),(0,2),(0,3),(2,1),(1,2),(3,1),(1,3) therefore gives an S5-invariant separately symmetric tensor in every bidegree (a,b) with a+b≥2 EXCEPT (1,1). Here a counts ψ labels and b counts χ labels. The bidegree coverage is explicit: when one coordinate is 1, use the mixed base degrees 2 and 3 in the other and add pure degrees 2 and 3; when both coordinates are at least 2, multiply pure invariants; when one is zero use the other pure degree. No invariant exists at (1,1), by Schur's lemma and nonisomorphism. Degrees (1,0),(0,1) likewise have no invariant.

Let Ω=Uψ ⊔ Uχ ⊔ U0, with Uψ,Uχ disjoint Q- and τ-stable unions of factor orbits. Assume Uψ∪Uχ is nonempty. For every original full diagonal strip S, set a=|S∩Uψ|, b=|S∩Uχ| and require either (a,b)=(0,0), or a+b≥2 and (a,b)≠(1,1). For every ψ-labeled proper coordinate require the actual H-coordinate stabilizer to fix a ψ-vector; this holds when its nontrivial H0 projection contains 5, because its actual inner local normalizer lies in D10. For every χ-labeled proper coordinate require its actual H-coordinate stabilizer to fix a χ-vector; this holds when its nontrivial proper H0 projection is 5-free, since its A5 normalizer is intransitive on five points. If the H0 projection is trivial this last implication is not automatic: an actual C5 local image is transitive and fixes no χ vector.

Finally require at least one actual selected outside involution λ whose local mark at every σ-fixed factor in Uχ has nonzero χ-trace, equivalently is identity or an outer transposition rather than a double transposition. (This condition is automatic if Uχ contains no σ-fixed factor.) Then the folder is impossible.

Proof. Label ψ,χ,1 on their respective coordinate sets. On each full strip straighten twists using Aut(A5)=S5 and put the separately symmetric S5-invariant tensor furnished by its bidegree. This is fixed by the actual H normalizer, including permutations among same-color strip coordinates and permutations between strips, after choosing coherent transported tensors. At proper coordinates transport a chosen vector fixed by the actual coordinate stabilizer along H; correlations elsewhere are irrelevant. Their tensor is a nonzero actual H-fixed vector. The marked-class sum is scalar on the nontrivial irreducible N tensor representation. Its trace on any outside involution is nonnegative: fixed ψ factors give 5 or 1; fixed χ factors give 4,2 or 0; swapped pairs of either color give their dimension, 5 or 4. The stated λ gives one positive trace, so the scalar is nonzero. The original transversal identity annihilates the H-fixed vector, contradiction. All selected marks, not just a local subgroup projection, enter the positivity condition.

## Rooted components are two-coordinate paths

Every component containing a τ-stable full-coordinate orbit must be a path, all original full strips in that component have size exactly two, and the actual H-coordinate-stabilizer image at its unique proper boundary is A4. Its boundary H0 projection is V4 or A4. The full-coordinate Q-sets along this path are mutually isomorphic to the stable root Q-set; the proper boundary Q-set has the same Q-action kernel as the root.

For any proper coordinate i, let L_i be the actual H0 projection and J_i the actual image of the H-stabilizer of i. Then J_i<A5. If L_i≠1 this follows because L_i is a nontrivial proper normal subgroup of J_i, while A5 is simple. If L_i=1 then J_i is an odd quotient of a subgroup of Q, so again is proper. If 5 divides |J_i|, then J_i≤D10 and ψ^{J_i}≠0. If 5 does not divide |J_i|, then χ^{J_i}≠0: its proper A5 subgroups lie in A4 or an S3-type subgroup, whose natural five-point actions have at least two orbits. Thus every proper boundary has an available label ψ or χ, determined using J_i rather than only L_i. Furthermore ψ^{J_i}=0 among proper A5 subgroups only when J_i=A4, by the character averages in the pinned local source.

Take a rooted forest component. Its unique marked half-edge is the stable full root. Let U consist of all its full-coordinate orbits and the proper τ-partner of each boundary. It is Q- and τ-invariant. Label the stable root ψ; label each pair of full-coordinate orbits on an internal edge by one common choice of ψ or χ, and label each boundary full/proper pair by one common representation with a nonzero fixed vector under its actual J_i. Every χ-labeled orbit is τ-paired with a different orbit, so any selected outside involution σ=qτ swaps χ coordinates in pairs and fixes none. The χ contribution to its trace is therefore a positive factor 4 per swapped pair. At ψ factors, fixed local involutions have positive trace and a swapped pair contributes 5. Hence every actual selected outside involution has strictly positive trace in the tensor representation; its N-class sum is a nonzero scalar on the irreducible labeled N-module. As in f866, the original transversal identity consequently forbids an H-fixed vector.

On the other hand, if every original strip in the component has a separately symmetric S5-invariant in the bidegree of its incident colors, the actual H-fixed vector exists. Put the chosen invariant on each full strip, straightening its twists; H acts by a common S5 automorphism and within-color slot permutations, so the tensor over each Q-orbit of strips is H-invariant. At each proper boundary choose an actual J_i-fixed vector and transport it around its Q-orbit using H; return maps fix the vector. Correlations inside the proper residual cause no issue because each coordinate vector is individually fixed by the actual local image. Tensoring all these nonzero factors gives the contradiction. Coordinates outside U carry the trivial representation.

To analyze the rooted component, the root stable orbit meets every strip in its strip orbit exactly once by the pinned tensor consequence. More generally, at any other vertex, the incoming full-coordinate orbit from the unique path to the root meets each strip exactly once. If it met each at least twice, take the τ-stable union of the stable root orbit and both full-coordinate orbits for each edge on that path. Strips at earlier path vertices meet this union at least twice, those at the terminal vertex at least twice by assumption, and all others zero. The positive tensor theorem excludes this union. Each rooted vertex therefore has an incoming incidence of multiplicity one (the root uses its marked half-edge). A graph-degree-one vertex would then be a singleton full strip, impossible. Hence every vertex has degree at least two, and a component with two or more boundaries has a branching vertex of degree at least three.

If there are at least two boundary half-edges, assign their available colors independently. Give the root color ψ and keep that color along the root stem; along any degree-two segment keep a constant color, choosing its value to meet a boundary color at its leaf. A branching vertex allows arbitrary incident colors, because its strips have at least three coordinates. Each boundary-to-root path meets a branching vertex, so no two forced colors meet across a pure degree-two segment. Every strip then has an invariant: a degree-two strip sees one color, and any mixed strip has at least three coordinates. The resulting H-fixed tensor contradicts the positive selected-class sum. Thus there is exactly one boundary; a finite tree with one marked half-edge, one boundary and every vertex of degree at least two is a path.

If the boundary admits ψ^{J_i}≠0, label the entire path ψ and get the same contradiction. Therefore J_i=A4. Its boundary label must be χ. If any path vertex has a strip of size at least three, switch the path color from ψ to χ there; the mixed invariant gives the same contradiction. Hence all strips in the path have size two. Since L_i◁A4 and J_i/L_i is an odd quotient of a subgroup of Q, L_i is V4 or A4.

For each full-coordinate orbit on this length-two path, sending a coordinate to its unique full strip is a Q-equivariant bijection onto the corresponding strip orbit. The strip Q-action is well-defined because H0 fixes every full strip as a direct factor. Across an internal edge τ gives a θ-semilinear bijection between its two coordinate orbits. The stable root Q-set is isomorphic to its own θ-twist through its τ-action. Induction using these semilinear edge maps and the Q-equivariant strip bijections therefore makes all full-coordinate Q-sets isomorphic to the root, with the same θ-stable action kernel. The proper boundary is the τ-transport of the terminal full orbit; its kernel is the θ-image of the terminal kernel. The root kernel is θ-stable because τ preserves the root orbit, so the proper boundary has that same kernel.

## Chain direct sums force every stable fixed mark to be a double

Let χ be the four-dimensional deleted five-point representation of S5. Its values on involutions are 4 at identity, 2 at a transposition, and 0 at a double transposition. It has an S5-invariant symmetric bilinear tensor, and its restriction to an A4 point stabilizer has a nonzero invariant vector.

Fix any actual selected outside involution λ∈Λ=τK, and let σ be its factor permutation. Take any σ-fixed coordinate i in a τ-stable full-projection Q-orbit. The rooted-path theorem just proved says that its component in the original full-strip orbit forest is a path of two-coordinate strips ending at one proper-coordinate orbit whose actual H-coordinate-stabilizer image is A4.

Starting at i, move across its unique original two-coordinate strip. At each ensuing full coordinate on an internal full/full τ-pair, move across its σ-pair and then across the unique original strip. At the terminal full/proper τ-pair, move across its σ-pair to the proper coordinate. Since the orbit graph is a path, this procedure visits distinct strip-orbit vertices and terminates. Let Y be the resulting coordinate set. It consists of whole original strips and exactly one proper coordinate. It is σ-stable: σ fixes i and swaps precisely the interstrip coordinate pairs and the terminal full/proper pair within the chain. No other coordinate of Y is σ-fixed, because all those coordinates belong to τ-exchanged pairs of Q-orbits. This construction does not require σ to preserve H0 or to permute original strips.

Let ℒ=Q·Y be the orbit of Y as a set of coordinates. For each Z∈ℒ form the irreducible N-module W_Z=⊗_{j∈Z}χ_j, with trivial action outside Z. Distinct Z give inequivalent N-modules. Their direct sum V=⊕_{Z∈ℒ}W_Z is an irreducible G-module: N acts semisimply with distinct constituents, and G/N=Q permutes those constituents transitively. The natural S5 wreath action of factor automorphisms and permutations realizes V. The permutation σ fixes Y and normalizes the Q-action on label sets, so it preserves ℒ. Consequently the natural wreath operators extend the action to the formal group generated by G and λ, even if λ acts innerly on G. Equivalently, the representation extends over G⋊<τ>, because τ differs from λ by an element of G. This uses no assertion τ(H)=H.

Let H_Y be the actual set stabilizer of Y in H. Since H maps onto Q, H acts transitively on ℒ. H_Y preserves the set of whole original full strips in Y, because H normalizes H0 and permutes its full direct factors. It also fixes the unique proper coordinate of Y, because H preserves full/proper projection type. Put the S5-invariant symmetric χ⊗χ tensor on every whole strip of Y, and put at the proper coordinate a nonzero vector fixed by its actual local H-point-stabilizer A4. The product is fixed by H_Y: arbitrary strip twists and strip permutations fix the symmetric tensor, and the proper coordinate is fixed by H_Y through the actual A4 image. Transporting this vector over H/H_Y and summing in the distinct W_Z summands gives a nonzero H-fixed vector of V.

Every μ∈Λ is an outside involution. In the natural wreath action, its trace on V is a sum over those Z∈ℒ that μ preserves. For each such Z, a μ-fixed factor contributes one of 4,2,0 and each μ-swapped pair contributes 4, because μ²=1. Thus every summand, and Tr_V(μ), is nonnegative. For the chosen λ, the summand Z=Y has exactly one σ-fixed coordinate i and L swapped pairs, where L is the number of strips in Y. Its trace is 4^L χ(λ_i). It is strictly positive when the local mark at i is identity or transposition. Hence in either of those two cases Tr_V(Σ_{μ∈Λ} μ)>0.

The set Λ is G-conjugacy invariant, so its class sum acts on the irreducible G-module V as a scalar. Positive trace makes this scalar nonzero. The original transversal identity (Σ_{k∈K}k)(Σ_{h∈H}h)=Σ_{g∈G}g, multiplied by the τ-operator, then says the marked class sum annihilates V^H, because V has nontrivial N-action. This contradicts the nonzero H-fixed vector. Therefore the λ-mark at i is a double transposition. The selected λ and its fixed coordinate i were arbitrary, so every selected fixed mark in a stable full orbit is a double.

The pinned stable-proper theorem already forces every selected fixed mark on a stable proper orbit to be a double. Every fixed factor of any selected outside involution lies in a τ-stable Q-orbit, because its factor permutation is qτ. Consequently all f fixed factors of every selected class are doubles, and its common total fixed-mark weight is D=15^f. A τ-stable Q-orbit has odd size, so f>0. The pinned actual active-mark parity restriction requires v2(D)≥2f, whereas v2(15^f)=0. This contradiction excludes every τ-stable Q-orbit in the stated model.

## Boundaries of this argument

The mixed-character inputs are the displayed exact seven-class sums. The rest is analytic group and representation theory; no computational census or formal verification is claimed.  The theorem removes the stable-orbit branch of this entire odd-nilpotent pure factor-permutation model, including the nonfaithful 5-kernel possibility isolated by the earlier weighted theorem. It leaves construction and isomorphism classification in the paired-orbit branch, nonnilpotent or outer factor actions, other socles and radical extensions open. The unrestricted finite simple Bol classification remains open.

## Extension to arbitrary odd tops with stabilizer surjectivity

Replace the nilpotence hypothesis by: Q is any finite odd group, still acting faithfully by pure factor permutations, and the actual map H→Q is onto. Then the orbit-pairing conclusion holds. The opposite-endpoint path reduction, endpoint transport, residual order and deficiency bounds, and conditional saturated splitting in [the paired-path source](../sources/factor-permutation/radical-free-a5-paired-path-residual.md) also hold under this replacement. Surjectivity is an explicit hypothesis here, not a consequence asserted for every odd Q.

Here is the audit and replacement of the hypotheses in the proofs. The original strip separation and positive tensor theorem already assume surjectivity and impose no nilpotence. The original strip-orbit forest proof uses only this tensor theorem, surjectivity and corefreeness: cycles and paths between two stable half-edges give its forbidden tensor, and a boundary-free component gives another. The proof therefore supplies the same forest under the present hypotheses; this is an audit of its argument, rather than an application of a theorem with unmet hypotheses.

At a proper coordinate, L_i is normal in the actual point-stabilizer image J_i. If L_i is nontrivial, J_i is proper by simplicity of A5. If L_i=1, J_i is a quotient of an odd subgroup of Q, so again is proper. Also J_i/L_i is odd. Thus the local character calculation still forces J_i=A4 and every selected fixed mark to be a double on stable proper orbits: the direct sum of ψ modules first forbids a ψ-fixed J_i-vector, and the direct sum of χ modules then has an actual H-fixed vector and nonnegative traces, positive if a selected fixed mark is identity or transposition. This argument needs no Sylow decomposition of Q. Every Q-orbit has odd size.

The mixed symmetric tensors, forest coloring, rooted two-coordinate paths and selected-chain direct sum proved above now apply verbatim as arguments: each uses only surjectivity, proper J_i, odd orbit sizes and the forest. They force all selected fixed marks, including full-coordinate marks, to be doubles. To justify the final count without importing a nilpotent-scoped assertion, let θ be the induced involution on Q. If qθ is an involution, then θ(q)=q^−1. For the unique square root t of q in its odd cyclic subgroup, θ(t)=t^−1 and tθt^−1=qθ. Thus all outside top involutions form one Q-conjugacy class, of odd size k=[Q:C_Q(θ)]. The actual selected marked set maps onto this class. Their factor permutations have the same numbers c of pairs and f of fixed factors. With all fixed marks doubles, there is exactly one N-class above each top label, of size 60^c15^f. Since H→Q, the original transversal count gives

    |H0|=60^c4^f/k,       |Ω|=2c+f.

A Sylow-2 subgroup of H0 is elementary abelian of dimension 2c+2f, since it embeds in a product of A5 Sylow-2 groups. On one actual selected N-class, its square contains precisely the involution-support patterns needed by the parity argument: all fixed coordinates may be arbitrary involutions or identity, and paired coordinates have matched identity/nonidentity support. The original H0∩Λ²=1 makes identity the only such Sylow element. Expanding the matching indicators as in the [selected-mark parity argument](../sources/factor-permutation/radical-free-a5-full-strip-matching.md) forces an injective projection choosing at most one coordinate from each of c pairs. Its target has dimension at most 2c, contradicting 2c+2f when f>0. A stable odd orbit forces f>0. Therefore all Q-orbits are paired.

For the paired-path consequences, repeat the degree-one and branching color arguments in the paired-path source. They use no further property of Q: no fixed factors makes all mixed traces positive, all endpoint J_i are proper, and the Q-action transports two-coordinate strips and endpoint types. The θ-semilinear endpoint transport is unchanged. The odd-top conjugacy calculation above gives one fixed-point-free N-class per top label and hence |R|=60^M/k. The 3/5 deficiency estimates are coordinate-order bounds. In the conditional all-A4-projection case, the Sylow-2 quotient, parity graph, column elimination by C3 and coprime Goursat proof likewise uses only oddness of k and the actual endpoint images. This proves the stated extension, subject to the same canonical-folder and group-theoretic inputs.

This extension does not establish H→Q for an arbitrary odd top. The nilpotent argument uses a proper normal maximal subgroup containing every proper image; that implication fails in general, for example for a C3 complement in C7⋊C3. This is a warning about the group argument, not a Bol-folder counterexample.

# Opposite endpoint paths

Continue [0323-finite-simple-non-moufang-bol-loops:C0010](../sources/factor-permutation/../../claims/0010.md) under the faithful generated canonical folder of a finite simple nonassociative left Bol loop, with Soc(G)=A5^Ω and G=Soc(G)⋊Q for Q finite odd nilpotent acting faithfully by pure factor permutations. Assume the [mixed symmetric tensor and complete stable-orbit exclusion](../sources/factor-permutation/radical-free-a5-nilpotent-orbit-pairing.md), the [original full-strip orbit forest](../sources/factor-permutation/radical-free-a5-strip-orbit-forest.md), and the canonical folder/marked-set interfaces pinned there. Thus τ exchanges every pair of Q-orbits on Ω; no coordinate-orbit regularity, residual independence or quotient folder is assumed. The conclusions below are necessary structural restrictions, not a classification or construction.

Each full-strip forest component is a path with exactly two proper boundary half-edges, and every original full strip has size two. At the two boundary proper orbits, the actual H-coordinate-stabilizer images are oppositely typed: one is A4, the other is C5 or D10. The same opposite-type rule holds for every τ-pair consisting of two proper-coordinate orbits. At an A4-actual-stabilizer end the H0 projection is V4 or A4. At a C5 end it is 1 or C5; at a D10 end it is D10.

Let χ be the natural deleted five-point S5 module and ψ the five-dimensional S5 extension used in the pinned pairing source. The cited mixed tensor theorem allows separately symmetric S5 invariants in every bidegree a+b≥2 except (1,1), with a,b the ψ and χ multiplicities. At an actual proper-coordinate stabilizer J<A5, ψ^J and χ^J are not both zero: if 5 divides |J| then J≤D10 and ψ^J≠0; otherwise χ^J≠0. More precisely, among proper subgroups ψ^J=0 exactly for J=A4, while χ^J=0 exactly when J is transitive on five points, i.e. J=C5 or D10. Properness of J holds even if the H0 projection L is trivial: if L≠1, L is a nontrivial proper normal subgroup of J, while if L=1, J is an odd quotient of a subgroup of Q.

The mixed tensor theorem may be applied to any nonempty τ-stable union U of selected full-coordinate orbits and proper boundary orbits, colored χ or ψ constantly on each Q-orbit and identically on each τ-pair, provided every selected full strip has an allowed bidegree and each selected proper coordinate uses a representation fixed by its actual point-stabilizer. Positivity is automatic: there are no τ-stable Q-orbits, so every selected outside involution swaps selected factors in pairs and fixes none, whether their color is ψ or χ. The original transversal identity and the actual H-fixed tensor then give the contradiction. No product decomposition of proper coordinates is needed.

First no forest vertex has degree one, counting boundary half-edges. Pick such a vertex and a path to any boundary, whose existence is guaranteed by the forest theorem. Let U consist of both full-coordinate orbits of every edge of that path plus the terminal full/proper boundary pair. At the degree-one starting vertex, every strip has at least two coordinates from its sole incident orbit because singleton strips are forbidden. Every internal path vertex has at least two selected incidences, and the terminal vertex has an incoming incidence and a boundary; if the path has length zero, the sole boundary incidence itself has multiplicity at least two. Select one color fixed by the boundary's actual J and give that color to every orbit in U. All selected strips carry a nonzero homogeneous symmetric invariant and all selected proper coordinates carry a J-fixed vector. The mixed tensor theorem contradicts the folder. Thus every vertex has degree≥2, and every finite forest component has at least two boundary half-edges.

If a component has at least three boundaries, it has a branching vertex of degree≥3. Pick any available color at each boundary, color each degree-two segment monochromatically to meet the adjacent boundary when applicable, and allow colors to change at branching vertices. A strip at a branching vertex has at least three coordinates and hence has an invariant for every incident-color bidegree; a two-coordinate strip on a degree-two segment is homogeneous. Every boundary-to-boundary path in a tree with at least three boundary leaves meets a branching vertex, so this assignment is possible. Label the entire component and its proper boundary partners accordingly; the mixed tensor contradiction follows. Therefore each component has exactly two boundaries and is a path.

If its two actual endpoint stabilizers admit a common color, color the whole path and both boundaries with it; all strip tensors are homogeneous and give a contradiction. If their available color sets are disjoint, one is χ-only A4 and the other ψ-only C5 or D10. If any path vertex has strips of size at least three, switch from one color to the other at that vertex, again obtaining a mixed tensor contradiction. Thus every strip in the path has size two and the endpoint types are opposite. The subgroup possibilities for L follow from L normal in J and J/L an odd quotient of a subgroup of Q.

For a pure proper/proper τ-pair, label the two Q-orbits by any common available color. No full strip meets them, and no selected outside involution has a fixed factor on this pair, so a common color produces the same contradiction. Its endpoint stabilizers therefore also have opposite types.

For a full-strip path with r strip-orbit vertices, each strip meets each of its two incident coordinate orbits once. Sending a full coordinate to its strip gives a Q-equivariant bijection to the strip Q-orbit; an internal τ-exchange gives a θ-semilinear bijection between its paired full orbits. Each endpoint proper orbit is τ-transport of its adjacent full orbit. Consequently the two proper endpoint Q-sets are θ^(r+1)-semilinearly equivalent. The corresponding action kernels are related by θ^(r+1). Each pure proper/proper pair has θ-semilinearly equivalent endpoint Q-sets.

Let d_j be the common endpoint orbit degree of the j-th full-strip path component, p_i the common endpoint degree of the i-th pure proper/proper pair, and M=Σ_j d_j+Σ_i p_i. Let m be the number of original full strips. The total number of socle coordinates is 2m+2M: every full strip has two coordinates and every proper coordinate belongs to exactly one opposite-type pair. There are no fixed factors for a selected outside involution, so c=m+M. The actual marked set has one N-class above each of k=[Q:C_Q(θ)] selected top labels and its order is k·60^c. The exact transversal count and original full-strip splitting yield

    |R|=60^M/k.                                              (1)

Among the M proper coordinates of A4-actual-stabilizer type, let z3 count those with H0 projection V4; among the M coordinates of C5/D10-actual-stabilizer type, let z5 count those with H0 projection 1. Coordinate-kernel filtrations retain arbitrary correlations and give

    z3≤v3(k),    z5≤v5(k).                                  (2)

Indeed the first kind contributes no 3 while every other A4-type coordinate contributes at most one 3; the second kind contributes no 5 while every other opposite coordinate contributes at most one 5. The exact valuations of (1) are v3(R)=M−v3(k) and v5(R)=M−v5(k). In particular, if 3 does not divide k, all A4-type H0 projections are A4; if 5 does not divide k, all opposite projections contain 5. These order constraints do not assume a product residual or discharge the mixed transversal conditions.

## A bounded splitting of the actual residual

Assume additionally that every A4-type endpoint has actual H0 projection A4. This holds in particular if 3 does not divide k, by (2). Then, after ordering the opposite and A4 endpoints,

    R = W5 × preimage(C) ≤ C5^M × A4^M,

where W5≤C5^M and C≤(A4/V4)^M, and the preimage is taken in A4^M. In particular R contains the independent V4^M subgroup; no opposite endpoint has a D10 projection. Correlations inside W5 and inside C remain.

Fix one actual selected outside N-class. Following its swapped-coordinate pairs and the independent size-two strips turns each full-strip path into a bijection of its two proper endpoint sets; pure proper pairs already have such a bijection. Every set of matched endpoint involutions extends along the resulting disjoint coordinate chains by choosing involutions in their original full strips. The local class-square criterion allows arbitrary nonidentity involution values, since A5 has only one such class. Thus R has no nonidentity involution with matched zero/nonzero support on every one of these M endpoint pairs.

Let W=R∩C5^M, embedded with identity on the A4 side. The natural quotient identifies X=R/W with a subgroup of C2^t×A4^M, where t counts the initially D10-type ambient endpoints. Since k is odd, |X| has 2-part 4^M. Its intersection with the normal elementary abelian subgroup C2^t×V4^M has 3-group quotient, so it is the unique Sylow-2 subgroup P of X, of order 4^M. Choose a Sylow-2 subgroup of R mapping isomorphically to P; W is odd. In such involution lifts a zero C2 endpoint is actually identity in C5, while a nonzero C2 endpoint is a reflection. Hence the support test on P is exactly the actual residual involution test.

For the identity indicators e_D,e_V of a paired C2 and V4 coordinate, the matched-support indicator is 1+e_D+e_V over F2. Expand the product over the M pairs and sum over P. Only identity matches, so its parity is one. Each expansion term counts the kernel of a projection selecting at most one endpoint of each pair. Unless every V4 endpoint is selected, its target has order smaller than 4^M and the kernel has even size. Thus the sole possible odd term is the full V4 projection, proving P→V4^M is an isomorphism. Write P as the graph of a linear map φ:V4^M→F2^t.

Let C be the image of X in C3^M. Normality of P makes φ invariant under its conjugation action: the C2 factor is central in the ambient quotient. The A4-projection hypothesis makes every coordinate of C nontrivial. For each i choose an element of C acting by order three on V4_i; its operator minus identity is invertible there. Invariance therefore gives φ(V4_i)=0. Hence φ=0. Lifting the pure V4^M Sylow subgroup back to R gives identity at every opposite endpoint, since an involution with trivial C2 image there lies in odd-order C5. Thus the actual independent V4^M lies in R. Its 2-part is already the full 2-part of R and it is normal in the ambient endpoint product; no D10 endpoint projection is possible.

After quotienting this independent V4^M, the residual is a subgroup of C5^M×C3^M. Its two projected images have coprime orders, so their common Goursat quotient is trivial. It is their direct product. Taking the full preimage proves the displayed splitting. This is a sufficient endpoint-saturation hypothesis; V4-only A4-type projections are not disposed of by this proof.

## Boundaries of this argument

These are analytic arguments with no computational certificate. The paired path geometry and actual residual order retain arbitrary original correlations. The splitting is proved only under its additional 3-bearing endpoint hypothesis; it does not classify the remaining quotient subgroups, top lifts, cross-label collisions, simplicity or isomorphisms. The unrestricted classification remains open.
