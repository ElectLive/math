## Full marks and proper-coordinate stabilizers

Write N=A5^(2n), H0=H∩N. Each top coset of outside involutions is a single N-orbit of size 60^n, so K is the union of cosets indexed by D⊆C_n with 0∈D. K generates G, so D generates C_n. Sandwich closure gives 2a+b∈D for a,b∈D; thus D is a union of cosets of 2C_n. Its image generates C_n/2C_n, so D=C_n. Hence K is the full anti-diagonal of S. Simplicity gives HN=G; consequently |H0|=60^n/n and n has only prime factors 2,3,5.

If both A5 coordinate orbits have full H0 projections, the full-strip decomposition supplies independent strips covering every coordinate. Pick a nontrivial involution on each: all τ-pairs then have conjugate involution coordinates, yielding H0∩K²≠1. If exactly one orbit is full, core-freeness forbids singleton strips and its transitive rotation-invariant strip partition has m≤n/2 blocks, and H0 splits into those m A5 strips and a proper-coordinate residual of order 60^(n−m)/n. This residual has both 3- and 5-divisors because v3(n),v5(n)<n/2. All its coordinate projections are conjugate under a top lift, so each has to contain both primes, impossible in a proper A5 subgroup. Thus all coordinates are proper. Uniformity under the top rotation forces one orbit to carry 5 and the other to carry 3, because no proper A5 subgroup contains both. The 5-side projections are C5 or D10, and the 3-side projections are C3, S3 or A4.

If the 3-side projection is C3, the 2-part capacity is at most 2^n, smaller than 2^(2n−v2(n)). If it is S3, a Sylow-2 subgroup P is a vector subspace of C2^n×C2^n of dimension 2n−v2(n). Its intersection with the matched-support diagonal has dimension at least n−v2(n)>0, so it supplies a nonidentity H0∩K² involution. Hence the 3-side projection is A4.

Let Q≤F3^n be the image of H0 under the coordinate A4/V4 maps. It has dimension n−v3(n); it is invariant under rotation after standardizing the coordinate groups with even τ-compatible paired conjugations. Its coordinate characters λ_i are nonzero and cyclically permuted. If two are proportional, all proportionality blocks have equal size ≥2, so dim Q≤n/2, contradicting v3(n)<n/2. The V4_i are therefore pairwise nonisomorphic simple F2[Q]-modules. Since H0′≤C5^n×V4^n and its projection to each V4_i is onto, E=O2(H0′) is a Q-invariant submodule projecting onto every V4_i. Semisimplicity and inequivalence force E=V4^n. Its 2-order is already 2^(2n), so v2(n)=0 and all 5-side projections are C5.

Thus n=3^a5^b and, by Goursat, H0=W×preimage(Q), where W≤F5^n has codimension b and Q≤F3^n has codimension a. The coordinate standardization can be chosen in A5 at each pair: all C5 subgroups are A5-conjugate, and their normalizer D10 acts transitively on the five A4 point stabilizers. Applying the same even conjugator in both coordinates of each τ-pair normalizes G and commutes with τ. A top lift now has entries in D10 on the 5-side and A4 on the other. Its n-th power lies in H0, so its D10 reflection signs sum to zero around the cycle. Coordinatewise even reflections in D10∩A4 change that sign vector by (1−shift) of an arbitrary binary vector; these changes fill precisely the even-sum subspace. This τ-compatible gauge removes all reflection signs. Only after this step is W invariant under the pure shift. On the other side the A4 base acts trivially on A4/V4, so Q is already invariant under its backward shift. Arbitrary coordinatewise odd conjugations, which need not normalize this G, are not used.

## Top-lift cohomology and the power restriction

Put M5=F5^n/W and M3=F3^n/Q, of dimensions b and a. A chosen top-one lift h=(xσ,yσ^-1), after the even sign gauge, has x∈C5^n and y∈A4^n. Its images define a cocycle η of C_n in M5⊕M3: η(j+k)=η(j)+g^jη(k), and the n-th norm is zero because h^n∈H0. The generator acts on the two modules by forward and backward shifts, respectively.

For j≠0 an actual H element over top j can lie in K² exactly when both components of η(j) lie in (1−g^j)M_p. Here is the precise collision interface. The first base entries are C5-elements, and the second A4-elements; conjugacy in S preserves the conjugacy classes of the products around the σ^j-cycles, up to permutation of those cycles. Such products on the two sides have orders in {1,5} and {1,2,3}, so a collision forces every cycle product to be the identity. Conversely η_p(j)∈(1−g^j)M_p means that, after adding a vector from W or Q, the corresponding ambient exponent vector lies in (1−shift^±j)F_p^n. This is exactly the zero-sum condition on each shift cycle. Multiplication by actual H0 elements makes all first C5 cycle products equal to 1 and all second A4 cycle products lie in V4. The independent V4^n inside H0 then clears each second cycle product to 1. Both resulting S elements are conjugate, by base elements, to σ^j and σ^-j, respectively, and hence are conjugate inverses. An element (u,v) with opposite top lies in K² precisely when v=z^-1u^-1z for some z∈S, as is seen by multiplying two anti-diagonals. Thus this is an actual nonidentity H∩K² collision. No projected group is assumed to be a folder.

In particular, if both cocycles restrict to coboundaries on a nontrivial subgroup of C_n, its nontrivial generator produces such a collision. The two opposite shifts cause no problem: their difference images on the ambient modules agree, and both restricted actions have the stated prime-power orders.

Suppose a≥2. Let g be a generator of the Sylow-3 subgroup C_(3^a), acting on M3 by the backward shift. Its action has 3-power order and is unipotent, so (g−1)^a=0 on the a-dimensional module. The value of the cocycle on the order-3 generator g^(3^(a−1)) is the norm operator applied to η(g): 1+g+···+g^(3^(a−1)−1)=(g−1)^(3^(a−1)−1), which vanishes because 3^(a−1)−1≥a. The M5 restriction to the same order-3 subgroup is a coboundary by averaging, since 3 is invertible in F5. The preceding collision contradicts a≥2. Likewise b≥2 is impossible: on the b-dimensional M5, the order-5 norm has exponent 5^(b−1)−1≥b, and the M3 restriction is a coboundary by averaging. Therefore a,b≤1 and n∈{1,3,5,15}.

## All lifts at the surviving lengths

For n=1,3,5,15, a or b, when nonzero, equals 1. A one-dimensional quotient of the cyclic shift over F3 or F5 has eigenvalue λ∈Fp* satisfying λ^n=1. Since n is odd and divides 15, and |F3*|=2, |F5*|=4, necessarily λ=1. Consequently every present W or Q is the augmentation kernel, and each present module is the trivial one-dimensional module. Let γ5∈F5 and γ3∈F3 denote the phases of the top-one lift, omitting a phase if its prime does not divide n. The order-p subgroup argument shows that every present phase is nonzero. Conversely, two lifts with the same phases differ by H0, so H is completely determined by its phases after the even coordinate gauges.

An index multiplication j↦uj with u∈(Z/n)* applied to both S factors commutes with τ and normalizes the model; rechoosing its top-one generator scales the two phases by the same unit or its inverse, depending on the indexing convention. CRT makes its reductions modulo 3 and 5 independently arbitrary nonzero elements, so it acts transitively on all nonzero phase choices for n=15, and also for n=3 or 5. With the convention σ:i↦i+1, this reindexing sends σ to σ^u and renormalizing to a top-one lift multiplies each phase by u^-1 modulo its prime. These coordinate permutations, together with the even τ-compatible standardization and reflection gauges above, prove at most one folder-isomorphism class at each surviving length. At n=1 there are no phases.

## Direct construction and all normal quotients

For each n∈{1,3,5,15}, take W=F5^n when 5∤n and the augmentation kernel when 5|n; take Q=F3^n when 3∤n and the augmentation kernel when 3|n. Let H0=W×preimage(Q)≤C5^n×A4^n and choose h=(xσ,yσ^-1) whose coordinate sums have prescribed nonzero phases γ5,γ3 whenever the corresponding module is present. The remaining phases are immaterial. Because n annihilates each present phase, h^n∈H0. Put H=<H0,h>; then H/H0≅C_n and |H|=60^n. For n=1 take h=1. The set K is the full anti-diagonal {(s,s^-1):s∈S}; it contains 1, is inverse-closed and satisfies the Bol sandwich identity, and Λ=tK is G-conjugacy invariant.

We verify H∩K²=1. At top zero, the first S-coordinate lies in C5^n and the second in A4^n. If these are conjugate inverses in S, every cycle-type coordinate agrees up to a cyclic shift, forcing both to be identity since A4 contains no element of order 5. At top j≠0, if 5|n and jγ5≠0, the sum of the first base coordinates is nonzero. Under σ^j this sum is the sum of its cycle products in C5, so at least one wreath-cycle product has order 5; every opposite cycle product lies in A4 and has order 1,2 or 3. The two S-coordinates cannot be conjugate inverses. If 3|n and jγ3≠0, at least one opposite wreath-cycle product has nonzero image in A4/V4 and order 3, while every first cycle product lies in C5. Again they are not conjugate inverses. At least one present phase is nonzero on every j≠0 by CRT. The standard wreath-product conjugacy necessity used here is immediate: conjugation may rotate the index cycles and conjugate their cycle products in A5, but preserves their orders. Thus H∩K²=1.

Since |H||K|=|G|, the collision condition and G-conjugacy-invariance of K²=Λ² imply that K is a transversal to H and all its conjugates. The full anti-diagonal generates G because S′=A5^n. The centralizer of N in G is trivial. Any nontrivial normal subgroup of G meets N, and its intersection with N is an invariant product of simple factors, hence contains one entire n-coordinate side N1 or N2. It follows that H is core-free, since all its H0 coordinate projections are proper. The exact normal-subloop criterion applies to the actual canonical folder.

A normal subgroup L whose intersection with N is exactly N1 centralizes N2, and C_G(N2)=N1, so L=N1; similarly for N2. A subgroup with trivial socle intersection centralizes N and is trivial. The remaining normal subgroups are precisely the preimages of subgroups of C_n above N. For L=N1 or N2, π_L(K)=S and π_L(H) is nontrivial: its base contains, respectively, V4^n or W, and |W|=5^(n−v5(n))>1. Thus |π_L(K)|=|S|>[S:π_L(H)]. If L contains N, then π_L(H)=π_L(K)=G/L, a cyclic group; for a proper L the strict normal-subloop inequality is |G/L|>1. These cases cover every nontrivial proper normal subgroup and prove simplicity. The stabilizer is nontrivial, so the loop is nonassociative. Finally H is properly contained in G∩[(D10^n⋊C_n)×(A4^n⋊C_n)], which is proper in G. Thus the faithful canonical action is imprimitive, and the simple-nonassociative-Moufang primitivity input of C0001 excludes the Moufang case. This proves existence and properness without invoking an unproved extension of Nagy's construction.

The n=1 case gives the standard C5/A4 folder. The distinct orders n·60^n distinguish the four classes.
