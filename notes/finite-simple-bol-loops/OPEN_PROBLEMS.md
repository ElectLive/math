# Open problems in the classification of finite simple Bol loops

These are mathematical targets, not a maintenance backlog. Each would close a
structural gap in the classification. **The complete classification remains open.**
Efficient enumeration is a later goal, not a requirement for resolving these questions.

## Conventions

Here a **proper left Bol loop** means a left Bol loop that is not Moufang.
A loop is **simple** if it has no nontrivial proper normal subloops. We classify
loops **up to isomorphism**, not merely up to isotopy.

For a loop Q, write G = LMlt(Q) for the permutation group generated by its left
translations L_x(y) = xy. This is not the full multiplication group Mlt(Q), which
also includes right translations. The **socle** of a finite group is the subgroup
generated by its minimal normal subgroups.

The status statements below describe the gaps in this research programme; they
are not claims that every question is new to the literature. Linked manuscripts
are research drafts with their own assumptions and review records, not substitutes
for those assumptions or for external verification.

## 1. Can the left multiplication group act primitively?

**Question.** Does there exist a finite simple proper left Bol loop Q for which
LMlt(Q) acts primitively on Q?

Equivalently, can the left translations have no common nontrivial invariant
partition of Q? A positive answer requires an actual loop, with proofs of the
Bol identity, properness, simplicity and primitivity. A negative answer must
exclude every such loop, not just particular constructions or permutation-group
types.

**Why this matters.** This asks whether a whole major permutation-group route to
classification is possible. Primitivity of Mlt(Q) is a different condition and
does not answer it.

**Starting point and status.** This is the primitive-action question discussed
in Nagy's [*Some remarks on simple Bol loops*](https://cmuc.karlin.mff.cuni.cz/pdf/cmuc0802/nagy.pdf)
(2008), Problem 2, translated to our left-Bol convention. It remains unresolved
in this programme. Existing exclusions cover selected action types, not all
primitive groups. See the [references](REFERENCES.md) for the relevant literature.

## 2. Classify the factor-permuting A₅-socle branch

**Question.** Classify all finite simple proper left Bol loops Q satisfying

- the solvable radical of G = LMlt(Q) is trivial;
- Soc(G) is isomorphic to a direct product of r copies of A₅, for some r ≥ 1.

Allow conjugation by G to permute these r simple direct factors. Do not assume
that G equals its socle, that a factor-permutation action is regular, or that
the canonical inversion automorphism pairs the factors in a prescribed way.

**Desired result.** Structural parameters constructing every loop in this class,
necessary and sufficient admissibility conditions, and an exact criterion for
when two parameter choices give isomorphic loops. A complete theorem for a
specified previously uncovered permutation action would be useful partial progress.

**Why this matters.** Classifying direct powers alone does not classify groups
lying above them. Permutation of the simple factors introduces correlations that
the already treated nonpermuting case does not capture.

**Starting point and status.** The [nonpermuting assembly](advances-2026-11-16/manuscripts/radical-free-a5-code-inversion.md) treats envelopes preserving every factor. The [all-graph nilpotent parameterization](manuscripts/0073-all-nilpotent-path-parameters.md) now supplies construction, exhaustion, explicit normal-cut tests and exact isomorphism equivalence for the entire stated pure A₅ odd-nilpotent top model, including nonzero residual dependency graphs. Its four independent model reviews are conditional on the pinned inputs; they are not human refereeing or formal certification. The earlier zero-graph boundary and normalization gap are no longer the current boundary for this model.

**2a. Nonzero graph realizability and orbit evaluation.** Do any admissible parameters with nonzero residual graph produce a faithful simple proper loop? If so, determine their isomorphism orbits in a specified family. The theorem explicitly does not assert such parameters exist. This is an unevaluated existence/orbit question, not a request to reprove the now-established exhaustive parameterization. No separate realization or impossibility result was located in the inspected checkpoint.

**2b. Odd nonnilpotent tops: actively investigated, not untouched.** In the same pure permutation setting G=A₅^Ω⋊Q with Q odd, prove that the actual identity stabilizer H maps onto Q, or construct and classify the failures. The [surjectivity investigation](manuscripts/0074-odd-top-surjectivity-boundary.md) already derives necessary socle/mark restrictions and tests a proper normally-generating top subgroup. That group-theoretic control example is not a Bol loop. The extension of the path theorem covers arbitrary odd Q **assuming** H→Q is surjective, with recorded separate automated reviews. The non-surjective investigation also has reviewed necessary restrictions, including exclusion of transitive pure A₅ tops. The general intransitive case remains open. Neither proves general surjectivity or exhausts nonnilpotent tops.

**2c. General outer actions: partially investigated.** Classify the remaining A₅-socle folders when factor permutations and genuine outer actions occur together. The [nonpermuting parity-code work](advances-2026-11-16/manuscripts/radical-free-a5-outer-codes.md) already treats outer data in its specified nonpermuting models and exhibits G≠Soc(G). Thus outer actions are neither excluded nor wholly uninvestigated. The pure-permutation all-graph theorem does not classify arbitrary subgroups above the socle in Aut(A₅) wr Sym(Ω). A general combined outer/permutation construction, exhaustion and equivalence theorem remains missing.

## 3. Which odd prime-power regular tops are admissible?

This is a concrete subproblem of Problem 2.

**Model.** Let P be a finite group of order p^e, where p is an odd prime and e ≥ 1.
Let P act on its own elements by left multiplication, and form the semidirect
product S = A₅^P ⋊ P. Write top: S → P for the projection and
ab: P → P/[P,P] for abelianization. Define

```text
G(P) = { (x,y) in S × S : ab(top(x)) ab(top(y)) = 1 },
τ(x,y) = (y,x).
```

The displayed product takes place in the abelian group P/[P,P]. For nonabelian P,
this condition is not the same as requiring top(y) = top(x)⁻¹.

**Question.** Determine which P admit a finite simple proper left Bol loop whose
canonical left multiplication group and inversion automorphism are isomorphic
to the pair (G(P), τ). For every admissible P, classify all such loops up to
isomorphism.

**Concrete route.** The
[uniform reduction](advances-2026-11-16/manuscripts/radical-free-a5-prime-power-top.md)
reduces this exact model to cyclic e-dimensional modules over the field with p
elements, cocycles, a necessary-and-sufficient collision test, and a specified
coordinate equivalence action. Resolve those module/cocycle orbits for further
families of P, or prove a structural existence criterion for arbitrary P.
Do not quotient by arbitrary changes of basis or by cocycle coboundaries unless
they induce the required loop isomorphisms.

**Status.** General P remains unresolved. The [complete regular extraspecial census](manuscripts/0064-extraspecial-regular-census.md) proves 120 classes in the exact models, including 88 for order 5⁵ and exponent 25. The [faithful transitive Camina assembly](manuscripts/0065-transitive-camina-tops.md) extends to the stated nonregular actions and gives 128 classes. Their proofs and finite form verifier are now public; repeating these counts is not the next open problem. General module/cocycle orbit evaluation and groups outside these structural families remain open.

## 4. Classify the next odd derived-group layer

**Question.** Classify all finite simple proper left Bol loops Q of odd order
for which the commutator subgroup G′ of G = LMlt(Q) is nilpotent of class exactly
three.

Here “class exactly three” means that the lower central series of G′ satisfies
γ₄(G′) = 1 and γ₃(G′) ≠ 1. Impose no additional bound on the order or exponent
of G′, and do not assume its center is central in G.

**Desired result.** A construction and exhaustion theorem with explicit
admissibility, simplicity and isomorphism criteria. A solution confined to an
extra hypothesis, such as exponent p or centrality of the derived center, should
identify that restriction and the residual cases explicitly.

**Why this matters.** This is a defined next layer beyond the class-two framework,
rather than an unspecified request to “handle higher complexity.” It tests
whether the existing extension methods genuinely iterate.

**Starting point and status.** The
[class-two framework](packet/manuscripts/odd-class-two-classification.md)
does not cover this question. The
[central Lazard framework](packet/manuscripts/odd-lazard-central-classification.md)
covers restricted higher-class data with additional center and quotient
hypotheses, not this entire class. The
[unbounded-class construction](packet/manuscripts/odd-unbounded-derived-class.md)
also rules out treating class three as the final layer of the odd-order problem.
An unrestricted classification of the class-three slice is not established in
the current programme.

**Concrete follow-up not separately resolved in the inspected checkpoint.** Can
the coprime part of the stabilizer action move the center of a higher-class
nilpotent derived Sylow subgroup? The [coprime-center reduction](packet/manuscripts/odd-class-two-coprime-center-reduction.md)
excludes this for every class-two derived Sylow at arbitrary exponent, but not
for higher class. Own-prime noncentral center actions already exist and do not
answer this question. A counterexample must be an actual simple proper loop,
not just an abstract group with the desired action.

## 5. Mixed socles beyond the classified paired model

**Question.** Classify radical-free canonical folders with at least two
nonisomorphic nonabelian simple factor types, allowing the remaining socle
types, inversion patterns and extensions above the socle.

**Status: partially investigated.** The [mixed paired classification](manuscripts/0028-0030-mixed-paired-classification.md)
already gives construction, exhaustion and exact isomorphism tests for
G=A₅^(2A) × PSL₂(7)^(2B) with the stated paired inversion, as well as explicit
families and intrinsic exact-factorization/isotopy obstructions. The
[mixed quotient reductions](update-2026-11-16/manuscripts/radical-free-mixed-simple-quotients.md)
provide further restrictions. These results must not be relabeled as an
untouched mixed-socle problem. The open target is exhaustion beyond those
specified types and paired direct-product hypotheses, including factor
permutations and permitted outer extensions. Any next subcase should name its
socle types, action and inversion assumptions explicitly.

## 6. Radical extensions and actual loop-section lifting

**Question.** Give necessary and sufficient extension data reconstructing
faithful simple proper Bol folders with nontrivial solvable radical, with
simplicity and exact isomorphism tests. In particular, determine which
projected group/stabilizer/marked-set data lift through an elementary normal
layer to an actual Bol section.

**Status: partially investigated, not a blank branch.** Odd-order constructions,
class-two classifications and [elementary-layer obstructions](packet/manuscripts/odd-elementary-layer-obstructions.md)
already address substantial radical data. Their projected multitransversal,
overlap and defect constraints are necessary interfaces, not a general
sufficient lifting theorem. A projected marked set is not automatically a
quotient loop. Unrestricted radical extensions, including compatible actual
stabilizer cocycles and section data across successive layers, remain open;
the pure A₅ radical-free theorems do not settle them. This target must include
nonsplit and prime-overlap cases where the existing restricted extension
normal forms do not apply.

## Investigation-status audit

This board was reconciled on 16 November 2026 against research checkpoint
`508ed66f7323592d4ac8b74efc943b1e3072211a`, including the manuscripts rather
than only the older PR summary. “No separate result located” means no dedicated
resolution was found in that inspected material, not that nobody has ever
investigated the question. The clearest specific follow-ups are nonzero-graph
realizability (2a) and higher-class coprime center action (4). Odd-top
surjectivity is already being investigated; outer actions, mixed socles and
radical extensions have meaningful partial results. The unrestricted branches
remain open despite those advances. New deductions should update both the
[claims database](claims) and this board, preserving each theorem's hypotheses
and separating proved, proposed and unevaluated statements.

This does not settle intransitive or outer-action models.
