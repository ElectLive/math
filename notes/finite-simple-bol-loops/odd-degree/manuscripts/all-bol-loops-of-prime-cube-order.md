# Finite left Bol loops of prime-cube order: the cubic hidden in the centerless cases

**Mira Cult**

This is an ordinary loop classification, not a simple-loop existence assertion. The hard centerless branch is not excluded just because the actual left multiplication group is nilpotent. The construction below finds both a linear and a cubic series there.

## Classification

For a prime p≥5 put ε=4 if p≡1 mod 3, and ε=0 if p≡2 mod 3. The complete list of left Bol loops of order p³ up to ordinary isomorphism consists of four disjoint rows:

| Actual left multiplication group | Objects | Isomorphism classes | Isotopy classes |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Regular | Five groups | 5 | 5 |
| Class two, nonregular | Centerless elliptic quadratic family | (p+1)/2 | 1 |
| Class three | Four signed Engel groups with a stabilizer line | p+5 | 4 |
| Class four | Centerless irreducible cubic family | (p(p+1)+ε)/3 | (p+1+ε)/3 |

Thus there are p+10 centrally nilpotent ordinary classes, and (p+1)/2+(p(p+1)+ε)/3 centerless classes. In total there are **28** ordinary classes at order 125 and **41** at order 343. The number of isotopy classes is 10+(p+1+ε)/3. The two centerless rows have different *physical* left multiplication groups, both of order p⁵ but of respective nilpotency classes two and four. For all rows, the group, the literal marks, the allowed subgroups and the isomorphism rule below construct actual multiplication tables, not just counts. The low primes are outside the class-four Lazard argument: at 2 and 3 the already known total ordinary counts are 11 and 15. We do not extrapolate either displayed formula to them. Opposite multiplication gives the corresponding right Bol count.

The class-two result proved separately in [the abelian-associate normal form](abelian-bruck-fibers-as-alternating-algebras.md) is an input for row two and the group accounting. It has a direct proof over the known KNV class-two implication. The other rows below use neither a general Schur-cover theorem nor an assumption that the native Aschbacher radical vanishes. The previous [centrally nilpotent candidate, October 2026](https://www.evidencepress.org/releases/bol-prime-cube-classification/) gives the same p+10 count, but does not treat general centerless loops. We derive that shared branch independently as part of the exhaustive folder argument and do not claim the p+10 result as new. See [Grishkov–Kinyon–Vojtěchovský, §5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.14646) for the published order-27 classification, p=5,7 centrally nilpotent counts and exact formulation of their centrally nilpotent problem.

## One honest signed p-group covers every native table

Let D be the actual left multiplication group of any loop Q in question, with marks a_x=L_x. It is a p-group by [Foguel–Kinyon–Phillips, Theorem 6.7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0208231v2). Form the subgroup

```math
E=\langle k_x=(a_x,a_x^{-1}):x\in Q\rangle\le D^2,
 \qquad \sigma(u,v)=(v,u).
```

The marked set K={k_x} has size p³, contains identity, and is inverse- and sandwich-closed by the left Bol identity. It is *the entire* set inverted by σ. Indeed Kσ is a nonempty set of involutions invariant under conjugation by its members, and these generate E⋊⟨σ⟩. Sylow conjugacy makes it the single class of all outside involutions (using a formal order-two sign if necessary). It follows also that K⁻¹K=K² is precisely the union of the E-conjugates of K: a product of outside involutions can be conjugated so its first factor is σ, and conversely the conjugacy-invariant product set contains K.

The actual action by first projection gives H≤E of index p³ with H∩K²={1}, since distinct native marks send any one point to different points. Conversely any such H in a proposed signed group with p³ generating inverted marks makes K sharply transitive on E/H (the same avoidance applies in every conjugate H); the multiplication k_{x*y}H=k_x k_yH is left Bol by sandwich closure. The actual left multiplication group is E/core(H). A nonidentity central inverted element of an actual faithful signed group is a central loop point: its product with every mark is an inverted mark, its left translation commutes with all others, and its right translation equals its left. Conversely a central point's left translation is central in D. We shall track the core instead of assuming faithfulness of the double.

Write γ_i for the lower central series of E. Each γ_i/γ_(i+1) has pure sign (−1)^i: the first quotient is generated by negatives, the others by their degree-i group commutators, multilinearly in the first quotient. The signed odd index |K|=|E:C_E(σ)| is the product of the orders of the negative layers. To see multiplicativity, fixed cosets lift to fixed elements for any σ-stable normal N in an odd group: in N⋊⟨σ⟩ the involution represented by g⁻¹σg for a fixed coset gN is conjugate to σ by N (Sylow), so some representative centralizes σ. Apply successively. The same reasoning works with central fixed or inverted factors of nonprime order.

If E has class ≤2 then so does D, and the [abelian-associate classification](abelian-bruck-fibers-as-alternating-algebras.md) applies. Otherwise γ₃/γ₄ is nonzero, and an abelianization of a noncyclic finite p-group needs at least two generators (Frattini). The negative budget p³ forces E_ab=C_p², |γ₃/γ₄|=p and γ₅=1. By commutator generation, γ₂/γ₃ is a nonzero quotient of Λ²C_p², hence C_p. This proves class at most four **before** invoking [Lazard's correspondence](https://www.numdam.org/item/ASENS_1954_3_71_2_101_0/) ([categorical formulation, Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.4654)): for p≥5, class <p, and we may work in the finite Lie ring L=log E. This preserves automorphisms, lower central series, groups of powers, subgroups, and conjugation is exp(ad). In particular σ has additive eigenspaces, the genuine marks are exp(L⁻), and group p-th power is multiplication by p in L. We do **not** assume exponent p before deriving it.

## Four possible class-three groups, one possible class-four group

Choose negative generators a,b lifting a basis of E_ab; negative representatives exist by the additive splitting since 2 is invertible. The images generate E by Frattini, hence L as a Lie ring (finite p-subgroups are closed under the divisions in Lazard). Put d=[a,b]. The map u↦[u,d] onto γ₃/γ₄ has one-dimensional kernel on L_ab. Choose b on that line and a off it; recomputing d only scales its class. Let c=[a,d]. The bracket [b,d] lies in γ₄ and is negative, so it vanishes since that layer is positive. Then [b,c]=[b,[a,d]]=[[b,a],d]+[a,[b,d]]=0. Consequently γ₄ is generated by w=[a,c], if nonzero. The commutator map from L_ab×(γ₃/γ₄) bounds its order by p; all higher brackets vanish. In particular d,c,w have pure respective signs +,−,+. We have pc=0 since pc is negative and belongs to γ₄. Since pa and pb lie in the negative part of γ₂, write pa=A c, pb=B c (with c generating that part since γ₂/γ₃ and γ₄ are positive). Now pd=[pa,b]=0. If w≠0, 0=[a,pa]=A w and 0=pd=[a,pb]=B w force A=B=0. All possibilities are therefore the following explicit Lie rings with their indicated additive orders and brackets:
* Class three: a,b,d,c, with pa=A c, pb=B c, pd=pc=0; [a,b]=d, [a,d]=c. All other basic brackets zero. Order p⁴.
* Class four: a,b,d,c,w all of order p; [a,b]=d, [a,d]=c, [a,c]=w; all other basic brackets zero. Order p⁵.

Conversely these presentations define Lie rings of the asserted orders (an abelian ideal with one Jordan-chain derivation and the displayed compatible additive relations); their groups have precisely p³ inverted elements and these generate. In the first case the negative part has size p³ even when its additive exponent is p². The sign is negative on a,b,c and positive on d,w. In either case V=⟨b,d,c[,w]⟩ is abelian and is characteristic: it is the centralizer of γ₂, since [a,d]=c is nonzero of order p and any coefficient of a divisible by p is already in V. On V the adjoint N=ad(a) shifts the Jordan chain b→d→c→w→0, truncated after c in class three. In particular all computations below are with genuine marks and actual stabilizers.

## Finding every stabilizer, not just constructing convenient ones

Every element with nonzero a-coordinate modulo V is conjugate to a negative. Indeed conjugation in the Lie ring by exp(v), v∈V, adds [v,x]=−sNv to x when its a-coordinate is s≠0, and higher terms vanish. Nv spans ⟨d,c[,w]⟩; choose v to kill all its positive coordinates. Thus H≤exp V. Write Lie coordinates on V as b,d,c[,w] in that order.

In class three, every vector with nonzero b-coordinate is also conjugate to a negative: exp(tN) changes the d-coordinate by t times its b-coordinate. In ⟨d,c⟩ the vectors avoiding all conjugates of negatives apart from 0 are exactly those with d-coordinate nonzero. Thus the only stabilizers are

```math
H_h=\exp\langle d+h c\rangle,\qquad h\in\mathbb F_p .
```

Each really avoids K² and has the right order p. The center of E is ⟨c⟩: the centralizer of γ₂ is V and the kernel of N on V is ⟨c⟩. No H_h meets it, so the p-group action is faithful (every nontrivial normal subgroup meets the center). The native center is exactly of order p by the central-mark criterion, hence all these tables are centrally nilpotent because the quotient has order p² and is abelian ([Burn](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004100055213); or [GKV, Theorem 4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.14646)). None is associative since its faithful LMlt is nonregular.

In class four the center is ⟨w⟩. If w∈log H, quotient by this positive central line. Each inverted element lifts uniquely to an inverted element in the class-four group (in additive Lie coordinates choose the negative lift), so the quotient stabilizer avoids all conjugates of negatively inverted elements there as well: an offending mark-conjugate lifts to one and H contains the whole kernel. This makes an already counted class-three faithful folder; it yields no new table. If w∉log H then H is core-free and its faithful loop is centerless since the group center has no nonidentity inverted elements.

In that last case log H is a two-dimensional subspace of V and meets ⟨c,w⟩ trivially: every vector with nonzero c-coordinate there is exp(tN)(u c), with freely determined w-coordinate; the remaining w-line was excluded. Hence it is **necessarily** a graph

```math
H_{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}:\quad (i,j,\alpha i+\beta j,\gamma i+\delta j),
                    \qquad i,j\in\mathbb F_p.                  \tag{1}
```

The conjugates of negatives within V are exactly exp(tN) applied to ⟨b,c⟩, because V is abelian and inner conjugation there is just exp(tN). For b-coordinate zero those vectors were already excluded, except zero. For b-coordinate one they are

```math
(1,t,t^2/2+v,t^3/6+t v),
       \qquad\text{so }w=t c-t^3/3.
```

Scaling handles every other nonzero b-coordinate. Thus (1) gives a true sharply transitive mark set exactly when

```math
P_H(T)=T^3-3\beta T^2+3(\delta-\alpha)T+3\gamma          \tag{2}
```

has no root in F_p, equivalently when it is irreducible. Every monic irreducible cubic occurs, with α free. This finite-field condition controls the actual two-involution avoidance; polynomial fitting to small sample counts is not involved.

## Ordinary isomorphism versus isotopy

Once the action is faithful, ordinary loop isomorphisms are exactly group isomorphisms of the actual LMlt preserving K and H: one direction follows by conjugating all native left translations, the other by evaluating the induced coset action at the identity. Such a group map is signed since σ is determined on the generating K by inversion. Hence below we use **all** signed Lie ring isomorphisms, not only the automorphisms visibly fixing the displayed basis.

In class three the characteristic V and parity force a new pair of negative generators to have the form

```math
a'=s a+u b+v c,\qquad b'=t b+r c,\qquad s,t\ne0.
```

Coefficients mod p suffice after absorbing p-multiples into c. Conversely these generate, d'=st d, c'=s²t c, and their additive parameters become

```math
A'=(sA+uB)/(s^2t),\qquad B'=B/s^2 .
```

Thus there are four signed types: (A,B)=(0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(0,ν) for ν nonsquare. Under signed automorphisms on a fixed type, the slope h of H_h scales by s. Every s≠0 is available for B=0 (choose t suitably when A=1); only and all s=±1 for B≠0. So the four counts are 2,2,(p+1)/2,(p+1)/2, summing to p+5. All are actual loop isotypes from distinct stabilizer orbits.

In class four the same generators give d'=st d+sr w, c'=s²t c, w'=s³t w. All are allowed; the free changes to the a-image have no further effect on V. Their action on the graph parameters is therefore

```math
(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta)\longmapsto
  (r/t+s^2\alpha,\ s\beta,\ s^3\gamma,\ r/t+s^2\delta).
```

The common shift r/t removes exactly the free choice of α for the same cubic. Consequently isomorphism is scalar equivalence of monic irreducible cubics, P(T)↦s³P(T/s). There are (p³−p)/3 such polynomials, by counting Frobenius triples outside F_p in F_(p³). A nonidentity scalar stabilizer requires s³=1 by the nonzero constant. If p≡1 mod 3, each of the two order-three scalars fixes exactly the 2(p−1)/3 binomials T³+c with −c a noncube. Burnside's lemma gives the class-four isomorphism count in the table.

For completeness, isotopy of these faithful signed folders is exactly signed group isomorphism **up to conjugating the point stabilizer**. If (α,β,γ) is an arbitrary loop isotopy, the second mark set is γ K β⁻¹; since it contains identity, β=γ a for some a∈K. Sandwich closure gives K a⁻¹=a^(1/2) K a^(−1/2). Conjugation by the point bijection γ a^(1/2) thus carries one genuine mark set onto the other, hence their generated groups and signs, but might move the actual identity point. This accounts exactly for conjugacy of the stabilizer. Conversely, rebase any sharp K at a point r: with β(x)=k_x(r) the new law satisfies β(x*ᵣy)=x*β(y), hence a true isotopy; a stabilizer conjugation is such a base-point change. Ordinary isomorphism of the marked folders handles the remaining group map. This proves both directions, not just that the examples lie in some common isotopy class.

In class three conjugation by exp(u a) replaces h by h+u, giving one isotopy type per signed type. In class four V acts trivially on H and exp(u a) replaces the cubic by P(T−u). Indeed for b-coordinate one the quantity w−j c+j³/3 is invariant when the vector (1,j,c,w) is acted on by exp(uN), while j becomes j+u. Thus isotopy is exactly affine change of variable on irreducible cubics, with leading coefficient normalized. Because 3 is a unit, each translation orbit has one depressed monic cubic (T² coefficient zero); there are (p²−1)/3. Now quotient by scalars: the same two possible order-three scalars and the same fixed binomials give (p+1+ε)/3. The physical groups of different rows have different class or order; isotopy preserves them. Each group is isotopic only to its own group isomorphism type (or use the signed-set argument for regular actions). The single class-two isotope follows from the separate intrinsic algebra source.

## Scope, prior evidence, reproduction

Nonassociativity in the last three rows is seen from nonregularity. This result does not produce an odd simple loop of prime-cube order; finite Bol p-loops of odd order are known to be solvable. It also does not classify arbitrary larger p-powers. The known [GKV paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.14646) separates the centerless order-27 branch from the centrally nilpotent one and goes to p=5,7 in the latter; [Bianco–Bonatto](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.12801) is prior work on prime-power connected quandles and in particular the Bruck subclass (see GKV §1); [the October 2026 versioned candidate](https://www.evidencepress.org/releases/bol-prime-cube-classification/) already claims the p+10 centrally nilpotent result for every odd p and expressly does not claim the centerless or isotopy enumeration. We neither assume that candidate nor declare an exhaustive historical-priority search.

The [native group checker](../code/verify_prime_cube_bol_folders.py) constructs the four exponent-p / genuine exponent-p² groups and the class-four group via truncated Jordan semidirect products, obtains K by the actual involution equation, computes every physical K², and tries all subgroup lines or all 806 and 2,850 planes in V for p=5,7 respectively. It also checks all p³-by-p³-by-p³ native Bol triples for each of the twenty higher-class isotypes at p=5, the actual centers, actual common Bruck tables per signed ambient group, full phase/shear conjugacies, and the polynomial orbits through p=19. [Frozen output](../evidence/prime-cube-bol-folders.out). These finite tests are controls, not a proof of the universal bounds, Frattini/negative-layer calculation or Lazard steps. The characteristic-three class-four failure was not silently interpolated.
