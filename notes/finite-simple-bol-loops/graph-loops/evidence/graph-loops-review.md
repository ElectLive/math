Expanding the central defect gives exactly


### 2. All-ring Bruck law and the characteristic-three Moufang specialization



### 4. Eisenstein arithmetic and the alternating obstruction

I checked the distinction between negation, conjugation, and multiplication by
a unit. Conjugation sends $`a+b\omega`$ to $`(a-b)-b\omega`$, fixes the rational
units, and swaps the two nontrivial cube roots. It is not negation.

