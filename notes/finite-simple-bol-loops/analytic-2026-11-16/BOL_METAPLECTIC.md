## Intrinsic associator operators and a global spectral gap

For a finite left Bol loop, put $`\ell_{x,y}=L_{x\circ y}^{-1}L_xL_y`$

counting measure. The Bol identity gives
$`\ell_{u,v}^{-1}=\ell_{v,u\circ v}`$. Thus $`\mathcal A`$ is self-adjoint, an
assertion that fails for the explicit five-element non-Bol control. If $`P`$

family satisfies $`\|\mathcal A-P\|=p^{-2}`$, including all zero, degenerate and
imprimitive right-nucleus characters. The fixed vector axis explains why

has exact centered norm $`\Lambda`$ above, and
$`\|\mathcal M^t-\Pi\|=\Lambda^t`$ for every positive integer $`t`$. Its
energy controls distance to constants with sharp lower coefficient
$`2(1-\Lambda)`$. For $`p=3,q=7`$ and the earlier rank-$`4h`$ coupled pencils
over $`E_A`$, the exact norm is $`1/3+(2/3)|E_A|^{-4h}`$, at every conductor without a
histogram threshold. All these operators and energy statements are
natural under actual loop isomorphisms. No marked word or vector is part
of their definition.

Every multiplicative map from this reciprocal family to an associative
