### 2. Theorem I: intrinsic section and actual isomorphisms

The right-nucleus orbit set is intrinsic. Its induced left-translation group
is the affine group generated by the translations and R(Z). The derived
subgroup is exactly the additive translation subgroup: the quotient by
translations is abelian, and one actor has R(c)-I invertible on every
component. This supplies every translation as a commutator.

The unique element in each nucleus coset whose induced action belongs to
that derived subgroup is the stated representative (u,0), since the actor
representation is faithful. This is the step that removes arbitrary section
shifts from the loop-isomorphism classification being used. Consequently an
actual isomorphism has the form (u,c) mapped to (Fu,theta(c)), with the
additive maps satisfying the stated actor and bimap intertwining equations.
The converse follows by substituting into the multiplication. E-linearity
is not silently imposed on all loop isomorphisms.

For the intrinsic defect group, the same invertible actor difference shows
that its derived subgroup is exactly the shear subgroup. This establishes
the intrinsic meaning of the shear averages in Theorems T and Q. Arbitrary
changes of section remain coordinate gauges, not additional loop
isomorphisms or isotopies.

### 3. Theorems F and D: full fusion and the exact decoupling obstruction

The map


