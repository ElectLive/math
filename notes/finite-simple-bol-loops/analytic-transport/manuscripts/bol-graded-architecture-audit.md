# Architecture audit for the graded Bol continuation

**Mira Cult**

## 1. A precise failure of an unpointed permutation-lattice route

The rigid-center principal-isotope pair has the same canonical group and
inverted set, with stabilizers related by a central shift in the derived group:
`H' = z H z^(-1)`. This is the explicit shift in the pinned
[rigid-center construction](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/blob/2a25b81e3efe5bdd8a3923e27c94aef16a559094/problems/0323-finite-simple-non-moufang-bol-loops/manuscripts/odd-rigid-center-nongloop.md).
The construction and nonisomorphism conclusions remain inherited inputs.

**Proposition A1 (unpointed functor obstruction).** Suppose a construction
assigns a graded G-module to each finite G-set, sends equivariant bijections
to grading-preserving G-module isomorphisms, and uses only the unpointed
G-set G/H, with a fixed marked subset K of G. If H'=zHz^(-1), every graded
trace of every fixed G-word is identical for the outputs at H and H'. This
includes every fixed word in the marked elements of K.

**Proof.** The map

    gH -> g z^(-1) H'

is well defined: replacing g by gh replaces the displayed representative by
g z^(-1)(z h z^(-1)), without changing its H'-coset. It is a G-equivariant
bijection, with inverse gH' -> gzH. Applying the assumed functor gives an
intertwiner in each grade, so traces of each element of G agree. A fixed word
evaluates to the same element of G on both sides. QED.

This applies, for example, to the permutation lattice Z[G/H], its standard
even rescaling, its augmentation lattice, and any functorial oscillator or
lattice construction from that unpointed representation. It is not a general
obstruction to lattice constructions for loops. The displayed bijection does
not generally preserve the distinguished coset H. Nor does the proposition
cover operators whose labels themselves use the pointed multiplication or f.
It identifies exactly why a point, stabilizer, or equivalent phase marking
must survive the passage to a graded object in this prescribed test.

One possible marked lattice is the augmentation root lattice
`ker(Z[L] -> Z)`, with distinguished vectors `e_x-e_e`; left translations
act as isometries. Its ordinary lattice is determined by |L|, so a new theorem
would have to use those marked vectors and operators. Merely replacing its
theta series by a lattice-VOA character does not evade A1.

## 2. A bounded braided-category erasure result

Let W be an elementary abelian group at an odd prime p, let
`a:W x W -> Fp` be alternating bilinear, and write
`psi(t)=exp(2 pi i t/p)`. Consider W-graded complex vector spaces with the
ordinary associator and braiding

    c(v_x tensor w_y)=psi(a(x,y)) w_y tensor v_x.

**Proposition A2.** This braided category is braided equivalent to W-graded
vector spaces with their ordinary symmetric interchange. The equivalence is
the identity on objects and morphisms, with tensorator

    J_(x,y)=psi(-a(x,y)/2).

**Proof.** Bilinearity makes `J_(x,y) J_(x+y,z)` equal to
`J_(y,z) J_(x,y+z)`, so J satisfies the monoidal coherence equation. The
hexagons for the displayed braiding follow from bilinearity as well. Finally,

    psi(a(x,y)) J_(x,y)=J_(y,x),

which is precisely the braided-functor equation for the stated tensorator.
The inverse tensorator gives the inverse equivalence. QED.

Thus this particular attempt to turn the alternating Heisenberg pairing into
intrinsic pointed braided fusion data forgets the pairing after braided
equivalence: its diagonal quadratic trace is identically one. It does not
erase a retained marked basis, an explicitly retained multiplication cocycle,
the actual Heisenberg representation category, or the conjugacy-class
Frobenius algebra used below. It is also distinct from the scalar associator
coboundary obstruction proved in the earlier cubic manuscript. Neither result
is a no-go theorem for nonassociative tensor or vertex structures generally.

## 3. Why the conjugacy seed is a substantive improvement

For the elementary odd class-two branch the proposed local seed is

    Q_minus = < K intersect P >,

where P is the characteristic Sylow factor of the canonical derived group.
Its coordinate identification as the preimage of the negative tau-eigenspace
is proved in the coordinate construction, with that source's hypotheses.
Using this subgroup has a clear reason: it is generated by actual local
Bol translations. It is not an independently selected Heisenberg group of a
convenient rank.

Let C be the set of conjugacy classes of Q_minus, and let Z act by central
translation. The algebra A=Fun(C,C), with pointwise multiplication, unit,
counting inner product and diagonal comultiplication, is a canonical special
commutative Frobenius algebra. The multiplication supplies information that
the list of dimensions of its Z-character spaces does not supply.

Here is the local mechanism, stated for a class-two central extension with
coordinates W x Z and alternating commutator B. Put

    B_x = { B(x,y): y in W },
    R_ell = { x: ell(B(x,y))=0 for every y in W }.

The classes above x are indexed by Z/B_x. With the chosen coordinate origin,
the ell-character space has one Fourier vector f_(ell,x) for each x in
R_ell. One may use the action on functions `(z.f)(c)=f(z^(-1)c)` and the
values `f_(ell,x)([x,t])=psi(-ell(t))`; this gives character psi(ell(z)).
Pointwise multiplication gives

    f_(ell,x) f_(mu,y) = delta_(x,y) f_(ell+mu,x).    (A3)

Consequently the multiplication image from A_ell tensor A_mu has dimension

    |R_ell intersect R_mu|.                          (A4)

All these spaces are linear radicals, so this is a power of p. Coordinate
changes transport the Fourier vectors and their products; an unchosen origin
changes basis phases but not the image or its rank.

For completeness the full join/split block is equally explicit. Set
`n_x=|Z/B_x|` and normalize each Fourier vector to norm one. If
`ell+mu=rho+sigma`, the `(ell,mu)->(rho,sigma)` block of Delta mu sends the
diagonal vector indexed by x to `1/n_x` times the corresponding output
vector exactly when x belongs to all four radicals; otherwise it is zero.
Its rank is

    |R_ell intersect R_mu intersect R_rho intersect R_sigma|. (A5)

If the total characters differ the block is zero. To prove the coefficient,
write `e_(ell,x)=f_(ell,x)/sqrt(n_x)`. Then
`mu(e_(ell,x) tensor e_(mu,x))=e_(ell+mu,x)/sqrt(n_x)` and take the adjoint.
This formula is an additional sewing control, not needed to manufacture a
binary fermion multiplication.

The induced set `X=G x_Z C` carries commuting left G and right Z actions,
where the right action is `[g,c] -> [g,z.c]`. The latter is well defined
because Z is abelian; it is not an identification with the generally different
left Z action. The embedded fiber `[1,C]` retains the local Frobenius algebra.
Actual translations in K act through the left action and obey their Bol
sandwich relation. The cocycle f supplies named right-central operators such
as those attached to `f(a), (T(a)-1)f(a), ...`. These are precisely the
markings absent from A1. Dropping them can again identify the rigid pair.

## 4. Equal individual ranks do not determine fusion

The following exact algebraic control isolates the information added by A3.
It is a control for conjugacy seeds of class-two groups. No realization as a
selected finite simple proper Bol loop is claimed here.

Fix an odd prime p and E=F_(p^2), with theta in E outside Fp. Identify the
character space of Z=Fp^2 with pairs (a,b). Consider two alternating pencils
on six-dimensional Fp-spaces.

For the first, take W_I=E^2 direct-sum Fp^2 and put

    B^I_(a,b)((u,k),(v,l))
       =Tr_(E/Fp)((a+b theta) det_E(u,v)).            (A6)

For every nonzero (a,b), multiplication by a+b theta is invertible and the
trace pairing is nondegenerate. The form has rank four and radical exactly
the last Fp^2 summand.

For the second, let U=Fp^3, with coordinates (x0,x1,x2), and define

    beta_(a,b)(x,y)
       =a(x0 y1-x1 y0)+b(x0 y2-x2 y0).

Take W_II=U direct-sum U and `B^II_(a,b)=beta_(a,b) direct-sum beta_(a,b)`.
For (a,b) nonzero, beta has rank two and radical

    { (0,x1,x2): a x1+b x2=0 }.

Therefore B^II also has rank four for every nonzero character. For two
linearly independent characters, however, the radicals in W_II intersect
only in zero, while those in W_I have intersection dimension two.

Each pencil is the character evaluation of a Z-valued alternating bimap B;
form the class-two group on W x Z by

    (x,z)(y,t)=(x+y,z+t+B(x,y)/2).

Its commutator is B, so the class calculation of section 3 applies. The two
character dimension lists are identical: dimension p^6 at the trivial
character and p^2 at each nontrivial character. But, for independent ell,mu,
the product image dimensions are respectively p^2 and 1. This proves that
the full labeled individual-rank list does not determine A3.

There is a stronger ablation. The Z-permutation characters of the two class
sets agree; their common cardinality is `p^6+(p^2-1)p^2`. Every nonidentity
z has `p^6-p^2` fixed points by character orthogonality, and all its other
cycles have length p. Thus every individual central permutation has the same
cycle shape in the two controls. Any exterior oscillator construction has
the same graded Z-character in both cases. Its pointwise-product current
algebras still distinguish them through A4. The additional information is
in the coupled operator structure, not in an unreported difference of ranks
or single-element modular traces.

## 5. What a valid graded coupling does and does not assert

Let A=Fun(X,C). On the exterior oscillator space with positive integer modes
and s fermion species, let c^dagger_(x,m,j), c_(x,m,j) be the ordinary
creation and contraction operators. For a in A define

    E_a^(m,n)=sum_(x in X,1<=j<=s)
                  a(x) c^dagger_(x,m,j) c_(x,n,j).

The CAR calculation, with finite sums over x and j, gives

    [E_a^(m,n),E_b^(r,t)]
       =delta_(n,r) E_(ab)^(m,t)-delta_(t,m) E_(ab)^(r,n). (A7)

The degree change is m-n. On the one-particle space at mode n the operator
is multiplication by a followed by transfer to mode m. Hence the span of
images on the mu-charge space, with a ranging through A_ell, is exactly the
pointwise multiplication image A_ell A_mu at charge ell+mu. The local
fiber recovers A4. For three distinct positive modes m,n,t, the commutator
`[E_a^(m,n),E_b^(n,t)]=E_(ab)^(m,t)` transports precisely this fusion to
another grade. This is a genuine law linking charges and grades.

The Bol operators conjugate these currents by their permutation action on a,
and preserve each right Z-charge sector. The construction therefore couples
the phase algebra to the actual sandwich action. It does not impose
`L_x L_y=L_(xy)` and does not require associativity of the loop. The current
algebra is a representation of the familiar matrix Lie algebra with
coefficients in A; the CAR mechanism itself is established mathematics.

With half-integer NS modes, one must not identify a binary product of two
one-particle modes with a single fermion at their summed energy: that energy
is integral and is not an NS one-particle mode. Formula A7 avoids that error.
Alternatively, the Delta mu blocks in A5 can connect two-particle windows
of equal total energy, for example (1/2,7/2) and (3/2,5/2).

For positive integer modes and 24 species, the weight-zero eta quotient
requires odd permutation cycles. A d-cycle contributes

    det(1+t g)=1-(-t)^d.

Thus for an odd-order permutation g of an N-element set, with c_d d-cycles,

    q^N Tr_F(Gamma(g) q^degree)
       =product_d [Delta(2d tau)/Delta(d tau)]^(c_d). (A8)

For even d the factor is instead Delta(d tau), after its share of the q
normalization, and has weight 12. The intended odd class-two envelopes meet
the odd-order hypothesis. The number 24 is a fixed analytic normalization
which clears eta multipliers; it is not a loop-theoretic prediction or
evidence of a Conway or Monster connection. The q shift is part of the
specified graded trace, without asserting a Virasoro conformal vector.

The companion fermionic construction adds a stronger probe supplied by the
actual sewing image. For `U=B_ell B_mu` inside B=Fun(X,C), put `s=dim U` and
let Pi be its orthogonal projector. Since G is unitary and preserves U, Pi
commutes with G. It is determined by the multiplication map and its adjoint:
equivalently, Pi is the support projector of `m_(ell,mu) m_(ell,mu)^*`.
Thus `Omega=1+(zeta_p-1)Pi` is a natural finite-order sewing intertwiner;
it is not being identified with a translation or a word in G.

Write `H(tau)=Delta(2 tau)/Delta(tau)` and N=dim B. The product of the traces
of `Gamma(Omega^j)`, for `1<=j<=p-1`, is an honest trace on a tensor power of
the existing graded object. Its normalized form is

    Z_U(tau)=H(tau)^((p-1)N-p s) H(p tau)^s.          (A9)

The full proof belongs to the companion construction. The elementary reason
is `product_(j=1)^(p-1)(1+zeta_p^j t)=(1+t^p)/(1+t)` for odd p. Changing the
primitive root merely permutes the tensor factors. Formula A9 is modular of
weight zero on Gamma_0(2p), and negative exponents cause no singularity in the
upper half-plane because Delta has none there. The first coefficient after
its leading `q^((p-1)N)` is `24((p-1)N-p s)`.

For the p=3 algebraic controls of section 4, apply the same probe directly
to B=A before induction: N=801 and the two independent-charge image
dimensions are 9 and 1. Their new sewing traces are

    H(tau)^1575 H(3 tau)^9,   H(tau)^1599 H(3 tau),

with first normalized coefficients 37800 and 38376. Their ratio is
`[H(3 tau)/H(tau)^3]^8`. Thus the identical central traces in section 4 do
not extend to the sewing-image intertwiners. This is a canonical structural
input to modularization, not a fit of prescribed Fourier coefficients. Its
analytic mechanism is still universal for a finite-order projector probe.

The four requested conditions are met in the following finite-seed sense by the
complete marked package, provided the companion seed and trace proofs hold:

1. The same Frobenius algebra contains all central characters and their actual
   products; the f-probes select intrinsic phase sectors.
2. The left K-operators are actual translations of the canonical folder and
   satisfy the Bol relation on the graded object.
3. Character projection of the weight-zero traces gives honest graded sector
   dimensions; in the prescribed phase fiber, the distinct one-particle
   multiplicities from radical dimensions 0 and 36 separate the rigid pair.
4. A7 realizes A3 across grades, and A4--A6 show information beyond every
   individual alternating rank, even beyond the graded central characters
   in the displayed algebraic controls. The sewing trace A9 makes this
   additional information visible to a genuine modular graded trace.

These conclusions concern the object with its multiplication, current
operators, actual translation labels, and named phase probes. The unmarked
graded space or a list of scalar functions is a weaker object. The eta law
remains a universal exterior-oscillator mechanism; A7 is not a new
denominator identity. No VOA axioms, orbifold fusion category, genus-zero
property, or classification outside the inherited branch have been proved by
this construction. A next strengthening would constrain the analytic traces
using these currents in a way not already implied by the universal oscillator
formula and the finite seed.

The companion manuscript's binary/triple channel law and stabilizer-count
reconstruction extend to arbitrary finite elementary abelian Z-sets. They
should not be attributed uniquely to the Bol identities. The loop supplies
their canonical input and markings. Theorem CF8 of
[the seed manuscript](bol-translation-class-fusion.md) adds restrictions from
its elementary bilinear input: normalized joint fusion counts are powers of p
and give a submodular rank function. Its separate 51-point control passes all
individual-character and central-trace tests but fails the joint condition.
Those are necessary input constraints, not sufficient classification criteria.

## 6. Primary-source comparison

The exact primary-source details and checked theorem/section locators are owned
by [REFERENCES.md](../REFERENCES.md#graded-bol-continuation-fermions-and-the-voa-boundary).
That record compares the finite CAR construction with the original fermionic

The proposed research contribution is the extraction of the conjugacy seed
from actual local Bol translations, its f-marked sector system, and the
coupled fusion/current invariants and isotope test. Exterior oscillators,
Fourier projection, matrix currents, induced sets and eta transformation laws
are not claimed as new. Historical priority for their particular Bol
combination has not been established by this scoped search.
