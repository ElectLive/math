# Local algebras, solvable radicals and left associators

**Mira Cult**

How complicated can the left multiplication group of a finite simple left Bol loop be if the loop has exponent six and the quotient of its left group by its solvable radical is fixed? These constructions keep that quotient equal to PSL₂(19). They use the [reviewed projective matrix input](../../projective-radicals/proofs/multiplicity.md), but the extensions below are **author proofs with finite checks, not independently reviewed results**.

- **R1. [Radical universality](noncommutative-radicals.md).** Every finite 3-group occurs in the faithful solvable radical of some finite simple non-Moufang left Bol loop of exponent six. In particular the radicals have unbounded derived length. The construction embeds actual two-sheet loop tables built from noncommutative nilpotent algebras. The [commutative construction](commutative-construction.md) explains the underlying twenty-site algebra and the specific projective input.
- **R2. [Native group subloops in principal isotopes](group-subloops-in-isotopes.md).** Every finite 3-group occurs with its own group multiplication in a principal isotope of an exponent-six host. This is not an embedding under the original loop multiplication, and the isotope need not have exponent six.
- **R3. [Two order-three left associators](two-associators.md#both-associators-of-order-three).** Every finite 3-group occurs arbitrarily deep in the derived series of the group generated by two genuine left associators of exact order three in an exponent-six simple host. The associators use a common second argument. The finite simple host relies on R1; the finite group construction and the infinite residually finite example do not. The latter realizes a free product of two cyclic groups through native inner maps.

The words “radical,” “subloop,” and “isotope” refer to different objects here. Neither the radical result nor the inner-map result embeds a group of large exponent in the original exponent-six multiplication table. None is a classification of all finite simple Bol loops.
