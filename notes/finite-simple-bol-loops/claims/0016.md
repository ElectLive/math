## Statement

Without invoking `CFSG`, the following structural obstructions and infinite-family eliminations hold for Uniform Orbital Displacement (`UOD`) and multiplication groups $`\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q_{\mathrm{loop}})`$ of finite loops on rank $`\ge 3`$ Almost-Simple (`AS`) spaces:
1. **Self-pairedness and Spectral Trace Integrality Obstruction**:
   - **(a)** Any transitive rank-$`3`$ permutation group $`P`$ with non-self-paired orbitals ($`\Delta_2 = \Delta_1^\top \neq \Delta_1`$) has odd order; hence **every** primitive rank-$`3`$ `AS` group has self-paired orbitals, defining a connected undirected strongly regular graph $`\Gamma = \mathrm{srg}(n, k_1, \lambda, \mu)`$ ($`n = 1 + k_1 + k_2`$).
   - **(b)** If $`\Gamma`$ has integer eigenvalues $`k_1 > r > 0 > s`$ of multiplicities $`1, f, g`$, then any $`\Delta_1`$-UOD element $`g_1 \in \mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(\Gamma)`$ and any $`\Delta_2`$-UOD element $`g_2 \in \mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(\Gamma)`$ satisfy
     $$c_{\Delta_1} := \frac{k_2 + 1 + s}{r - s} \in \mathbb{Z} \quad (|c_{\Delta_1}| \le f,\; |-1 - c_{\Delta_1}| \le g), \qquad c_{\Delta_2} := \frac{s - k_1}{r - s} \in \mathbb{Z} \quad (|c_{\Delta_2}| \le f,\; |-1 - c_{\Delta_2}| \le g).$$
     Consequently, **no** loop $`(Q_{\mathrm{loop}}, *)`$ satisfies $`\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q_{\mathrm{loop}}) \le \mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(\Gamma)`$ on: (i) $`E_{6,1}(q)`$ for all prime powers $`q \ge 2`$; (ii) $`\mathrm{NO}_{2m}^-(3)`$ for all $`m \ge 3`$ and $`\mathrm{NO}_{4t+2}^+(3)`$ for all $`t \ge 1`$; (iii) $`\mathrm{NU}_{2k}(2)`$ for all $`k \ge 2`$ with $`3 \nmid k`$; or (iv) any of the $`18`$ non-integral individual rank-$`3`$ `AS` graphs (`S_8(35)`, `PSL_3(4)(56)`, `M_22(77)`, `S_10(126)`, `M_22(176)`, `M_23(253)`, `McL(275)`, `G_2(4)(416)`, `M_24(1288)`, `PSU_6(2)(1408)`, `Suz(1782)`, `Co_2(2300)`, `Fi_22(3510)`, `Ru(4060)`, `Fi_22(14080)`, `Fi_23(31671)`, `Fi_23(137632)`, `Fi_24'(306936)`).
2. **$`\mathbb{F}_3`$-Hurwitz–Paige Moufang Collapse on $`\mathrm{NO}_{2m}^\varepsilon(3)`$ ($`m \ge 3`$)**: For every $`m \ge 3`$, $`\varepsilon \in \{+, -\}`$, and $`\delta \in \{\pm 1\}`$, the full `AS` stabilizer of $`\Omega_{\varepsilon, \delta} = \{[v] \in \mathbb{P}(\mathbb{F}_3^{2m}) : Q(v) = \delta\}`$ inside $`\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(\mathop{\mathrm{P\Omega}}\nolimits_{2m}^\varepsilon(3))`$ (including triality at $`(8, +)`$) is $`\mathop{\mathrm{PO}}\nolimits(V, Q) = O(V, Q) / \{\pm I_V\}`$. Any loop $`(Q_{\mathrm{loop}}, *)`$ on $`\Omega_{\varepsilon, \delta}`$ with $`\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q_{\mathrm{loop}}) \le \mathop{\mathrm{PO}}\nolimits(V, Q)`$ forces **$`(2m, \varepsilon) = (8, +)`$** and **$`Q_{\mathrm{loop}} \cong M(3)`$** (Paige's simple Moufang loop of order $`1080`$). In particular, zero proper left Bol loops exist at $`(8, +)`$, zero loops exist for $`(2m, \varepsilon) \neq (8, +)`$ (including $`\mathrm{NO}_{4t}^+(3)`$ for all $`t \ge 3`$), and zero loops exist on $`\Omega_7(3)`$ acting on $`G_2(3)`$-cosets (`deg 1080`).
3. **Complete `CFSG`-free elimination of half-spin, odd-orthogonal, unitary $`\mathrm{NU}_m(2)`$, symplectic hyperplane, and $`G_2(q)`$ actions**:
   - **(a) Odd-$`w`$ half-spin $`D_{w,w}(q)`$ ($`w \ge 5`$ odd, all $`q \ge 2`$)**: $`\mathop{\mathrm{Stab}}\nolimits_{\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(\mathop{\mathrm{P\Omega}}\nolimits_{2w}^+(q))}(\mathcal{M}_+)`$ has zero UOD elements on the $`\dim(E \cap X) = 1`$ orbital (closing all half-spin actions for $`w \ge 4`$ together with `0323-finite-simple-non-moufang-bol-loops:C0009`).
   - **(b) Odd-dimensional orthogonal nonsingular points $`\mathrm{NO}_{2m+1}^\varepsilon(q)`$ ($`m \ge 2`$, all odd $`q = p^f \ge 3`$, $`\varepsilon \in \{+, -\}`$)**: $`\mathop{\mathrm{P\Gamma O}}\nolimits_{2m+1}(q)`$ has zero UOD elements on the perpendicular orbital $`B(e, x) = 0`$ (also eliminating $`G_2(3)`$ on `deg 351` and `deg 378`).
   - **(c) Unitary nonsingular points $`\mathrm{NU}_m(2)`$ (all $`m \ge 4`$)**: $`\mathop{\mathrm{P\Gamma U}}\nolimits_m(2)`$ has zero $`\Delta_0`$-UOD elements for all odd $`m \ge 5`$ and zero $`\Delta_1`$-UOD elements for all even $`m = 2k \ge 4`$; hence **no** loop on $`\mathrm{NU}_m(2)`$ satisfies $`\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q_{\mathrm{loop}}) \le \mathop{\mathrm{P\Gamma U}}\nolimits_m(2)`$ for any $`m \ge 4`$.
   - **(d) Symplectic/orthogonal hyperplanes $`\Gamma\mathop{\mathrm{Sp}}\nolimits_{2r}(2^f)`$ on $`\Omega_\varepsilon`$ ($`O_{2r}^\varepsilon(2^f)`$-cosets, $`r \ge 2, f \ge 2`$)**: $`\Gamma\mathop{\mathrm{Sp}}\nolimits_{2r}(2^f)`$ has zero $`\Delta_0`$-UOD elements on $`\Omega_+`$ for all $`r \ge 2, f \ge 2`$ and on $`\Omega_-`$ for all odd $`r \ge 3, f \ge 2`$ (eliminating $`G_2(4)`$ `deg 2016` and $`G_2(8):3`$ `deg 130,816`); on $`\Omega_-`$ for even $`r = 2s \ge 2`$, **no** left Bol loop has $`\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q_{\mathrm{loop}}) \le \Gamma\mathop{\mathrm{Sp}}\nolimits_{4s}(q)`$ when $`q = 4`$ ($`\forall s \ge 1`$) or when $`s = 1`$ ($`\forall q = 2^f \ge 4`$), and at $`q = 8`$ ($`\forall s \ge 2`$) every loop with $`\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q_{\mathrm{loop}}) \le \Gamma\mathop{\mathrm{Sp}}\nolimits_{4s}(8)`$ lies inside $`\mathop{\mathrm{Sp}}\nolimits_{4s}(8)`$ of rank $`\ge 5`$ (zero rank-$`3`$ loops).
   - **(e) $`G_2(q)`$ octonion singular points (all $`q \ge 2`$)**: $`\Gamma G_2(q)`$ has zero UOD elements on the opposite-point orbital $`B(e, x) \neq 0`$ of $`\mathrm{GH}(q, q)`$.
