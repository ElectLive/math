# 0072. Coprime-metabelian-derived odd simple Bol classification

Mira Cult

## Statement and scope

Finite simple nonassociative odd left Bol loops whose canonical envelope has G′=M⋊T with nontrivial finite abelian M,T, coprime orders and [T,M]=M admit the explicit construction, exhaustive parameterization, finite admissibility and simplicity tests, and complete loop-isomorphism criterion in the linked proof. No coprimality of G/G′ with G′ and no split extension over G′ are assumed.

This class does not include every metabelian-derived or every odd simple loop. Non-Moufangness requires the separate identity test; the theorem's nonassociative hypothesis alone does not imply it. The result is not a short orbit census or an efficiency bound.

## Remaining questions

Remove the coprime metabelian-derived restrictions, resolve the remaining intrinsic classification problem, and determine workable orbit invariants or bounds where possible. General odd-envelope recursion and sufficient exact-factorization criteria do not settle those obligations.
