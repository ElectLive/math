## Statement

1. **Principal isotopy-orbit theorem for centerless exponent-$`2`$ left Bol loops**: Let $`Q = (K, *)`$ be a left Bol loop of exponent $`2`$ with faithful folder $`(G, H, K)`$ satisfying $`Z(G) = 1`$. Then every loop isotopic to $`Q`$ is a left Bol loop isomorphic to $`Q_{k_0}`$ with faithful folder $`(G, H, k_0 K)`$ for some $`k_0 \in K`$; isomorphism classes of loops isotopic to $`Q`$ biject with $`\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(Q)`$-orbits on $`K`$, with $`\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(Q_{k_0}) = \mathop{\mathrm{Stab}}\nolimits_{\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(Q)}(k_0)`$; $`k_0 = 1`$ is the unique exponent-$`2`$ loop in the isotopy class; and $`Q_{k_0}`$ is a left Bruck loop iff $`k_0 \in N_\rho(Q)`$.
2. **Complete 4-loop isotopy census of $`N_{96}^{\mathrm{op}}`$**: The isotopy class of Nagy's order-$`96`$ simple exponent-$`2`$ loop $`N_{96}^{\mathrm{op}}`$ (`|G| = 3840`, `Z(G) = 1`, `N_\rho = {1}`, `|\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(N_{96}^{\mathrm{op}})| = 80`) consists of **exact count $`4`$** pairwise non-isomorphic simple proper left Bol loops of order $`96`$ (none arising from an exact group factorization, and all satisfying $`\mathop{\mathrm{RMlt}}\nolimits = \mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits = A_{96}`$):
   - Orbit size $`1`$ ($`N_{96}^{\mathrm{op}}`$): $`|\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits| = 80`$, max order $`2`$, exponent $`2`$, Bruck (`AIP = True`);
   - Orbit sizes $`10`$ and $`5`$: $`|\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits| = 8`$ and $`16`$, element-order spectrum $`\{1:1, 2:31, 4:64\}`$, max order $`4`$, exponent $`4`$, **non-Bruck** simple proper left Bol $`2`$-loops;
   - Orbit size $`80`$: **$`|\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits| = 1`$ (rigid)**, element-order spectrum $`\{1:1, 2:11, 3:24, 4:36, 6:24\}`$, max order $`6`$, exponent $`12`$, **non-Bruck**.

## Hypotheses

Left Bol loops of exponent $`2`$ with $`Z(\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q)) = 1`$ in (1); Nagy's `N07E` §3 folder of order $`96`$ in (2). Completely CFSG-free.

## Proof

Complete proofs of Theorems 1.1 and 2.1 are given in [C0006.md](../sources/foundations/C0006.md): conjugating the principal-isotope folder $`(G, s_0 H s_0, K L_b)`$ ($`s_0 = L_{b * a}`$) by $`s_0`$ gives $`(G, H, k_0 K)`$ ($`k_0 = s_0 L_b s_0 \in K`$), and since $`\mathop{\mathrm{Inn}}\nolimits(k_i)`$ inverts $`k_i K`$ while $`Z(G) = 1`$, any folder isomorphism $`(G, H, k_0 K) \xrightarrow{\sim} (G, H, k_1 K)`$ lies in $`\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(Q)`$ and maps $`k_0 \mapsto k_1`$; Burnside's $`p^a q^b`$ theorem on $`|X| = 96 = 2^5 \cdot 3`$ rules out exact-factorization realizations since $`G \twoheadrightarrow S_5`$ is nonsolvable.
