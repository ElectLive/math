## Statement

Let $`Q`$ be a finite simple loop ($`|Q| > 2`$) with primitive full multiplication group $`M = \mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q) \le \mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits(Q)`$.
1. **Elimination of `PA`, `CD`, `HC`, `TW`, `SD`, and `HS` for all finite simple nonassociative loops**: No finite simple loop ($`|Q| > 2`$) has $`\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$ of O'Nan–Scott type `PA`, `CD`, `HC`, `TW`, or `SD` ($`k \ge 3`$); and any loop on a finite nonabelian simple group $`T`$ ($`1_Q = 1_T`$) with $`\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q) \le N_{\mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits(T)}(T_L \times T_R)`$ is equal to $`(T, \cdot)`$ or $`(T^{\mathrm{op}}, \circ)`$ (eliminating `HS` and `SD_2`).
2. **Elimination of `HA` and unconditional almost-simplicity for nonassociative left Bol loops**: No finite simple nonassociative left Bol loop has $`\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$ of O'Nan–Scott Affine type (`HA`). Consequently, for **every** finite simple nonassociative left Bol loop $`Q`$ (in particular every finite simple proper left Bol loop), $`\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$ is an **Almost Simple (`AS`)** primitive permutation group ($`T \trianglelefteq \mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q) \le \mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(T)`$ for a unique nonabelian finite simple group $`T`$).

## Hypotheses

Finite simple loops ($`|Q| > 2`$) in (1); finite simple nonassociative left Bol loops in (2). The Liebeck–Praeger–Saxl O'Nan–Scott Theorem (`LPS88`) classifying primitive permutation groups into the 8 types is CFSG-free. The affine exclusion depends on the prime-power portion of C0011; the later arguments use Feit–Thompson (1963) and Brauer–Suzuki (1959).
