# 0013: Odd-p N07E Affine-Subspace Rigidity and Multi-Module Solvable-LMlt Odd-Order Simple Family Q_{p,q;R}

Mira Cult · Claim family 0013

**Status:** partial. **Recorded model-review status:** unreviewed.



## Statement

1. **Universal odd-$`p`$ affine-subspace fiber rigidity**: Let $`p \ge 3`$ be an odd prime, $`(G, H, K)`$ a finite faithful left Bol folder, $`U`$ a finite elementary abelian $`\mathbb{F}_p G`$-module, and $`(\mathcal{G}, \mathcal{H}, \mathcal{K})`$ a left Bol folder in $`\mathcal{G} = U \rtimes G`$ with $`\pi(\mathcal{K}) = K`$, $`\mathcal{K} = \bigsqcup_{k \in K} (F_k, k)`$ ($`0_U \in F_{1_G}`$) and $`H \le H_1 := \pi(\mathcal{H}) \le G`$. Then $`W_1 := F_{1_G}`$ is an **$`\mathbb{F}_p`$-linear subspace** of $`U`$ (in sharp contrast to `N07E` §5 at $`p = 2`$). If $`|K|`$ is odd, then $`F_k = \psi_0(k) + k^{1/2} W_1`$ for all $`k \in K`$, $`\dim_{\mathbb{F}_p} U - \dim_{\mathbb{F}_p}(\mathcal{H} \cap U) - d = \dim_{\mathbb{F}_p} W_1`$ ($`[H_1 : H] = p^d`$), and $`H_1 = H`$ with $`|K| > 1`$ and $`|W_1| > 1`$ always produces a proper nontrivial normal subloop $`\mathcal{K} \cap U \mathcal{H} \trianglelefteq \mathcal{Q}`$ of order $`|W_1|`$.
2. **Proposed single-factor deformation dichotomy and absorption over $`Q_{p,q}`$ (unproved)**: Over the odd-order folder $`(G, H, K)`$ of $`Q_{p,q}`$ (`C0003`, odd prime $`p \ge 3`$, prime $`q \mid \frac{p^p - 1}{p - 1}`$), every candidate faithful lift $`(\mathcal{G}, \mathcal{H}, \mathcal{K})`$ on a direct sum $`U`$ of single-factor irreducible $`\mathbb{F}_p[G / O_p(G)]`$-modules ($`M_{r,0}, M_{0,r}`$ of $`\mathbb{F}_p`$-dimension $`p`$, plus optional trivial summands) either fails generation $`\langle \mathcal{K} \rangle = \mathcal{G}`$, violates transversality, collapses to a non-simple loop, or is canonically absorbed via folder isomorphism into the multi-module exact-factorization family $`Q_{p,q;R}`$ below.
3. **Proposed multi-module solvable-$`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits`$ simple family $`Q_{p,q;R}`$ of order $`p^{k p + 1} q`$ (not certified by this audit)**: Let $`p \ge 2`$ be a prime (odd order for all odd $`p \ge 3`$), $`q`$ a prime dividing $`\frac{p^p - 1}{p - 1}`$, $`\mathcal{O} := (\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z})^\times / \langle p \rangle \cong C_{(q-1)/p}`$, and $`R = \{[r_1], \dots, [r_k]\} \subseteq \mathcal{O}`$ any nonempty subset of size $`1 \le k \le \frac{q-1}{p}`$ ($`k = 1`$ recovers $`Q_{p,q}`$ of `C0003`). Set $`M_R := \bigoplus_{\ell=1}^k M_{r_\ell}`$ ($`M_r = \mathbb{F}_{p^p}`$ with $`a \in C_q`$ acting by $`\zeta^{r a}`$ and $`\phi(z) = z^p`$), $`\widetilde{X}_R := M_R \rtimes (C_q \rtimes \langle \phi \rangle)`$, $`\widetilde{Y}_1 := C_q \rtimes \langle \phi \rangle`$, and
   $$\widetilde{Y}_0 := \Bigl\{\bigl(m_1, \dots, m_k;\; 0, j\bigr) \in \widetilde{X}_R : m_\ell \in M_{r_\ell},\; j \in \mathbb{F}_p,\; \sum_{\ell=1}^k \mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\nolimits_{\mathbb{F}_{p^p}/\mathbb{F}_p}(m_\ell) = j\Bigr\}.$$
   Then $`Q_{p,q;R} := Q_{(\widetilde{X}_R, \widetilde{Y}_0, \widetilde{Y}_1)}`$ is a **finite simple proper left Bol $`G`$-loop of order $`p^{k p + 1} q`$** with **solvable** $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q_{p,q;R})`$ of order $`p^{2 k p + 1} q^2`$, $`N_\lambda = N_\mu = \{1\}`$, $`N_\rho(Q_{p,q;R}) \cong C_p`$, $`|\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(Q_{p,q;R})| = p \cdot |\mathop{\mathrm{Stab}}\nolimits_{\mathcal{O}}(R)|`$, and $`Q_{p,q;R} \cong Q_{p,q;R'} \iff R' = [s] \cdot R`$ for some $`[s] \in \mathcal{O}`$ (even $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q_{p,q;R}) \not\cong \mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q_{p,q;R'})`$ across distinct $`\mathcal{O}`$-orbits when $`p \ge 3`$). At $`(p, q) = (3, 13), k = 2`$, this yields **two** non-isomorphic odd-order simple proper left Bol $`G`$-loops $`Q_{3,13;\{1,2\}}`$ ($`|\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits| = 3`$) and $`Q_{3,13;\{1,4\}}`$ ($`|\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits| = 6`$) of order $`3^7 \cdot 13 = 28{,}431`$ with $`\mathop{\mathrm{rank}}\nolimits(\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits) = 2`$.

## Hypotheses

Odd primes $`p \ge 3`$ in (1)–(2), with $`U`$ restricted to direct sums of single-factor irreducibles $`M_{r,0}, M_{0,r}`$ (and trivial summands) over $`Q_{p,q}`$ in (2); arbitrary primes $`p \ge 2`$, primes $`q \mid \frac{p^p - 1}{p - 1}`$, and nonempty $`R \subseteq \mathcal{O}`$ in (3). Completely `CFSG`-free (`assumptions = []`).

## Proof

The fiber linearity, affine-fiber formula and dimension count in [§1 of the proof](../sources/foundations/C0013.md) hold under the displayed hypotheses. The normal-subloop conclusion also requires the explicit condition $`|K|>1`$. If the base is trivial, take $`G=H=K=1`$, $`U=\mathbb F_p`$, and the lifted folder $`(U,\{0\},U)`$: its $`W_1=U`$ is the whole loop, not a proper normal subloop.

The proposed dichotomy and absorption in part 2 remain unproved, even for simple lifts. The cited inverting-automorphism result requires a trivial left nucleus, the argument does not establish that its Bol sections are group cocycles or classify them, and the single-pair calculation does not settle arbitrary multiplicities and isotypic subspaces. The argument in §3 is retained as a proposed construction and classification, without asserting that this audit validates it. In particular, its C0012 invocation and the complete section require separate review. Neither the restricted lift proposal nor this family establishes exhaustiveness for all odd-order simple Bol loops.

Independent audit by the `audit_odd_module_rigidity` agent examined source revision [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/08e0fbb08fcc9b7da728e2ae6558695992ca2682) for the fiber argument and the absorption proof. Its findings are incorporated here; this is not an acceptance review of the revised claim or of part 3.

The additional faithful-lift, stabilizer, tiling and trivial-summand lemmas in the proof have a scoped independent review by contributor 1 at [the source revision](https://github.com/Mira-Cult/math-wip/commit/71f7eae94a6d7ce43563047929bd0be7e8fe1285). The full absorption and classification assertions remain partial.
