# 0012: Universal Imprimitivity and G-Loop Theorem for Exact-Factorization Left Bol Loops and Complete 20-Class Principal-Isotope Classification of Q_1536

Mira Cult · Claim family 0012

**Status:** proved. **Review status:** unreviewed. Aggregate claim not reviewed; section-level checks do not certify the whole claim.

**Dependencies:** [0001](0001.md), [0002](0002.md), [0006](0006.md)

## Statement

1. Universal imprimitivity of exact-factorization $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$: For any group $`X`$ with an exact factorization $`X = Y_0 Y_1`$ ($`Y_0 \cap Y_1 = \{1_X\}`$, $`|Y_0|, |Y_1| \ge 2`$), the left multiplication group $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)})`$ acts imprimitively on $`Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}`$ via a nontrivial block system of $`|Y_1|`$ blocks of size $`|Y_0|`$. In particular, $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q_{60}) \cong A_5 \times A_5`$ acts imprimitively on $`Q_{60} = Q_{(A_5, C_5, A_4)}`$ with stabilizer $`C_5 \times A_4`$ (not of primitive diagonal type).
2. **Universal $`G`$-loop theorem for exact-factorization left Bol loops**: Every exact-factorization left Bol loop $`Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}`$ (of arbitrary order and exponent) is a **$`G`$-loop** (isomorphic to all of its loop isotopes). Consequently, no left Bol loop whose isotopy class contains $`\ge 2`$ isomorphism classes can be realized by an exact factorization $`X = Y_0 Y_1`$.
3. Complete $`20`$-class isotopy classification of $`Q_{1536}`$: Nagy's deformed exponent-$`2`$ simple proper left Bol loop $`Q_{1536}`$ ([C0005.md](0005.md); [N07E](../REFERENCES.md#n07e) Theorem 5.2, $`k = 1`$, $`|\mathcal{G}| = 983{,}040`$) satisfies $`N_\rho(Q_{1536}) = \{1\}`$, $`Z(\mathcal{G}) = \{1\}`$, and $`|\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(Q_{1536})| = 1280`$. Its isotopy class decomposes into exact count $`20`$ pairwise non-isomorphic simple proper left Bol loops of order $`1536`$ ($`1 + 3 \cdot 5 + 6 \cdot 10 + 9 \cdot 20 + 1280 = 1536`$, none realizable by an exact factorization, all having $`|N_\lambda| = |N_\mu| = |N_\rho| = 1`$ and $`\mathop{\mathrm{RMlt}}\nolimits = \mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits = A_{1536}`$):
   - $`1`$ left Bruck loop of exponent $`2`$ ($`Q_{1536}`$ itself, $`|\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits| = 1280`$);
   - $`18`$ pairwise non-isomorphic **non-Bruck** simple proper left Bol $`2`$-loops of exponent $`4`$ ($`3`$ with $`|\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits| = 256`$, $`6`$ with $`|\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits| = 128`$, and $`9`$ with $`|\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits| = 64`$);
   - $`1`$ **automorphism-rigid** ($`|\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits| = 1`$) non-Bruck simple proper left Bol loop of maximum element order $`6`$ and exponent $`12`$ (orbit size $`1280`$).

## Hypotheses

Arbitrary groups $`X = Y_0 Y_1`$ ($`Y_0 \cap Y_1 = \{1_X\}`$) in (1)–(2), with $`|Y_0|, |Y_1| \ge 2`$ in (1); the concrete order-$`1536`$ deformed folder $`(\mathcal{G}, \mathcal{H}, \mathcal{K})`$ of `C0005` Theorem 2.1 ($`k = 1`$) in (3). Completely `CFSG`-free (`assumptions = []`).
