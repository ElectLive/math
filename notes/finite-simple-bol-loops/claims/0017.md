## Statement

Assuming the Classification of Finite Simple Groups (`CFSG`):
1. **Complete `#0323/S1` Rank-3 Exclusion Theorem**: **No** finite simple proper (non-Moufang) left Bol loop $`(Q, *)`$ satisfies $`\mathop{\mathrm{rank}}\nolimits(\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q)) = 3`$. Equivalently, among all finite simple nonassociative left Bol loops $`Q`$, $`\mathop{\mathrm{rank}}\nolimits(\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q)) = 3`$ holds **if and only if** $`Q`$ is isomorphic to Paige's simple Moufang loop $`M(2)`$ of order $`120`$ or $`M(3)`$ of order $`1080`$. Consequently, any counterexample to Johnson–Smith `#0323/S1` must satisfy $`\mathop{\mathrm{rank}}\nolimits(\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q)) \ge 4`$.
2. **Prime-Power Order Rank-2 Theorem**: Let $`(Q, *)`$ be any finite loop of prime-power order $`|Q| = p^a`$ ($`p`$ prime, $`a \ge 1`$) whose full multiplication group $`M = \mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$ acts primitively of Almost-Simple (`AS`) type on $`Q`$. Then $`M`$ acts **$`2`$-transitively** on $`Q`$ ($`\mathop{\mathrm{rank}}\nolimits(\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q)) = 2`$). In particular, when $`|Q| = 2^a \ge 4`$ is a power of $`2`$, $`\mathop{\mathrm{Soc}}\nolimits(M) \in \{A_{2^a}, \mathop{\mathrm{PSL}}\nolimits_2(2^a - 1)\}`$ (where $`\mathop{\mathrm{PSL}}\nolimits_2(2^a - 1)`$ occurs only when $`2^a - 1`$ is an odd prime power).

## Hypotheses

Finite simple nonassociative left Bol loops $`(Q, *)`$ (Part 1) and finite loops of prime-power order $`p^a`$ with `AS`-primitive $`\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$ (Part 2), conditional on `CFSG` (`assumptions = ["CFSG"]`, invoked for completeness of the finite primitive rank-$`3`$ `AS` census and Guralnick's prime-power-index simple-group classification; the replacement $`\mathop{\mathrm{Sp}}\nolimits_4(q)`$ exclusion also uses the CFSG-dependent Bruck-envelope structure theorem).
