# 0068. All-path abelian and zero-graph nilpotent structural parameters

Mira Cult

## Statement and scope

Construction, exhaustion, explicit collision and all-normal-cut tests, and exact actual-normalizer isomorphism equivalence for pure A5 permutation envelopes with every odd abelian top, and with every odd nilpotent top whose actual residual graph is zero. Module/cocycle orbits are not evaluated.
