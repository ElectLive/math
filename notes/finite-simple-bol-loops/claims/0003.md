## Statement

1. **Infinite prime-power semilinear solvable-`LMlt` family $`Q_{p,q}`$**: Let $`p \ge 2`$ be a prime, $`V = \mathbb{F}_{p^p}`$, $`\phi(z) = z^p`$, and $`q`$ a prime dividing $`p^p - 1`$. In $`X_{p,q} = V \rtimes (C_q \rtimes \langle \phi \rangle) \le \mathop{\mathrm{A\Gamma L}}\nolimits_1(p^p)`$ ($`|X_{p,q}| = p^{p+1} q`$), set $`Y_1 = C_q \rtimes \langle \phi \rangle`$ and $`Y_0^{(c)} = \langle \ker(\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\nolimits_{\mathbb{F}_{p^p}/\mathbb{F}_p}), (v_c, \phi) \rangle`$ ($`\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\nolimits(v_c) = c \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times`$). Then:
   - Each $`Y_0^{(c)}`$ ($`c \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times`$) is a regular subgroup of order $`p^p`$ ($`Y_0^{(c)} \neq V`$) giving an exact factorization $`X_{p,q} = Y_0^{(c)} Y_1`$, and as $`Y_0`$ ranges over all $`q(p - 1)`$ non-translation regular subgroups of $`X_{p,q}`$, all valid triples $`(X_{p,q}, Y_0, Y_1')`$ yield a single loop $`Q_{p,q}`$ up to isomorphism.
   - If $`q \mid p - 1`$, then $`Q_{(X_{p,q}, Y_0^{(c)}, Y_1)}`$ is **not simple** (it admits a proper normal subloop of index $`q`$).
   - If $`q \mid \frac{p^p - 1}{p - 1}`$ (recovering $`Q_{24}`$ at $`(2, 3)`$ and odd-order $`Q_{1053}`$ at $`(3, 13)`$, and yielding odd-order loops for all odd $`p`$), then $`Q_{p,q}`$ is a **finite simple proper left Bol $`G`$-loop of order $`p^{p+1} q`$** with **solvable** $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q_{p,q})`$ of order $`p^{2p+1} q^2`$, $`N_\rho(Q_{p,q}) \cong C_p`$, and $`\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(Q_{p,q}) \cong C_p`$.
2. **Solvable-wreath simple loop $`Q_{1152}`$**: In $`X = S_4 \wr C_2 \le S_8`$ ($`|X| = 1152 = 2^7 \cdot 3^2`$, $`O_2(X) \cong V_4 \times V_4`$), $`Y_1 = S_3 \wr C_2`$ ($`|Y_1| = 72`$) and $`Y_0 \cong \mathop{\mathrm{SD}}\nolimits_{16}`$ ($`|Y_0| = 16`$, $`Y_0 \cap O_2(X) = Z(Y_0) \cong C_2`$) define a **finite simple proper left Bol $`G`$-loop $`Q_{1152}`$** with solvable $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q_{1152})`$ of order $`331{,}776 = 2^{12} \cdot 3^4`$ lying strictly outside the family $`Q_{p,q}`$ (and unique up to isomorphism among core-free exact factorizations of $`S_4 \wr C_2`$).

## Hypotheses

Primes $`p \ge 2`$ and $`q \mid p^p - 1`$ in (1); $`X = S_4 \wr C_2`$ in (2). Completely CFSG-free.

## Proof

Complete proofs of Theorems 1.1 and 2.1 are given in [C0003.md](../sources/foundations/C0003.md): when $`q \mid \frac{p^p - 1}{p - 1}`$, irreducibility of $`V`$ under $`C_q`$ reduces intransitive normal subgroups of $`\hat{G}`$ to $`V \times V`$ and $`V \times X'`$, where $`[\hat{G} : \hat{H} N] = q < p q = |S N / N|`$ fails `0323-finite-simple-non-moufang-bol-loops:C0001` Theorem 2.2(3); uniqueness and $`\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(Q_{p,q}) \cong C_p`$ follow from transitive action of $`N_{\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(X)}(Y_1) \cong (C_q \times \mathbb{F}_p^\times) \rtimes C_p`$ on the $`q(p - 1)`$ regular subgroups $`Y_0 \neq V`$; and for $`Q_{1152}`$, transitive $`\langle (t, t), \sigma \rangle`$-action on the four $`V_4`$- and $`C_3`$-summands reduces intransitive $`\sigma`$-invariant normal subgroups to $`N = V_{16} \times V_{16}`$, where $`[\hat{G} : \hat{H} N] = 9 < 72 = |S N / N|`$.
