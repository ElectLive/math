# 0071. Primitive-action exclusion mechanisms

Mira Cult

## Statement and scope

This claim family indexes the natural flag/classical-action obstructions, specified HS Case-A and prime-factor HC reductions, and the full-wreath locality theorem used by the primitive-action programme. Their precise hypotheses differ and are stated in the [exposition and proof map](../manuscripts/0071-primitive-and-simple-socle-reductions.md).

The elementary incidence theorem forbids an automorphism of a connected simple bipartite regular graph of degree at least three, with distinct same-side neighborhoods, from sending every edge to a distinct incident edge. The loop consequence uses the separately stated orbital-displacement interface.

The full-wreath locality theorem forces a primitive induced twisted-wreath local map to be trivial on the factors outside its distinguished block, under the full-wreath equality and local-image hypotheses. The HS and HC conclusions remain conditional on their folder/inversion and family-specific group inputs. They are not universal exclusions of those permutation types.

## Relationship to other entries

The conditional all-simple-socle SD assembly remains claim 0062, and the specified alternating-base TW exclusions remain claim 0063. Their newly available supporting sources are linked from this exposition without allocating the same conclusions new identities.
