## Statement

Let $`(Q, *)`$ be a finite nonassociative left Bol loop with left multiplication group $`G = \mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$, point stabilizer $`H = \mathop{\mathrm{LInn}}\nolimits(Q) = G_{1_Q}`$, and canonical transversal $`K = \{L_x : x \in Q\}`$, and suppose that $`G`$ acts **primitively** on $`Q`$ (addressing Nagy `CMUC 2008` Problem 2). Without invoking `CFSG`:
1. **Universal loop-simplicity and external twisted semidirect product**: $`Q`$ is automatically loop-simple with $`N_\lambda(Q) = \{1_Q\}`$ and Almost-Simple full multiplication group $`\mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$. Setting $`\widetilde{G}_{\mathrm{ext}} := G \rtimes_\tau \langle t \rangle \cong G \rtimes C_2`$ ($`t \notin G`$, $`t^2 = 1`$, $`t g t = \tau(g) = k^{-1}`$ on $`K`$) and $`\Lambda := t K \subseteq t G`$, every $`\lambda \in \Lambda`$ is an involution, $`t \in \Lambda`$, $`|\Lambda| = |Q| = [G : H]`$, $`\langle \Lambda \rangle = \widetilde{G}_{\mathrm{ext}}`$, $`\Lambda`$ is a union of $`\widetilde{G}_{\mathrm{ext}}`$-conjugacy classes (`\lambda_1 \lambda_2 \lambda_1 = t(k_1 k_2^{-1} k_1) \in \Lambda`), $`H \cap \Lambda^2 = \{1_G\}`$, and $`\Lambda`$ cannot lie in a single coset of any index-$`2`$ normal subgroup $`G_+ \trianglelefteq G`$.
2. **Complete `CFSG`-free elimination of Holomorph Affine (`HA`) and Simple Diagonal `SD_2`**:
   - **(a) Type `HA`**: For every prime $`p \ge 2`$ and every $`d \ge 1`$, **no** finite nonassociative left Bol loop has $`G = \mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$ acting primitively of Holomorph Affine (`HA`) type on $`Q`$.
   - **(b) Type `SD_2`**: **No** finite left Bol loop has $`G = \mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$ acting primitively of Simple Diagonal type `SD_2` ($`\mathop{\mathrm{Soc}}\nolimits(G) = T_1 \times T_2 \cong T^2`$ minimal normal in $`G`$) on $`Q`$.
3. **Holomorph Simple (`HS`, $`G = (T_1 \times T_2)\Delta(A)`$, $`T_i \trianglelefteq G`$, $`|Q| = |T|`$)**:
   - **(a) Factor-swapping Case B ($`\tau(T_1) = T_2, \tau(T_2) = T_1`$)**: Any `HS` Case-B folder forces $`G = T \times T`$, $`H = \Delta(T)`$, and $`K = K_\theta := \{(u, \theta(u)^{-1}) : u \in T\}`$ for an automorphism $`\theta \in \mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(T)`$, and $`(T \times T, \Delta(T), K_\theta)`$ is a faithful left Bol folder **if and only if** $`\theta(v) \notin (v^{-1})^T`$ for every $`1_T \neq v \in T`$. In particular, no `HS` Case-B folder exists whenever $`T`$ possesses an $`\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(T)`$-invariant conjugacy class of involutions.
   - **(b) Factor-preserving Case A ($`\tau(T_1) = T_1, \tau(T_2) = T_2`$)**: Writing $`(\alpha_1(\lambda), \alpha_2(\lambda)) \in \mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(T)^2`$ for the involution pair induced on $`(T_1, T_2)`$ by $`\lambda \in \Lambda`$ and $`\mathcal{C}(\alpha) := (\alpha^T)^2 = \{u \alpha(w) w^{-1} u^{-1} : u, w \in T\} \subseteq T`$, there exists $`\lambda_* \in \Lambda`$ with **both** $`\alpha_1(\lambda_*) \neq \mathrm{id}_T`$ and $`\alpha_2(\lambda_*) \neq \mathrm{id}_T`$ satisfying
     $$|C_T(\alpha_1(\lambda_*))| \cdot |C_T(\alpha_2(\lambda_*))| \ge |T| \qquad \text{and} \qquad \mathcal{C}(\alpha_1(\lambda_*)) \cap \mathcal{C}(\alpha_2(\lambda_*)) = \{1_T\}.$$
     Consequently (`100% CFSG-free`), **no** `HS` Case-A left Bol folder exists when $`T = A_m`$ for **any** $`m \ge 5`$, or when $`T`$ is **any** finite nonabelian simple group possessing a single conjugacy class of involutions.
4. **Holomorph Compound (`HC`), Product Action (`PA`), and Compound / Diagonal / Twisted Wreath (`SD_{k \ge 3}`, `CD`, `TW`)**:
   - **(a) Complete `CFSG`-free elimination of `HC` Case B**: In type `HC` ($`B = B_1 \times B_2`$, $`B_i \cong T^\ell`$ minimal normal in $`G`$, $`\ell \ge 2`$), the factor-swapping case $`\tau(B_1) = B_2`$ has **zero** solutions (`K \subseteq B \lneq G`).
   - **(b) Universal factor-swapping involution bound**: In types `SD_k` ($`k \ge 3`$), `HC` (Case A), `CD`, `PA`, and `TW` with $`\mathop{\mathrm{Soc}}\nolimits(G) \cong T^m`$ ($`m \ge 2`$), there exists $`\lambda_* \in \Lambda`$ permuting the $`m`$ simple factors as a nontrivial involution ($`r \ge 1`$ transpositions and $`f = m - 2r`$ fixed factors) with $`|T|^r \prod_{\pi_*(s) = s} [T : C_T(\alpha_s)] \le [G : H]`$.
   - **(c) `PA` double-coset exclusion**: In type `PA` ($`H \cap B = R^\ell`$, $`1_T \neq R \lneq T`$, degree $`d^\ell`$ with $`d = [T : R]`$), the transposition isomorphism $`\theta : T_1 \xrightarrow{\sim} T_2`$ induced by $`\lambda_*`$ satisfies $`R \cap g \theta(R) g^{-1} = \{1_T\}`$ for all $`g \in T`$, forcing $`|R|^2 \le |T|`$ and unconditionally excluding **every** `PA` action with component degree $`d < |T|^{1/2}`$, as well as every `PA` action where $`R`$ meets an $`\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(T)`$-invariant involution class $`\mathcal{I}_0 \subset T`$. Likewise, any $`\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(T)`$-invariant involution class $`\mathcal{I}_0`$ excludes `SD_{2r}` with a fixed-point-free factor-involution $`\lambda_* \in \Lambda`$ and `HC` Case A with a shared transposed index pair in $`\pi_1(\lambda_*), \pi_2(\lambda_*)`$.
