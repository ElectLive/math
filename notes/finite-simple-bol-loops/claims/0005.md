## Statement

1. **Wreath-socle exact-factorization dichotomy and infinite family on $`T \wr C_2`$**: Let $`X = T \wr P = B \rtimes P`$ ($`T`$ finite nonabelian simple, $`P \le S_r`$ transitive with $`r \ge 2`$, $`B = T^r`$) have a core-free exact factorization $`X = Y_0 Y_1`$ ($`Y_0, Y_1 \neq 1`$). If $`B = (B \cap Y_0)(B \cap Y_1)`$, then $`Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}`$ is **not simple** (having proper nontrivial normal subloop $`Q_{(B, B \cap Y_0, B \cap Y_1)}`$). If $`B Y_0 = B Y_1 = X`$, then $`Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}`$ is a **finite simple proper left Bol $`G`$-loop** with $`\mathop{\mathrm{Soc}}\nolimits(\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q)) \cong T^{2r}`$ ($`2r \ge 4`$). This case is realized infinitely often on $`T \wr C_2`$ whenever $`T = A_0 A_1`$ is an exact factorization with $`[A_0 : M_0] = 2`$, including $`T = \mathop{\mathrm{PSL}}\nolimits_2(q)`$ for all prime powers $`q \equiv 3 \pmod 4`$, $`q \ge 7`$ (smallest order $`56{,}448`$ at $`q = 7`$) and $`T = A_{4k}`$ for all $`k \ge 2`$.
2. **Universal `LMlt` evenness**: Every finite simple exact-factorization left Bol loop $`Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}`$ with $`|Q| > 2`$ satisfies $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q) \le A_{|Q|}`$.
3. **Strict cross-family non-isotopy**: Up to permutation isomorphism, $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q), \mathop{\mathrm{RMlt}}\nolimits(Q), \mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q) \le \mathop{\mathrm{Sym}}\nolimits(Q)`$ are loop-isotopy invariants, and no loop in any one of the five constructive families — (1) solvable-`LMlt` (`C0003`), (2) almost-simple (`C0002`), (3) affine-simple/quasisimple (`C0004`), (4) transitive-socle wreath (`C0005`), and (5) `N07E` exponent-$`2`$ deformations $`Q_{96 \cdot 16^k}^{\mathrm{op}}`$ and their isotopes — is loop-isotopic to a loop in any other.

## Hypotheses

Finite groups and loops as stated in (1)–(3). Completely CFSG-free.

## Proof

Self-contained proofs of Theorems 1.1, 2.1, and 3.1 are given in [C0005.md](../sources/foundations/C0005.md): transitive action of $`\langle S, \sigma \rangle`$ on the $`2r`$ simple factors of $`B \times B`$ forces $`B \times B \le \mathop{\mathrm{Core}}\nolimits_{\hat{G}}(U)`$ and $`\hat{H}\mathop{\mathrm{Core}}\nolimits_{\hat{G}}(U) = \hat{G}`$ whenever $`B Y_0 = B Y_1 = X`$; product-action sign computation $`\mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\nolimits_Q(u, v) = \mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\nolimits_{X/Y_0}(u)^{|Y_0|} \mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\nolimits_{X/Y_1}(v)^{|Y_1|}`$ plus $`X' Y_0 = X`$ proves $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q) \le A_{|Q|}`$; and solvability, $`\mathop{\mathrm{Fit}}\nolimits(G)`$, simple-factor count of $`\mathop{\mathrm{Soc}}\nolimits(G)`$, and $`(G / O_p(G))^{\mathrm{ab}}`$ separate all five families up to isotopy.
