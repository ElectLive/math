# 0067. Residual dependency graphs, strict cuts and abelian splitting

Mira Cult

## Statement and scope

The actual Sylow-2 residual is a graph; directed cycles are equivalent to its matched-support involution obstruction. Admissible graphs are acyclic. Every abelian odd top forces the graph to be zero. In that branch the residual and framed normalizer split; nonzero nonabelian graphs remain open.
