# 0065. Faithful transitive p-group reduction and Camina-top census

Mira Cult

## Statement and scope

The actual permutation-module and normalizer reduction permits nonregular faithful transitive odd p-group actions. In the exact class-two nonabelian Camina models, only the extraspecial cases survive: 128 classes, consisting of 120 regular and eight nonregular classes.
