# 0015: Universal Aut(T)-Invariant Involution Class Theorem and Elimination of Factor-Swapping HS (Case B) Primitive LMlt(Q) Under CFSG

Mira Cult · Claim family 0015

**Status:** conditional. **Review status:** unreviewed. Aggregate claim not reviewed; section-level checks do not certify the whole claim.

**Dependencies:** [0001](0001.md), [0007](0007.md), [0014](0014.md)

## Statement

Assuming the Classification of Finite Simple Groups (`CFSG`):
1. **Existence of an $`\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(T)`$-invariant involution class**: Every finite nonabelian simple group $`T`$ possesses at least one $`\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(T)`$-invariant conjugacy class $`\mathcal{I}_0 \subset T`$ of involutions.
2. **Complete elimination of `HS` Case B and `PA` / `SD_{2r}` / `HC` Case-A invariant-involution regimes**: Let $`(Q, *)`$ be a finite nonassociative left Bol loop with $`G = \mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$ acting primitively on $`Q`$. Then:
   - **(a)** **No** Holomorph Simple (`HS`) factor-swapping (**Case B**: $`\tau(T_1) = T_2, \tau(T_2) = T_1`$) left Bol loop exists for any finite nonabelian simple group $`T`$;
   - **(b)** **No** Product Action (`PA`) left Bol loop exists whose component stabilizer $`1_T \neq R \lneq T`$ satisfies $`R \cap \mathcal{I}_0 \neq \emptyset`$ for an $`\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(T)`$-invariant involution class $`\mathcal{I}_0 \subset T`$;
   - **(c)** **No** Simple Diagonal `SD_{2r}` left Bol loop admits a fixed-point-free factor-involution $`\lambda_* \in \Lambda`$, and **no** Holomorph Compound `HC` Case-A left Bol loop admits a twisted involution $`\lambda_* \in \Lambda`$ whose factor permutations $`\pi_1(\lambda_*), \pi_2(\lambda_*) \in S_\ell`$ share a transposed index pair (in particular whenever $`\pi_1(t) = \pi_2(t)`$).

## Hypotheses

Finite nonassociative left Bol loops $`(Q, *)`$ with primitive left multiplication group $`G = \mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$, conditional on `CFSG` (`assumptions = ["CFSG"]`, used solely to exhaust finite nonabelian simple groups $`T`$ in Part 1).
