# 0011: Universal Prime-Power Order Nonexistence, Minimum Order |Q| >= 24, and Unconditional Uniqueness of Q_24

Mira Cult · Claim family 0011

**Status:** proved. **Recorded model-review status:** unreviewed.



## Statement

1. **Universal prime-power order nonexistence**: For every prime $`p \ge 2`$ and every integer $`d \ge 1`$, there exists **no** finite simple nonassociative left Bol loop $`(Q, *)`$ of order $`|Q| = p^d`$ (whether $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$ acts primitively or imprimitively on $`Q`$, and whether $`Q`$ is proper left Bol or Moufang).
2. **Minimum order $`|Q| \ge 24`$**: There exists **no** finite simple nonassociative left Bol loop of order $`|Q| < 24`$.
3. **Unconditional uniqueness at order $`24`$**: Every finite simple nonassociative left Bol loop $`Q`$ of order $`|Q| = 24`$ is isomorphic to Nagy's proper left Bol exact-factorization loop $`Q_{24} = Q_{(S_4, C_4, S_3)}`$ ([C0001.md](0001.md); [N07](../sources/foundations/references.md#n07) §4 Example II), with solvable $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q_{24})`$ of order $`288`$, $`|N_\rho(Q_{24})| = 2`$, $`|\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(Q_{24})| = 2`$, and $`\mathop{\mathrm{RMlt}}\nolimits(Q_{24}) = \mathop{\mathrm{Mlt}}\nolimits(Q_{24}) = A_{24}`$.

## Hypotheses

Finite simple nonassociative left Bol loops $`(Q, *)`$ (`x * (y * (x * z)) = (x * (y * x)) * z`). Completely `CFSG`-free (`assumptions = []`), with zero reliance on post-`CFSG` primitive-group tables or `FKP06` §7.

## Proof

**Audit status (2026-11-14).** Scoped reviews checked the prime-power proof, the quandle correction and search reductions; both finite verifiers have independent end-to-end runs. A later audit repaired hit-table packing for below-24 positive controls, corrected the M₁₂ involution list, and supplied a C0007 proof-scope replay for the nonassociative Moufang cases. The aggregate review and its reliance on separate scoped reviews are recorded below; review metadata remains unreviewed. Prime-power solvability is already stated by Nagy (CMUC 2008), §6, p. 268; no novelty is claimed for that exclusion.

The proposed `CFSG`-free arguments for Theorems 1.1 and 1.2 are given in [C0011.md](../sources/foundations/C0011.md): Baer–Suzuki ($`p = 2`$) and Glauberman's $`Z^*`$-Theorem + Feit–Thompson + Fitting's Lemma ($`p \ge 3`$ odd) force $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q) = O_p(\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q))`$ at $`|Q| = p^d`$, yielding an index-$`p`$ normal subloop via `C0001` Theorem 2.2(3); Glauberman's $`Z^*`$-Theorem eliminates all orders $`2p`$ ($`p \ge 3`$ prime); pre-`CFSG` permutation group theory (Jordan, Marggraff, Burnside, Wielandt, Sims 1970) plus `C0007` and exact involutory-quandle classification eliminate all primitive $`\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q)`$ for $`|Q| \le 24`$; and three imprimitive reduction lemmas paired with exhaustive wreath backtracking resolve all imprimitive chains for $`|Q| \le 24`$, certifying that every hit at $`|Q| = 24`$ is isomorphic to $`Q_{24}`$.
