# 0069. Odd permutation-top PSL2 projective-tensor exclusions

Mira Cult

## Statement and scope

For odd prime powers q congruent to 1 modulo 4 outside 5, 9, 29, exclude the entire pure PSL2(q) factor-permutation model with odd top and surjective stabilizer image; in particular all odd nilpotent tops. General outer actions, mixed socles and nonsurjective tops are not covered.
