# 0066. Odd-top orbit pairing and opposite-endpoint paths

Mira Cult

## Statement and scope

In the pure A5-socle model, every odd nilpotent top has all factor orbits paired by canonical inversion. The pairing extends to arbitrary odd tops with surjective stabilizer image. Surviving full strips form two-coordinate paths with opposite endpoint types; unequal and nonregular orbits are included.
