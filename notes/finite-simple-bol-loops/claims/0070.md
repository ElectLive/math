# 0070: Bruck radical constraints and fixed-parameter schemes

- **Status:** Structural results with explicit envelope and representation dependencies; finite-at-fixed-parameters reduction, not a global classification.
- **Scope:** Finite simple nonassociative right Bruck loops with faithful generated Baer envelopes and the allowed product PGL₂ quotients. Additional hypotheses for central-Frattini and local chief-factor statements are stated separately in the manuscript.
- **Main assertions:** An arbitrary radical has dim(P/Φ(P))≤D and |P|≤2^(r+D) for the explicit D(A,r,m); a finite complete extension-data scheme exists at fixed A,r,m. Central Frattini implies vanishing of the stabilizer shadow core, conditional on the recorded representation interfaces. A local cohomological chief-factor obstruction excludes the stated configurations.
- **Proof:** [Reader-facing manuscript, including the arbitrary-radical bound proof](../manuscripts/0070-bruck-radical-constraints.md); [full proof-source index](../sources/bruck-completion/README.md).
- **Dependencies:** Baumeister–Stein Bruck-envelope structure; core-reduced Frattini bounds; for central-Frattini vanishing, the exact modular-representation interfaces in its proof source; for the local obstruction, the series, cohomology and fixed-space assumptions stated there.
- **Evidence boundary:** Source review and computational scope statements are preserved, with private review identities removed. This publication does not claim a new independent formal verification of all 69 source manuscripts. Restricted computations do not classify all loops of the same order.
- **Open parameters:** A,r,m in the arbitrary-radical case; A,m in the central-Frattini subclass. No global finite family list or completed Bol classification follows.
