# 0004: Affine-Simple and Quasisimple Exact-Factorization Family Q_{(V rtimes G_0, Y_0, G_0)}

**Dependencies:** [0001](0001.md), [0002](0002.md)

## Statement

Let $p$ be a prime and $V \cong \mathbb{F}_p^d$ ($d \ge 2$).
1. **Universal modular regular-subgroup criterion**: For any subgroup $G_0 \le \mathop{\mathrm{GL}}\nolimits(V)$, the semidirect product $X = V \rtimes G_0$ admits a non-translation regular subgroup $Y_0 \neq V$ ($|Y_0| = p^d$, $Y_0 \cap G_0 = 1$, giving $X = Y_0 G_0$) **if and only if $p \mid |G_0|$**.
2. **Simplicity and isomorphism theorem for faithful irreducible quasisimple $G_0$**: Whenever $G_0 \le \mathop{\mathrm{GL}}\nolimits(V)$ is faithful, irreducible, and quasisimple ($G_0 = G_0'$, $G_0 / Z(G_0)$ nonabelian simple) with $p \mid |G_0|$, every non-translation regular subgroup $Y_0 \neq V$ of $X = V \rtimes G_0$ yields a **finite simple proper left Bol $G$-loop $Q_{(X, Y_0, G_0)}$** of order $p^d |G_0|$ with $\mathop{\mathrm{LMlt}}\nolimits(Q) \cong X \times X$ and $N_\rho(Q) = N_{G_0}(Y_0)$. Two such loops $Q_{(V \rtimes G_0, Y_0, G_0)}$ and $Q_{(\widetilde{V} \rtimes \widetilde{G}_0, \widetilde{Y}_0, \widetilde{G}_0)}$ are isomorphic iff there exists a group isomorphism $\alpha : V \rtimes G_0 \xrightarrow{\sim} \widetilde{V} \rtimes \widetilde{G}_0$ sending $Y_0 \mapsto \widetilde{Y}_0$ and $G_0 \mapsto \widetilde{G}_0$, and $\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(Q_{(V \rtimes G_0, Y_0, G_0)}) \cong N_{N_{\mathop{\mathrm{GL}}\nolimits(V)}(G_0)}(Y_0)$.
3. **Infinite subfamilies and low-order censuses**: This produces infinite families on $\mathbb{F}_p^d \rtimes \mathop{\mathrm{SL}}\nolimits_d(p)$ ($d \ge 3$), $V_{\mathrm{del}} \rtimes A_n$ ($n \ge 5$, prime $p \le n$ with $p \nmid n$), $\mathbb{F}_{p^f}^d \rtimes \mathop{\mathrm{SL}}\nolimits_d(p^f)$ ($d \ge 2$, $(d, p^f) \notin \{(2,2), (2,3)\}$), and quasisimple $\mathbb{F}_p^2 \rtimes \mathop{\mathrm{SL}}\nolimits_2(p)$ ($p \ge 5$), including exactly **$5$** isomorphism classes at order $960$ ($3$ on $\mathbb{F}_4^2 \rtimes \mathop{\mathrm{SL}}\nolimits_2(4)$ and $2$ on $2^4 \rtimes A_5$) and **$7$** isomorphism classes at order $1344$ ($\mathbb{F}_2^3 \rtimes \mathop{\mathrm{SL}}\nolimits_3(2)$).

## Hypotheses

Prime $p$, dimension $d = \dim_{\mathbb{F}_p} V \ge 2$, and faithful irreducible quasisimple $G_0 \le \mathop{\mathrm{GL}}\nolimits(V)$ with $p \mid |G_0|$ in (2)–(3). Completely CFSG-free.
