# 0002: Exact-Factorization Left Bol Folders and Complete Isomorphism and CFSG Classification of the Nagy Almost-Simple Family

## Statement

Let $X$ be a finite group with a core-free exact factorization $X = Y_0 Y_1$ ($Y_0 \cap Y_1 = 1$, $Y_0, Y_1 \neq 1$, $\mathop{\mathrm{Core}}\nolimits_X(Y_i) = 1$), and let $Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}$ be the left Bol loop on $X$ defined by $x \circ y = (b_0 a_1)^{-1}$ where $(x y)^{-1} = a_0 a_1$ and $x^{-1} y^{-1} = b_0 b_1$ ($a_i, b_i \in Y_i$). Then:
1. **Reduced folder, properness, and $X' Y_0 = X' Y_1 = X$ necessity**: The subgroup $\hat{G} = \langle S \rangle = \{(u, v) \in X \times X : u v \in X'\}$ ($|\hat{G}| = |X| \cdot |X'|$, $\hat{H} = \hat{G} \cap (Y_0 \times Y_1)$) is a faithful left Bol folder whenever $X' Y_0 = X' Y_1 = X$, with $N_\rho(Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}) = N_X(Y_0) \cap N_X(Y_1)$; if $X' Y_i \lneq X$, then $X' Y_i \trianglelefteq Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}$ is a proper nontrivial normal subloop; and no simple exact-factorization loop $Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}$ ($Y_0, Y_1 \neq 1$) is Moufang.
2. **Simplicity and complete isomorphism theorem for almost-simple $X = T Y_0 = T Y_1$**: If $X$ is almost simple with nonabelian simple socle $T = \mathop{\mathrm{Soc}}\nolimits(X)$ and $X = T Y_0 = T Y_1$, then $Q_{(X, Y_0, Y_1)}$ is a finite simple proper left Bol $G$-loop. Moreover, for any two such triples $\mathcal{T} = (X, Y_0, Y_1)$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal{T}} = (\widetilde{X}, \widetilde{Y}_0, \widetilde{Y}_1)$, $Q_{\mathcal{T}} \cong Q_{\widetilde{\mathcal{T}}}$ iff there exists a group isomorphism $\alpha : X \xrightarrow{\sim} \widetilde{X}$ with $\{\alpha(Y_0), \alpha(Y_1)\} = \{\widetilde{Y}_0, \widetilde{Y}_1\}$, and $\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(Q_{\mathcal{T}}) \cong \mathop{\mathrm{Stab}}\nolimits_{\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(X)}(Y_0) \cap \mathop{\mathrm{Stab}}\nolimits_{\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(X)}(Y_1)$.
3. **CFSG parameter classification under $X = T Y_0 = T Y_1$**: Up to swapping $(Y_0, Y_1)$, the core-free almost-simple exact factorizations satisfying $X = T Y_0 = T Y_1$ are exhausted by the $6$ infinite families and the finite sporadic list in [C0002.md](0002.md).

## Hypotheses

Finite groups $X$ with core-free exact factorizations $X = Y_0 Y_1$ ($Y_0 \cap Y_1 = 1$, $Y_0, Y_1 \neq 1$). Part (3) invokes the Classification of Finite Simple Groups via the cited exact-factorization classifications `HLS87`, `Giu06`, `BL21`, `LWX23`, and `FLLWXZ25` (parts (1) and (2) are CFSG-free).
