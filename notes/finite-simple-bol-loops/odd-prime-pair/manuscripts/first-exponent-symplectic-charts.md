# Two-by-two charts for all noncanonical loops at the first two-prime degree

**Supporting parameterization.** [Scoped verification](../VERIFICATION.md). This is a finite parameter-and-equivalence classification, not a closed formula for the number of its orbits. The necessity depends on [the all-core minimum](all-core-prime-pair-minimum.md) 268180f2, [extraspecial first-degree rigidity](extraspecial-first-degree-rigidity.md) 9b897581 and their stated prerequisites. The sufficiency generalizes the [explicit extraspecial family](../../extraspecial-cores/extraspecial-odd-bol-construction.md) 9a22cb96, but does not assume that all stabilizer Lagrangians there are invariant under both q-axes. Those dependency and review scopes remain separate.

Fix distinct odd primes $`p,q`$, $`p\mid q-1`$, and let $`f=\mathrm{ord}_q(p)`$ be **even and prime to $`p`$**. Write $`d=pf`$, $`K=\mathbb F_{p^f}`$, $`\bar x=x^{p^{f/2}}`$, choose a primitive $`q`$-th root $`\zeta\in K`$ and an order-$`p`$ scalar $`\alpha\in\mathbb F_q^\times`$. Write $`S=\langle\alpha,p\bmod q\rangle\le\mathbb F_q^\times`$. Every $`a\in S`$ uniquely has form $`a=\alpha^r p^s`$ with $`r\bmod p,\ s\bmod f`$, by coprimality.

**Theorem.** At order $`p^{d+1}q`$, the standard abelian-core loop is one based isomorphism class. The other based isomorphism classes are exactly the following explicit finite charts modulo the equivalence below. Every chart that passes its indicated elementary linear tests gives an actual simple nonassociative left Bol loop; no loop table, candidate subgroup, future existence condition or final loop isomorphism oracle is hidden in the parameter list. The extra charts are nonempty (9a22cb96). For other admissible odd pairs, the separate [odd-$`f`$ result](extraspecial-first-degree-rigidity.md) and [case $`p\mid f`$](fixed-pair-minimum-rigidity.md) give the proposed single class at the least exponent. Nothing herein classifies larger degrees.

## 1. The charts

Use two field coordinates per site on $`U=(K^2)^{\mathbb Z/p}`$, so $`\dim_{\mathbb F_p}U=2d`$. A chart consists of:

1. Two labels $`a_1,a_2\in S`$ (repetition allowed), with $`a_i=\alpha^{r_i}p^{s_i}`$. Set three fixed operators on $`U`$: $`h`$ shifts the sites by $`(h x)_{i,j}=x_{i,j+1}`$; $`b_2`$ multiplies site $`j`$ by $`\zeta^{\alpha^{-j}}`$ on both coordinates; $`b_1`$ multiplies coordinate $`i`$ at site $`j`$ by $`\zeta^{a_i\alpha^j}`$. Thus $`h`$ acts on the first and second q-axis by the reciprocal scalars $`\alpha,\alpha^{-1}`$. These give a faithful action of $`X=(C_q\times C_q)\rtimes C_p`$ with no fixed points for $`b_2`$ on $`U`$ (other than zero).
2. An invertible two-by-two matrix $`M`$ over $`K`$ with $`\bar M^{\,T}=-M`$ and $`M_{ik}=0`$ if $`a_i\ne a_k`$. This gives the symplectic form
```math
 \omega(x,y)=\sum_j \mathrm{Tr}_{K/\mathbb F_p}(x_j^T M\overline{y_j}).
```
3. A two-by-two array $`C=(c_{ki})`$ over $`K`$. Set $`c_{ki}=0`$ unless $`a_k=\alpha^{r_i}p^{-s_i}`$. Define $`t`$ additively from a vector supported in coordinate $`i`$ at site $`j`$ with value $`x`$: the contribution in coordinate $`k`$ at site $`-j-r_i`$ is $`c_{ki}x^{p^{-s_i}}`$, where the Frobenius exponent is read modulo $`f`$. Require $`t^2=1`$ and $`\omega(tx,ty)=\omega(x,y)`$. It exchanges $`b_1,b_2`$ and inverts $`h`$ by the displayed rule.
4. A $`K`$-line $`\ell\le K^2`$ on which the skew-Hermitian form $`x^T M\bar y`$ vanishes, giving the Lagrangian $`L=\ell^{\mathbb Z/p}`$. Require $`L\cap tL=0`$, a rank test over $`\mathbb F_p`$. Finally choose $`\lambda\in\mathbb F_p^\times`$.

All parameter searches use two-by-two field matrices and a projective line; their tests require at most $`2pf`$ prime-field coordinates. For example if $`a_1=a_2=1`$, reversing the site indices on the second half of the $`m=1`$ construction at 9a22cb96 brings it to these charts. It is not assumed that $`b_1`$ stabilizes $`\ell`$.

**Equivalence.** Between two passing charts enumerate $`r=\alpha^u p^v\in S`$ and $`D\in\mathrm{GL}_2(K)`$. Let $`F`$ carry the vector $`x`$ at site $`j`$ to $`D x^{p^v}`$ at site $`j+u`$ in the other chart. Keep this transformation precisely when the following equalities hold:
```math
 F b_1=(b'_1)^r F,\qquad F t=t'F,\qquad F(L)=L',\qquad
 \omega'(Fx,Fy)=\nu\omega(x,y),\qquad \lambda'=\nu\lambda
```
for a scalar $`\nu\in\mathbb F_p^\times`$. The relations $`F b_2=(b'_2)^rF`$ and $`Fh=h'F`$ are built into $`F`$. Exchanging the arbitrary names of the two field coordinates is included by $`D`$, not a missing further case. This is **based loop isomorphism** modulo the finite tests, not merely a count of modules modulo change of basis.

## 2. Why every exceptional physical group has this form

By 9b897581, $`N`$ is extraspecial of exponent $`p`$ with center $`Z=C_p`$ fixed by the signed group, $`U=N/Z`$ is symplectic, and the true stabilizer image $`L\subset U`$ is a Lagrangian transverse to $`tL`$. Its $`t`$-negative and positive spaces have dimension $`d`$ each, both transverse to $`L`$. There are no $`B_2`$-fixed vectors on $`U`$. The complement $`B`$ above the two q-axes can be chosen $`t`$-stable. The actual fixed group $`C_N(B_2)=C_N(B)=Z`$, by coprime fixed-point lifting and the absence of fixed vectors. The normalizer of $`B`$ maps onto $`X`$ with kernel $`Z`$; it is $`B`$ extended by a $`t`$-stable Sylow $`p`$-group of order $`p^2`$. This latter group is abelian, $`t`$ fixes $`Z`$ and inverts its quotient, so it splits into $`Z`$ and an inverted cyclic factor of order $`p`$. Choose its generator $`h`$ giving $`\alpha`$ on the first axis. In particular an actual $`t`$-stable complement $`X`$ acts on $`N`$.

We spell out why the physical stabilizer can be aligned **by based gauges**. First choose the second q-axis to be the projected stabilizer axis (or interchange the original names). In the upper quotient write $`P`$ for the copy just chosen. The stabilizer image $`J`$ over that axis can be put at $`B_2\rtimes P`$ by conjugating by an element of $`B^t`$: the first-axis offset of its complement is eliminated by an arbitrary first-axis shift using $`\alpha\ne1`$, and the same second-axis shift has no effect on $`J`$ modulo its contained second axis. Next a Sylow $`q`$-subgroup of the true $`H`$ over $`B_2`$ can be aligned with $`B_2`$ by $`N`$-conjugacy inside $`N B_2`$. Such a conjugation on $`H`$ can be realized by an element fixed by $`t`$. Indeed $`N=(H\cap N) C_N(t)`$ since the images $`L,U^+`$ are complementary and $`Z\subset C_N(t)`$. A conjugator $`n=w c`$ with $`w\in H\cap N`$ and $`c\in C_N(t)`$ gives $`H^n=H^c`$ in the convention $`H^n=n^{-1}Hn`$. The linear projection and transversality are not changed by an inner $`N`$ adjustment. These steps do not merely identify isotopes.

In additive Heisenberg coordinates the action of $`X,t`$ has no unaccounted central linear terms. The class-two exponent-$`p`$ group has the canonical vector addition $`xy[x,y]^{-1/2}`$. Pick a $`B`$-invariant linear complement to $`Z`$ by coprime averaging. It is unique, because $`U`$ has no $`B`$-fixed linear functional. Thus it is also preserved by every normalizer of $`B`$, in particular $`h,t`$. Fixing a generator of $`Z`$ recovers the form $`\omega`$ and the multiplication with central term $`\omega/2`$. Now $`H\cap N`$ is the graph over $`L`$ of a linear functional; it is $`B_2`$-invariant, hence that functional vanishes. The normalizer of $`B_2`$ equals the normalizer of $`B`$ since both centralizers in $`N`$ are $`Z`$. The Frattini argument $`H=(H\cap N)N_H(B_2)`$ then gives
```math
             H=L\rtimes\langle b_2,z^\lambda h\rangle
```
for a scalar $`\lambda`$: over the generator of $`P`$ the only remaining choices are in $`Z B_2`$, and $`B_2`$ is already in $`H`$. Here and below $`L`$ in $`H`$ denotes its zero central lift. The scalar is nonzero since $`h`$ itself is inverted.

To obtain the two-by-two coordinates, $`U/L`$ is an irreducible moving $`J`$-module of dimension $`d`$, and $`L`$ is its dual via $`\omega`$. Because $`f`$ is even, the reciprocal q-characters are already Frobenius conjugate. After choosing the generator of the second q-axis and setting $`b_1=t b_2t`$, its nonzero weight support on $`U`$ is $`S`$, each with multiplicity **two**. The unique joint Frobenius and $`h`$ orbit on the quotient has size $`pf`$; normalization of its initial character by scaling $`b_2`$ gives this precise $`S`$. The $`p`$ rational q-constituent spaces are two-dimensional over $`K`$ (the $`q`$-action itself supplies that field structure); transport one basis around the sites by $`h`$ to obtain the plain cyclic shift. Consistency is exact since $`h^p=1`$. The commuting $`b_1`$ is $`K`$-linear at a site, diagonalizable because $`q\mid |K^\times|`$ and $`p\ne q`$. Diagonalize at one site and transport this basis; its two labels lie in $`S`$ because $`t`$ exchanges the q-axes and hence their whole weight supports. The conjugation by $`h`$ now gives precisely the chart's $`b_1`$ exponents.

No coordinate pairings occur between distinct sites: inverse $`b_2`$-weights are in the same Frobenius orbit and the site orbits are disjoint. At one site $`b_2`$-invariance makes the prime-field form sesquilinear after trace: the adjoint of multiplication by $`\zeta`$ is multiplication by $`\zeta^{-1}=\bar\zeta`$, which extends from the generator to all of $`K`$. By nondegenerate trace duality the form therefore has a unique matrix $`M`$ as in the formula. Alternation yields $`\bar M^T=-M`$, $`h`$ transports the same matrix to all sites, and $`b_1`$ forces the listed vanishing entries. The $`B_2`$-stable $`L`$ is a sum of $`K`$-subspaces at these sites; $`h`$ transports them and its dimension $`pf`$ makes them lines with the same coordinate description.

Finally any $`\mathbb F_p`$-linear intertwiner of the field scalar actions is semilinear between the matching Frobenius eigentypes. For $`t`$ to exchange $`b_1,b_2`$, an input in $`(i,j)`$ can contribute only at $`j'=-j-r_i`$, with Frobenius exponent $`-s_i`$ and target label $`a_k=\alpha^{r_i}p^{-s_i}`$. Indeed the two required exponent equalities are
```math
 (\alpha^{-j})^{[v]}=a_k\alpha^{j'},\qquad
 (a_i\alpha^j)^{[v]}=\alpha^{-j'},
 \quad u^{[v]}:=p^v u\pmod q.
```
The character-field action determines the Frobenius uniquely mod $`f`$, since the character is faithful and generates $`K`$. The relation $`th=h^{-1}t`$ makes the coefficients independent of $`j`$. This supplies exactly the stated $`C`$ ansatz, including multiple equal labels. No splitting over the modular group $`P`$ was presumed.

## 3. Every passing chart is sufficient, even for a moving Lagrangian

Build $`N=U\times\mathbb F_p`$ with central term $`\omega/2`$, let $`z`$ generate its center, put $`D=N\rtimes X`$, and $`H=L\rtimes\langle b_2,z^\lambda h\rangle`$ with signed $`t`$ and marks its full inverted set. The $`X`$ action is faithful; a single site-label orbit already detects both $`q`$-axes, because its exponents at sites 0,1 have linearly independent coefficients as $`\alpha\ne\alpha^{-1}`$. The $`q`$-axes have no fixed vectors on $`U`$. The complementary Lagrangians $`L,tL`$ give $`t`$-negative dimension $`d`$, and $`t`$ fixes the center. Thus $`|T|=p^{d+1}q=[D:H]`$. The marks generate: their images normally generate $`X`$ by $`h`$ and the anti-diagonal axes; commutators with $`N`$ then fill $`U=[U,V]`$, hence $`N`$ by Frattini. As every nontrivial normal subgroup of $`D`$ contains its disjoint-from-$`H`$ center $`Z(N)`$ (a normal subgroup disjoint from $`N`$ centralizes it, and $`C_D(N)=Z(N)`$), the action is faithful. Here and below this use of “center” means the specified central line, not that all of $`N`$ centralizes $`D`$.

For true marked-square avoidance write $`\mathcal C=Tt`$. Over any projected involution $`a\in Xt`$ all the true actual lifts are $`(u,0)a`$ with $`u\in E_a=\ker(1+a)`$. A product above $`ab`$ has vector summands in $`E_a`$ and $`E_{aba}`$ and central phase half their actual pairing. If the projected stabilizer element is in $`V_2\setminus1`$, it is not a product since an outside sign exchanges axes and cannot invert it. Over the identity the signs agree. We need $`E_a\cap L=0`$ for each outside involution. Write $`a=b_1^u b_2^v h^i t`$. Since $`h`$ and $`b_1`$ preserve $`tL`$, and the two axes commute,
```math
                     aL=b_2^v tL .
```
This is transverse to $`L`$ because $`b_2`$ preserves $`L`$ and $`tL\cap L=0`$. A negative vector in $`L`$ would also belong to $`aL`$; thus the only stabilization over the identity is the zero vector, then the two summands are negatives and have zero phase.

For projection $`b_2^v h^j`$ with $`j\ne0`$ use conjugation by $`V_2\subset H`$ to reduce to $`h^j`$. The only possible first sign normalizes $`\langle h\rangle`$ and is $`a=h^i t`$, with $`aba=h^{i+j}t`$. Write vectors as $`x+t y`$, $`x,y\in L`$. In these coordinates the cross-form $`\beta(x,y)=\omega(x,t y)`$ is nondegenerate alternating: $`t`$ is a symplectic involution and both complementary summands are Lagrangian. The restriction $`R=h|_L`$ is self-adjoint for $`\beta`$, by $`tht=h^{-1}`$. Thus $`h=(R,R^{-1})`$, $`t`$ exchanges the copies, and $`E_{h^i t}=\{(R^i x,-x)\}`$. The only possible summands with total in $`L`$ are $`(R^i x,-x),(-R^{i+j}x,x)`$. Their pairing is $`\beta(R^i x,x)+\beta(x,R^{i+j}x)=0`$. The real subgroup $`H`$ requires phase $`\lambda j\ne0`$. Hence $`H\cap\mathcal C^2=1`$. This proof does not require $`L`$ invariant under $`b_1`$ or all projected signs to exchange the same Lagrangian pair.

Sharp transitivity, the native Bol identity and simplicity now follow with precisely the arguments given in 9a22cb96: for completeness a signed-normal $`1<C\le N`$ contains $`Z(N)`$, is the full preimage of $`Y=C/Z(N)`$, and has orbit exponent $`1+\dim Y-\dim(Y\cap L)>\dim Y^-`$ since $`L\cap U^-=0`$. A signed-normal cut projecting outside $`N`$ contains the full $`V`$ in its upper image, then contains $`N`$ by commutators and Frattini. Its only proper possibility is $`NV`$, with physical orbit index $`p^{d+1}q`$ strictly larger than the mark fiber $`p^d q`$. The [odd signed-normal-cut criterion](../../packet/manuscripts/odd-folder-radicals.md#candidate-simplicity-criterion-using-τ-stable-normal-subgroups) applies to the actual faithful folder, independently of any further classification. Its nontrivial stabilizer excludes associativity.

## 4. Exact isomorphism, rather than orbit shapes alone

A based loop isomorphism conjugates the faithful generated left multiplication groups, sends all native marks and the point stabilizers to one another, and commutes with the inverting involution, which is uniquely determined on the generating marks. It carries the characteristic $`p`$-core and its central line to their counterparts, giving a prime-field linear similitude $`F`$ on $`U`$ with central multiplier $`\nu`$.

We must know which actual lifts its upper action preserves. A $`t`$-stable copy $`B`$ whose second axis lies in the *actual* $`H`$ is unique after the displayed alignment. Indeed every other copy in $`NB`$ is conjugate by $`N`$, its displacement represented uniquely by $`u\in U`$ since $`C_N(B)=Z`$. Stability under $`t`$ puts $`u\in U^+`$. If its second axis is in $`H`$, that axis is an $`H`$-Sylow $`q`$-subgroup, conjugate to $`B_2`$ by an element of $`L`$, because the upper part of $`H`$ normalizes $`B_2`$. The same displacement lies in $`L`$ since $`C_N(B_2)=Z`$. Hence $`u=0`$. An isomorphism must therefore carry the aligned $`B`$ to $`B'`$, and each axis to its counterpart: the second is distinguished by the true stabilizer; the first is its $`t`$-image. Their generators may be raised to the **same** $`r`$ since $`t`$ interchanges them. The normalized $`B_2`$ character support $`S`$ forces $`r\in S`$.

The image of $`h`$ in the quotient is in $`J'\cap t'J't'=\langle h'\rangle`$: it lies in $`J'`$ since $`z^\lambda h\in H`$, and its inversion under $`t'`$ puts it in the other subgroup as well. The conjugation scalar on $`b_1'`$ is the fixed $`\alpha`$, hence the power of $`h'`$ must be exactly one. In the actual group the image normalizes $`B'`$ and so can differ from $`h'`$ only by a central scalar; being $`t'`$-inverted forces this scalar to vanish. Consequently $`\lambda'=\nu\lambda`$. Further, the unique $`B`$-invariant section of the additive Heisenberg space precludes hidden linear central gauges. Thus $`F`$ must intertwine the indicated operators and carry $`L`$ to $`L'`$.

Conversely every listed $`F`$ induces the actual Heisenberg isomorphism $`(u,c)\mapsto(Fu,\nu c)`$ and the upper map $`b_i\mapsto (b_i')^r`$, $`h\mapsto h'`$. The tests give a signed isomorphism carrying the true $`H`$ to $`H'`$, sufficing for a based loop isomorphism. Finally $`F b_2=(b'_2)^r F`$ forces a source site $`j`$ to map only to $`j+u`$ by a $`p^v`$-semilinear map, using $`\alpha,p`$ of coprime orders and the primitive $`q`$-root. Commuting with $`h`$ transports the same matrix $`D`$ to every site. This exhausts all permitted isomorphisms, including nontrivial coordinate mixing at repeated labels. The tests compare no unspecified automorphisms of the full constructed loop.

## 5. A uniform lower bound on the number of isotopy classes

The charts give at least $`(p+3)/2`$ **different loop isotopy classes** at this minimum, not just different chart bases. One is abelian-core. A second is the repeated-label extraspecial family 9a22cb96 with $`a_1=a_2=1`$, having $`d`$ distinct simultaneous $`V`$-weights of multiplicity two. For each $`1\le r\le(p-1)/2`$ choose $`a_1=1,a_2=\alpha^r`$, a nonzero $`c\in K`$ with $`\bar c=-c`$, $`M=\mathrm{diag}(c,-c)`$ and $`\ell=K(1,1)`$. On the first coordinate let $`t`$ send the site $`j`$ to $`-j`$ with sign plus; on the second send $`j`$ to $`-j-r`$ with sign minus. This is one of the indicated arrays, an involutive isometry. If a vector is in $`\ell^p\cap t(\ell^p)`$, its scalar sequence is equal to its negative after a cyclic shift. Iterating the shift $`p`$ times forces zero in odd characteristic, proving transversality. These examples genuinely have $`b_1L\ne L`$.

The $`2d`$ simultaneous $`V`$-weights of each distinct-label example occur with multiplicity one. To separate the choices of $`r`$ even after forgetting the marks, any isomorphism of left multiplication groups respects the characteristic $`N`$, its Frattini quotient and $`V=F(D/N)`$. In $`V`$, the two axes are the unordered pair of eigenspaces of a nontrivial generator of $`(D/N)/V`$. Changing its lift or replacing it by a different generator does not change this pair. Therefore an abstract isomorphism can rescale each weight coordinate by an arbitrary nonzero scalar and possibly interchange the coordinates, but cannot otherwise mix the two axes. For a weight $`(x,y)`$ take the product $`xy`$ in $`\mathbb F_q^\times/\langle p\bmod q\rangle`$. The unordered set of these products is independent of basis up to a *common* multiplicative factor and is unaffected by exchanging axes. For this $`r`$ it is the two distinct residues $`\{1,\alpha^r\}`$, since the site factors cancel and the common Frobenius multiplier disappears modulo $`\langle p\rangle`$. Up to common scaling this recovers $`\alpha^r`$ up to inversion. The order-$`p`$ subgroup injects into the quotient because $`p\nmid f`$, so our specified $`r`$'s are pairwise distinguished. Nonisomorphic left multiplication groups preclude loop isotopy: under independent relabelings their row-difference permutation groups are conjugate. Thus the uniform count is a **lower bound**, not the exact number of chart orbits.
