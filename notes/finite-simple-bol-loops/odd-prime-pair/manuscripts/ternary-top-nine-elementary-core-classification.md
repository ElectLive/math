# Classification of the elementary-core ternary top-nine chart

**Research proof; see the scope of verification.** [Verification and provenance](../VERIFICATION.md).

**Theorem.** Let $`q`$ be an odd prime, $`f=\operatorname{ord}_q(3)=9m`$, and write $`m=3^a k`$ with $`3\nmid k`$. The number of finite simple proper left Bol loops of order $`3^{f+3}q`$ whose canonical top has order nine and canonical $`3`$-core is elementary abelian is
```math

\frac1{3m}\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}3^{\,2\cdot3^a\gcd(k,j)},\qquad \gcd(k,0)=k.
```

For $`q=19`$, $`f=18`$, there are **exactly 15** in this scope. Every class comes from the actual physical graph and lift below. The formula is for based loop isomorphism, not isotope equivalence.

## 1. Exhaustive ordinary signed group and physical charts

In the signed reduction write $`D`$ for the faithful left group, $`t`$ for its canonical sign, $`H`$ for the actual point stabilizer, and $`N=O_3(D)`$ for the elementary core. The top has order nine, so the original sign budget on $`N`$ is $`f+1`$. An axial pair costs $`f`$ because $`9\mid f`$, whereas any mixed orbit of nontrivial weights costs at least $`f(9-1)/2=4f`$. Hence there are two $`f`$-dimensional axial modules $`A_1,A_2`$ exchanged by $`t`$ and a fixed-for-$`B`$ subspace $`C`$ with one negative direction. Here $`B=B_1B_2`$ is a sign-stable Sylow $`q`$-subgroup of the normal preimage of the two axes. Write $`M=A_1\oplus A_2`$. The normalizer splits $`D=M\rtimes (B\rtimes S)`$ with $`S`$ its sign-stable Sylow $`3`$-subgroup and $`S/C=C_9`$. As in the previous signed argument $`S\cong D/(MB)`$ is generated by inverted images. Choose an inverted lift $`h`$ of a generator of its cyclic top. The span of the negative line of $`C`$ under the conjugation action of $`h`$ is all $`C`$: quotienting by this span makes the residual $`C`$ sign-fixed and hence central, leaving an abelian quotient generated by inverted elements, which kills that fixed part. Thus $`C`$ is one cyclic Jordan block, with signs alternating from minus and negative dimension one. The size-one case is forbidden by the physical order-three normal cut (it would be a central inverted line avoiding $`H`$). The size-two case has basis $`v,w`$ with $`Jv=w,Jw=0`$, $`h`$ acting by $`1+J`$, and $`t(v)=-v,t(w)=w`$. Also $`h^9=1`$, since it lies on the fixed line of $`h`$ in $`C`$ and is inverted. These are exactly the ordinary signed group and sign of the construction already audited.

Put $`E=\mathbb F_{3^f}`$ and $`E_0=\mathbb F_{3^m}`$. Fix a primitive $`q`$th root $`\zeta`$ and choose the generator of the top so its action on $`B_1`$ raises to $`\alpha=3^m\bmod q`$. Identifying $`A_1`$ with $`E`$ with its $`B_1`$-action by $`\zeta`$-multiplication, the rotation acts as a scalar times $`R:z\mapsto z^{3^m}`$. Its scalar has norm one because $`h^9=1`$, and finite-field Hilbert 90 kills it by a change of basis. Use $`t`$ to transport coordinates to $`A_2`$, where rotation acts by $`R^{-1}`$. In particular this normalization is signed.

The original $`W=H\cap N`$ has dimension $`f-1`$, lies in $`A_1\oplus C`$ by irreducibility of its moving projection to $`A_2`$, and maps onto $`C`$ by the physical $`p`$-group quotient $`D/(MB)=S`$ (the normally generating $`H`$ covers that quotient and its $`W`$ covers the kernel over the top). Its intersection with $`C`$ is a rotation-invariant subspace avoiding the central fixed line $`\langle w\rangle`$, so is zero. Thus $`W`$ is the graph of an equivariant map $`\eta:K\to C`$ over an $`R`$-invariant prime-field hyperplane $`K\subset A_1`$. In particular $`(R-1)A_1\subseteq K`$.

Normalize the actual subgroup $`H`$ using **sign-preserving** conjugations. A Sylow $`q`$-subgroup above its projected axis can first be conjugated to $`B_2`$ by an element of $`N`$; because it is an axial coprime lift, the only useful displacement is in the moving coordinate $`A_2`$. An element $`(z,z,0)\in N`$ fixed by $`t`$ realizes the same displacement: the other coordinates centralize $`B_2`$. Equivalently choose a displacement from the ordinary Sylow conjugacy in $`NB_2`$ and replace it by a sign-fixed one with the same $`A_2`$ coordinate. A sign-fixed diagonal element of $`B`$ then normalizes the projected $`H`$ to $`B_2\rtimes C_9`$ and preserves literal $`B_2`$. Now $`H=W N_H(B_2)`$; a lift of $`h`$ in $`N_H(B_2)`$ has its moving part in $`A_1`$, and discarding the $`B_2`$-factor gives the actual lift $`(x,c h)`$ with $`x\in A_1,c\in C`$. Its parameters are taken modulo multiplying this lift by $`W`$: changing $`x`$ by $`y\in K`$ changes $`c`$ by $`\eta(y)`$.

Write $`L=R-1`$ for the linear operator in the next two sections. The chart is valid **exactly when**
```math
\eta(L^2x)\ne0.
```

Necessity uses the actual cube of the lift. Its vector component is $`L^2x\in K`$ and its $`C`$ norm is $`J^2c=0`$. Canceling its vector by a true $`W`$ element leaves $`-\eta(L^2x)h^3`$ in the stabilizer. If the phase were zero, the nonidentity inverted $`h^3`$ would meet $`H`$. The phase belongs to $`\langle w\rangle`$ since $`\eta(L^2x)=J\eta(Lx)`$.

## 2. Sufficiency for all graph maps and all lift phases

Conversely let $`K`$ be any invariant hyperplane, $`\eta`$ equivariant, and $`x,c`$ such that the phase is nonzero. Then $`x\notin K`$ because $`J^2=0`$, and $`\eta`$ is onto $`C`$ because $`\eta(Lx)`$ has nonzero $`v`$ coordinate. The constructed $`H`$ is generated by the graph $`W`$, $`B_2`$ and $`(x,c h)`$; the ninth power of the lift lies in $`W`$ because its vector is $`L^8x`$ killed by $`\eta`$, and its $`C`$ norm is zero. Thus the original size, projected stabilizer and generation hypotheses are exactly those of the audited construction.

All actual two-sign product tests in that construction remain valid with these relaxed parameters. For identity projection to $`D/N`$, the same negative-graph argument forces the $`A_1`$ component of an $`H`$-element to be zero and hence forces the identity. A nonidentity pure $`B_2`$ element is never inverted by an outside sign. At any nonzero top $`j\bmod9`$, actual $`B_2`$ conjugation reduces the projected product to $`h^j`$, and a two-sign product with zero $`A_2`$ part has $`A_1`$ coordinate in $`(R^j-1)A_1`$. For $`3\nmid j`$ this is inside $`K`$ while the actual $`H`$-coordinate is $`jx\ne0`$ modulo $`K`$. For $`j=3r`$, $`r=1,2`$, the $`C`$ norm of the lift for $`3r`$ terms is zero and $`(R^j-1)A_1=L^3A_1`$. The map $`\eta`$ kills $`L^3A_1`$ by equivariance and $`LA_1\subset K`$. Hence an $`H`$ candidate with its vector in $`L^3A_1`$ has $`C`$ coordinate $`-r\eta(L^2x)\ne0`$ on the positive line and zero $`v`$ coordinate. The actual order-81 upper group forbids exactly such pure positive nonzero phases in a residue-zero top fiber of its two-sign products. This excludes all collisions.

The canonical generation, core-freeness and every strict signed cut from the audited construction apply: they require only the ambient group, the actual graph with $`K`$ a hyperplane and no intersection with $`C`$, and $`\eta`$ onto (so $`H`$ maps onto $`S`$). They do not assume the particular basis map or the zero lift phase. Consequently each valid chart produces a genuine simple proper loop.

## 3. The exact signed equivalence and normalized count

A loop isomorphism transports the canonical left group, generator set, stabilizer, and sign (the sign is unique on the generating marks). Conversely an isomorphism of signed groups taking the true stabilizer to the true stabilizer transports the full inverted mark set and gives a loop isomorphism. The Sylow $`q`$ in every normalized $`H`$ is the unique subgroup $`B_2`$: it is normal there because $`W`$ centralizes it and the actual top lift normalizes it. Thus any signed isomorphism preserves $`B_2`$, its sign-mate $`B_1`$, and their actual normalizer. The action on the axes is common scaling by a unit modulo $`q`$. On the axial modules it must be $`z\mapsto r z^{3^i}`$ on both coordinates with some $`i\bmod f`$ and $`r\in E_0^\times`$: since the acting primitive multiplication generates $`E`$, the common axis scaling must be $`3^i`$ and only such semilinear intertwiners are possible. Preserving the faithful multiplier $`\alpha`$ on the labelled first axis forces the image of $`h`$ to have top power 1; its $`B`$ coordinate lies in $`B_2`$ by the actual projected $`H`$, and invertedness then forces it to be zero. Hence $`h`$ maps to $`(a'v+(a'/2)w)h`$, precisely the inverted lifts of that top in the normalizer. Commuting with sign and the action of $`1+J`$ forces the linear map on $`C`$ to scale both $`v,w`$ by the same $`\lambda\in\mathbb F_3^\times`$. The decomposition into $`A_1,A_2,C`$ is intrinsic under this preservation of the labelled axes, so no further shears of $`N`$ are possible. Conversely all of these changes are genuine signed automorphisms: the rotation action and sign relations are preserved, and the order-nine relation on the proposed image of $`h`$ holds by its zero $`C`$ norm.

Scalar multiplication by $`E_0^\times`$ allows normalizing $`K`$ to $`\ker\operatorname{Tr}_{E/\mathbb F_3}`$: $`A_1/(R-1)A_1`$ is the $`E_0`$-line obtained by relative trace, and the finite-field trace pairing gives transitivity on its prime-field hyperplanes. The residual allowed scalar is in $`\mathbb F_3^\times`$. Normalize $`\operatorname{Tr}(x)=1`$ by that scalar, and use $`W`$ to put $`x=x_0`$ for a fixed trace-one normal generator of $`E`$ over $`\mathbb F_3`$. Such a generator exists by normal basis and scaling: its trace cannot vanish since its basis conjugates are independent. Use $`\lambda`$ to set $`\eta(L^2x_0)=w`$ and the inverted change of $`h`$ to leave the lift parameter $`c=u w`$ for $`u\in\mathbb F_3`$. No nontrivial scalar remains after this: each Frobenius preserves the absolute trace and the nonzero normalized phase (changing $`x_0`$ by a vector of $`K`$ does not change $`\eta(L^2x_0)`$). Only Frobenius remains, with the exact action, writing $`\sigma(z)=z^3`$ and $`\ell(av+bw)=b-a/2`$,
```math
\eta'=\eta\circ \sigma^{-i},\qquad
u'=u-\ell\big(\eta'(\sigma^i x_0-x_0)\big).
```

The phase correction is exactly removing the difference of lift vectors by the new $`W`$. The power $`\sigma^{3m}=R^3`$ acts trivially, since $`\eta`$ is equivariant and kills $`L^3 A_1`$. Thus it suffices to count orbits of this explicit action of $`C_{3m}`$, including possible kernel.

## 4. Closed evaluation by Burnside

Here use an indeterminate $`X`$ representing $`\sigma`$ in the absolute normal basis. The cyclic module is $`\mathbb F_3[X]/(X^f-1)`$ with $`x_0=1`$, trace kernel the ideal $`(X-1)`$, hence $`K\simeq\mathbb F_3[X]/((X^f-1)/(X-1))`$ by multiplying by $`X-1`$. The operator $`L`$ is $`X^m-1`$. The condition $`\eta\circ\sigma^{-i}=\eta`$, and equivariance to $`C`$ killed by $`J^2`$, mean exactly an equivariant map from the cyclic quotient
```math

\mathbb F_3[X]/g_i,\qquad g_i=\gcd((X^m-1)^2,X^i-1),
```

since $`(X^m-1)^2`$ divides $`(X^f-1)/(X-1)`$. For $`i=0`$ take $`g_0=(X^m-1)^2`$. The operator $`L`$ on this quotient has only Jordan blocks of lengths at most two, so the vector space of equivariant maps to the length-two block $`C`$ has dimension $`\deg g_i`$ (a length-one block contributes one, a length-two block two).

The normalized vector $`L^2x_0`$ has chart class $`(X^m-1)^2/(X-1)`$, which vanishes modulo $`g_i`$ outside the root $`1`$, and at $`1`$ has multiplicity $`2\cdot3^a-1`$. Thus it is nonzero modulo $`g_i`$ exactly when $`3^{a+1}\mid i`$ (including zero). It lies in the image of $`L`$, and if nonzero its normalization to $`w`$ imposes exactly one independent scalar condition, by the length-two block decomposition. If $`3^{a+1}\mid i`$, the phase drift vector has chart class $`(X^i-1)/(X-1)`$, divisible by $`g_i`$: at $`1`$ its multiplicity is at least $`3^{a+1}-1\ge2\cdot3^a`$ and away from $`1`$ divisibility is unchanged. For $`i=0`$ it is zero directly. All three $`u`$ are therefore fixed for every such normalized $`\eta`$. The fixed count is $`3^{\deg g_i}`$ at those $`i`$ and zero at others.

Writing $`i=3^{a+1}j`$ in $`0\le i<3m`$ gives $`0\le j<k`$ and $`\deg g_i=2\cdot3^a\gcd(k,j)`$: the common root set is that of $`X^{\gcd(k,j)}-1`$, each with multiplicity $`2\cdot3^a`$. Burnside's formula is the stated count. This arithmetic derivation was developed with the coordinating agent. The [author checker](../code/verify_ternary_top_nine_chart_count.py) independently enumerates the normalized affine cycles in normal-basis coordinates and compares the formula for $`m=1,2,3,4`$, obtaining totals $`3,15,81,555`$ from $`9,81,729,6561`$ states respectively. These are exact phase-orbit tests, not full multiplication-table runs and not an independent review.
