# Fixed-pair minimum rigidity when the character-field degree contains $`p`$

**Author proof, unreviewed.** Let $`p,q`$ be distinct odd primes and $`f=\mathrm{ord}_q(p)`$, with $`p\mid f`$. Then the least exponent $`a`$ for which there is a finite simple nonassociative left Bol loop of order $`p^a q`$ is $`f+1`$, and at that degree the loop is **unique up to isomorphism**. It is the affine exact-factorization construction with field $`\mathbb F_{p^f}`$ and order-$`p`$ Frobenius power. This includes even $`f`$, where opposite weights really occur and the elementary-kernel conclusion cannot be read off from weight support alone. The preceding [prime-pair result](two-prime-divisibility-existence.md) at $`\mathtt{2625d36bc331e3ec7157d7266dca390baf46233e}`$ gives existence and the minimum exponent. The [boundary rigidity](two-prime-boundary-folder-rigidity.md) at $`\mathtt{21a3344447d1470bc2a5222695428d976e56bd2e}`$ covers $`f=p`$. We use its elementary marked-gauge arguments below, not a claim of its review. No uniqueness for arbitrary larger exponents or for $`p\nmid f`$ is asserted.

## All active characters at the minimum

Write $`t,D,H,N,W=N/\Phi(N),X=V\rtimes P`$ as in the preceding proofs. We have $`O_q(D)=1`$; $`V=C_q^2`$ has two rational eigenaxes exchanged by $`t`$, and $`P=C_{p^k}`$ acts with faithful reciprocal eigencharacters. Since $`a=f+1`$, the inequalities
```math
 a=s+k,\qquad s=\log_p[N:C_N(t)]\ge r=\dim W^-(t)\ge f
```
force $`k=1`$, $`P=C_p`$, $`r=s=f`$. After scalar extension split $`W`$ into its $`V`$-weights. Every nonzero $`P`$-orbit has length $`p`$. In fact each orbit under $`P,t,\mathrm{Fr}`$ of nontrivial weights costs at least $`f`$ negative dimensions on its multiplicity spaces. If every conjugate of $`t`$ preserved each Frobenius orbit within it, their permutation group there would be abelian of exponent at most two, as in the two-prime proof. But then $`P`$ would act trivially on its weights, although its nontrivial elements fix no nonzero weight. Thus some conjugate exchanges two distinct Frobenius orbits of length $`f`$, at cost $`f`$. Consequently there is **exactly one** active $`P,t,\mathrm{Fr}`$-orbit, all weight spaces have multiplicity one, and $`Z=W^V`$ is $`t`$-fixed. The active rational summand $`U=[W,V]`$ is irreducible under $`X,t`$, because any rational invariant subspace's distinct one-dimensional weight support would be a union of these orbits.

If one $`P`$-orbit is $`t`$-stable, its $`f`$ Frobenius translates are distinct and $`t`$-stable, by the centralizer of the natural odd dihedral action. Each contributes $`(p-1)/2`$ negatives. This is possible only for $`p=3`$; then $`\dim U=3f`$, with no room for a negative sign on the fixed weights. Both coordinates of every active character are nonzero because the axes are interchanged by $`t`$.

Otherwise the signed dihedral orbits are pairs of exchanged $`P`$-orbits, each contributing $`p`$ negatives. There are $`f/p`$ such pairs, cyclically permuted by Frobenius. Its $`f/p`$-th power preserves either member pair as a set, commutes with its regular dihedral action, and has order $`p`$ on that set (each nonzero weight has exact Frobenius orbit length $`f`$). Thus it rotates each of the two $`P`$-orbits. It multiplies both coordinates by the order-$`p`$ scalar $`p^{f/p}\bmod q`$, whereas a rotation multiplies coordinates reciprocally. Both coordinates cannot be nonzero. The active support therefore consists of a full Frobenius orbit on each of the two different axes, and
```math
U=A_1\oplus A_2,\qquad \dim A_i=f,
```
where the $`i`$-th $`q`$-axis $`V_i`$ acts irreducibly on $`A_i`$ alone, and $`t`$ interchanges the $`A_i`$. This is a rational module statement, not yet a lifting or splitting assertion.

## Isotropy forbids the extra central commutators, even at even $`f`$

No negative mass is left in $`\Phi=\Phi(N)`$, so $`t`$ fixes it; the generating conjugates imply $`\Phi`$ is central in the signed group. Moreover $`H\cap\Phi=1`$: a central element fixing one physical point fixes all, and the first projection is injective on the $`t`$-fixed elements of the double (their two coordinates coincide). Thus we may treat the image $`W_H`$ of $`H\cap N`$ in $`W`$ as an injective elementary abelian group. The class-two commutator pairing $`\kappa:W\times W\to[N,N]`$ has elementary central target, since all $`p`$-th powers of $`N`$ are central. The space $`W_H`$ is totally isotropic for each scalar component of $`\kappa`$: all commutators of $`H\cap N`$ lie in $`H\cap\Phi=1`$.

The true orbit size $`[N:H\cap N]`$ is $`p^{f+1}`$: the fiber at $`N`$ has size $`p^f`$, and simplicity forces strict enlargement, up to $`p^{f+1}`$. The image $`J=HN/N`$ has index $`q`$ in $`X`$; as in the boundary proof put $`J=V_2\rtimes P`$ by the actual $`t`$-fixed axis-translation gauge, using $`t`$ itself to interchange axes if needed. All that is required at this stage is invariance of $`W_H`$ under $`J`$'s image on $`W`$. This follows regardless of the lifts because $`N`$ acts trivially on its Frattini quotient. For $`z=\dim Z`$, $`e=\log_p|\Phi|`$,
```math
 \dim W_H=\dim U+z+e-f-1.                                             \qquad\mathrm{(1)}
```

The active space cannot pair nontrivially with $`Z`$, by $`V`$-invariance and lack of a trivial weight on $`U`$. In the mixed ternary case, any scalar component of $`\kappa`$ nonzero on $`U\times U`$ is a **nondegenerate** alternating form there: its radical is signed-invariant and $`U`$ is rationally irreducible. But $`\dim(W_H\cap U)\ge 2f+e-1>3f/2`$ for $`f\ge3`$. This exceeds the maximal isotropic dimension, impossible.

In the paired case distinct axes cannot pair with each other since their supports lie on distinct nonzero $`V`$-axes. A nonzero signed-invariant scalar form on $`U`$ must restrict nondegenerately to each $`A_i`$, by irreducibility under $`V_i`$, trivial target and exchange by $`t`$. The $`V_2`$-stable $`W_H`$ either contains $`A_2`$ or has zero projection to it: coprime averaging projects onto the complementary $`V_2`$-fixed space $`A_1\oplus Z`$, and $`A_2`$ is irreducible. If it contains $`A_2`$ this already contradicts total isotropy. In the other case $`W_H\le A_1\oplus Z`$, so its actual intersection with $`A_1`$ has dimension at least $`f+e-1>f/2`$ by (1). It cannot be totally isotropic for a nondegenerate scalar form on $`A_1`$. This uses the **intersection**, not an uncontrolled graph projection whose pairing with $`Z`$ could cancel.

Therefore in either pattern the entire inverse image of $`U`$ in $`N`$ centralizes $`N`$. To finish the lifting step, choose a $`t`$-stable Sylow $`q`$-subgroup $`B`$ of the inverse image of $`V`$; $`B\cong V`$ because the $`q`$-core is absent. Set $`A=[N,B]`$. Its image is $`U`$, so $`A\le Z(N)`$. Coprime averaging gives $`N=A C_N(B)`$, $`A=[A,B]`$, $`A^B=1`$. Hence $`A\cap\Phi=1`$, $`A\cong U`$, and $`N=A\times N_0`$ with $`N_0=C_N(B)`$. All conjugates of $`B`$ in its normal preimage are $`N`$-conjugate, so both factors are signed-normal; all negative mass in $`N`$ is on $`A`$, making $`N_0`$ central and fixed. As in the boundary proof, $`D=A\rtimes L`$ for $`L=N_D(B)`$, since $`A\cap L=C_A(B)=1`$ and $`D=NL`$. The quotient $`L/B`$ is a central extension of $`C_p`$ by $`N_0`$, hence abelian. It is generated by inverted marks under the signed quotient map, yet $`N_0`$ is fixed. Thus $`N_0=1`$. We have the genuine split $`D=A\rtimes(V\rtimes C_p)`$, choosing a $`t`$-stable Sylow $`p`$-complement of $`B=V`$ in $`L`$.

In the mixed branch every $`V_2`$-constituent of $`A`$ is nontrivial of degree $`f`$, so its invariant subspace $`H\cap A`$ must have dimension divisible by $`f`$. Its actual dimension $`3f-(f+1)=2f-1`$ is not divisible by $`f`$. Thus the mixed branch never occurs.

## One trace hyperplane, one actual folder

In the paired branch, identify $`A_1=K=\mathbb F_{p^f}`$, with a generator of $`V_1`$ acting by a fixed primitive $`q`$-th root. Choose the generator $`h`$ of $`P`$ so its action on $`V_1`$ is the residue $`p^{f/p}`$; this generates the unique order-$`p`$ subgroup of $`\mathbb F_q^\times`$. Normalize its semilinear scalar by the finite-field norm-one identity, so $`h`$ acts on $`A_1`$ by $`\varphi:x\mapsto x^{p^{f/p}}`$. Use $`t`$ to transport coordinates to $`A_2`$ where $`h`$ then acts by $`\varphi^{-1}`$. The physical $`H\cap A`$ has dimension $`f-1`$, too small to project onto irreducible $`A_2`$ under $`V_2`$, so it is a hyperplane in $`A_1`$. Its $`P`$-invariance says that it contains $`(\varphi-1)K`$ because a unipotent action on a prime-field one-dimensional quotient is trivial.

Put $`K_0=\mathbb F_{p^{f/p}}`$. The kernel of the nonzero relative trace $`K\to K_0`$ equals $`(\varphi-1)K`$ by their dimensions over $`K_0`$. Hyperplanes of $`K`$ above it are precisely the kernels of the $`\mathbb F_p`$-linear forms $`x\mapsto\mathrm{Tr}_{K/\mathbb F_p}(c x)`$, $`c\in K_0^\times`$, by the nondegenerate trace pairing in $`K_0`$. Matched simultaneous $`K_0^\times`$-scaling of both $`A_i`$ commutes with the signed group and puts the hyperplane in standard absolute-trace form. This allows all hyperplanes when $`[K_0:\mathbb F_p]>1`$, not only one chosen invariant Jordan block. The argument uses nonzero field trace maps, not a false assertion that the trace of 1 is nonzero.

The stable-section calculation now agrees with the boundary source: modulo $`H\cap A`$, the module is a trivial $`\mathbb F_p`$-line plus $`A_2`$. Every $`A_2`$-cocycle over $`V_2\rtimes P`$ is principal by averaging over the normal, fixed-point-free $`V_2`$; the necessary translation can be chosen diagonal $`t`$-fixed using the $`A_1`$-coordinate. The residual scalar homomorphism kills $`V_2`$ and assigns $`h`$ a scalar $`\lambda`$. Zero would put the actual nonidentity inverted $`h`$ in $`H`$; all nonzero values are equivalent through simultaneous $`\mathbb F_p^\times`$-scaling preserving the trace kernel. Hence precisely one physical marked folder up to based isomorphism. It is realized by the preceding affine construction at $`d=f`$.
