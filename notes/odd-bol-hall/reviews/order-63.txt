**No nine-site section.** Projection to the two additive ternary coordinates is a homomorphism. In a putative nine-element subloop all nonempty projection fibers are translates of its zero fiber: a preimage within that subloop supplies a bijection by left multiplication, including surjectivity via its unique division. Its zero fiber is a subgroup of C7 whose order divides nine, hence trivial. The homomorphism restricted to the subloop is thus a bijective morphism to the whole abelian C3². In particular it must have commuting lifts of the fixed two coordinate basis vectors. In the native example those products differ for *every* choice of the two lifts. Independently generating the multiplication-closed set from each of these 49 possible basis choices yields the whole 63-point loop in every case; for the comparison table the same 49 choices give 49 **distinct** closed nine-site planes.
